Is selling on Walmart worth it? Pros, cons and profit

An honest look at Walmart Marketplace's fees, approval bar, traffic and competition, and how to decide product by product.

Is selling on Walmart worth it? For sellers who can get approved and price competitively, often yes. Walmart Marketplace has no setup, monthly or hidden fees, you pay a referral fee only when an item sells, and Walmart says Walmart.com gets more than 90 million monthly visitors. It's less worth it if you can't meet Walmart's approval bar, if your products are restricted, or if Walmart itself sells the same item cheaper. In the end it comes down to the profit on each product, not the platform.

Is selling on Walmart profitable?

It can be, product by product. Your profit is the sale price minus Walmart's fees, your cost of goods and any shipping or prep. Here's a simple example with Walmart's published rates and a hypothetical item:

  • Sale price: $24.99 (hypothetical) in a 15% category, so the referral fee is about $3.75.
  • WFS fulfillment: $3.45 for an item that ships at 1 lb or less, from Walmart's WFS fee table (updated May 27, 2026).
  • What's left before product cost: about $17.79, before storage, inbound shipping and prep.

If you paid $10 for the item, that's under $8 of profit before those extra costs. If you paid $16, it's barely worth the effort. Items priced under $10 also pay a $1 WFS surcharge per unit, which is why cheap items are often the hardest to make work. Full rates are on Walmart seller fees and WFS fees, and how much you can make selling on Walmart walks through realistic income.

Is Walmart Marketplace worth it for beginners?

Walmart is not the easiest first marketplace. Its seller requirements include a business tax ID or business license number (an SSN isn't accepted), documents verifying your business, a history of marketplace or ecommerce success, products with GS1 UPCs, fulfillment through WFS or a U.S. warehouse that handles returns, and a compatible payment processor. Walmart's registration guide says business verification takes from a few minutes to two business days, but it doesn't promise approval.

If you don't have a selling history yet, many sellers build one on another marketplace first. Our guide to opening a Walmart seller account covers the application.

Should I sell on Walmart or Amazon?

You don't have to pick one. The main differences:

  • Monthly cost: Walmart charges none. Amazon's Professional plan is $39.99 a month, or $0.99 per item sold on the Individual plan.
  • Referral fees: similar ranges. Amazon says most of its categories fall between 8% and 15%; Walmart's most common rate is 15%.
  • Traffic: Amazon has the larger marketplace audience; Walmart.com still draws more than 90 million monthly visitors.
  • Getting in: Walmart screens sellers before they can list; check Amazon's current rules on its site.
  • Fulfillment: WFS and FBA work differently; see WFS vs FBA.

Many sellers start on Amazon and add Walmart once they have the history Walmart asks for. Our Walmart vs Amazon page compares them in more detail.

Who selling on Walmart is worth it for

  • Worth a look: established ecommerce sellers who can pass approval, brands that want another channel, and resellers who can source items Walmart doesn't sell itself or sells out of.
  • Probably not yet: brand-new sellers with no business setup or selling history, and anyone whose catalog is mostly gated brands or very cheap items.

Questions

Is selling on Walmart worth it in 2026?

It can be if you can get approved and the numbers work on each product. Walmart charges no setup or monthly fees and takes a referral fee only when you sell, but approval requires a business and a selling history, and you may compete with Walmart itself.

Is selling on Walmart profitable?

Product by product, yes for many sellers. Subtract the referral fee (15% in many categories), WFS or shipping costs, and your product cost from the sale price. Cheap and heavy items are the hardest to make profitable.

Does Walmart charge a monthly fee to sell?

No. Walmart says Marketplace has no setup, monthly or hidden fees. You pay a referral fee on each sale, plus WFS fees if you use Walmart's fulfillment.

How hard is it to get approved to sell on Walmart?

Harder than some marketplaces. Walmart asks for a business tax ID, business documents, a history of marketplace or ecommerce success, GS1 UPCs, U.S. fulfillment with returns and a compatible payment processor.

Should I sell on Walmart or Amazon?

Many sellers do both. Amazon has the bigger audience and a $39.99 monthly Professional plan; Walmart has no monthly fee, often fewer sellers per listing, and screens sellers before approval. Compare the profit on your actual products.

Is there less competition on Walmart than Amazon?

Generally, sellers report fewer competing sellers on Walmart, but Walmart doesn't publish a seller count, and Walmart itself sells many items. Check each listing's sellers before you buy.

How can I tell if a product is worth selling on Walmart?

Look at the profit after fees, the sales estimate, how many sellers are on the listing and whether Walmart sells it. Margenos shows all of these on any Walmart.com listing.
