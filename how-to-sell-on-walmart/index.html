How to sell on Walmart Marketplace in 2026

Everything you need to get a Walmart seller account approved, set up Seller Center, understand the fees and pick products that make money.

Walmart.com is the second-biggest online marketplace in the U.S., and it has far fewer third-party sellers than Amazon. That gap is why so many resellers are adding Walmart. But you can't just sign up and list: Walmart Marketplace is application-only, and Walmart approves sellers it thinks will give shoppers a good experience. This guide walks through the whole process, from the documents you need to your first profitable sale.

Step 1: Check the Walmart seller requirements

Before you start the Walmart seller application, make sure you have these. Missing or mismatched paperwork is the number one reason applications stall.

  • A registered business. An LLC, corporation or registered sole proprietorship. Walmart does not accept individual sellers using a Social Security number.
  • An EIN (U.S. business tax ID) whose legal name matches your application exactly, plus a W-9.
  • A U.S. business address and a U.S. bank account in the business's name, for payouts.
  • Proof you already sell online. Walmart asks about your e-commerce history on Amazon, eBay, Shopify or your own site. Sellers with a track record get approved faster.
  • Products with valid UPCs. New listings need GS1-issued GTINs. If you resell existing brand-name items, you can match the listing that's already on Walmart.com.
  • A way to ship fast. Either ship from a U.S. location within one to two days and accept returns, or let Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) do it.

Step 2: Fill out the Walmart seller application

Go to marketplace.walmart.com and click Join Marketplace. The application asks for:

  1. Your company details: legal name, EIN and address, exactly as they appear on your IRS letter.
  2. Your selling history: which marketplaces you sell on, your yearly online sales and a link to your store.
  3. Your catalog: what you plan to sell, how many items and your main categories.
  4. Fulfillment: whether you'll ship yourself or use WFS.

Most complete applications get a decision in 1 to 4 weeks. If Walmart asks for more information, answer quickly and upload clear documents; a name that doesn't match your EIN is the most common holdup.

Step 3: Set up your Walmart Seller Center account

Once you're approved, you'll log in to Walmart Seller Center, the dashboard for your Walmart seller account. Finish these before you list anything:

  • Payment settings so Walmart can pay you. Payouts go to your business bank account.
  • Shipping settings: your shipping regions, prices and how many days you need to ship.
  • Return settings, including a U.S. return address.
  • Tax settings for the states where you need to collect sales tax (Walmart collects and pays it in marketplace-facilitator states).
  • WFS enrollment if you want Walmart to store and ship your inventory.

Step 4: Understand Walmart seller fees

Selling on Walmart has no monthly fee and no setup fee. You pay when you sell:

FeeWhat you pay
Referral feeA percentage of the total sale price, 15% in most categories (6% to 20% depending on category). See the full list.
WFS fulfillment feeOnly if Walmart ships for you; based on shipping weight. See the WFS fee chart.
WFS storageA monthly fee per cubic foot for inventory stored in Walmart's warehouses.
AdvertisingOptional. Only if you run Walmart Sponsored Search ads.

Example: you sell a $24.97 board game. The 15% referral fee is $3.75. If you use WFS, the fulfillment fee comes off too, and whatever you paid for the game. Our guide on how to calculate Walmart profit shows the full math.

Step 5: Find products to sell on Walmart

Most new sellers start with reselling: buying brand-name products for less and selling them on Walmart Marketplace. The three common ways are:

  • Retail arbitrage: buying clearance and sale items in stores. See how to resell Walmart clearance.
  • Online arbitrage: buying discounted items from other websites. See Walmart arbitrage.
  • Wholesale: buying cases from authorized distributors, which gives you steady, repeatable stock.

For every product, check four things before you buy: the price it sells for on Walmart.com, the fees, how many other sellers are on the listing, and how fast it sells. Walmart doesn't publish a sales rank, so use a sales estimator and watch competing sellers' stock.

Step 6: List your products

If the product already exists on Walmart.com, you match the existing listing by UPC and set your price, and you'll compete with other sellers for the Buy Box. If it's a new product, you create a full listing with a GS1 UPC, title, images and details. Start with 5 to 10 items so you can learn the system and keep your metrics clean.

Step 7: Keep your seller performance healthy

Walmart watches every seller's performance and can limit or suspend accounts that fall short. Its published targets include roughly 90% or more on-time deliveries, 99% valid tracking, a cancellation rate under 2%, a refund rate under 6%, and answering customer messages within 48 hours. Confirm the current numbers in Seller Center, because Walmart updates them.

Sellers who do well over time can earn the Walmart Pro Seller badge, which can help listings stand out.

Walmart vs Amazon: why sellers add Walmart

  • Fewer sellers per listing, so it's often easier to win the Buy Box.
  • No monthly subscription, unlike Amazon's $39.99 Professional plan.
  • Similar referral fees (most categories are 15% on both).
  • Harder to get in, because Walmart approves sellers one by one. That's also why there's less competition once you're in.

How do I sell on Walmart? The short answer

To sell on Walmart.com as a third-party seller, you apply to Walmart Marketplace, get approved, set up your account in Seller Center, and list your items. You start at marketplace.walmart.com and click Join Marketplace, which takes you to the sign-up at seller.walmart.com. That's how most resellers sell on Walmart online. It's a different process from selling to Walmart as a supplier.

Sell on Walmart Marketplace or sell to Walmart as a supplier?

If you resell brand-name products, Marketplace is your path. Walmart notes that registering as a supplier doesn't guarantee you'll be offered an agreement. New to the platform? Read what Walmart Marketplace is first.

Can I sell on Walmart as an individual?

Not with just your Social Security number. Walmart's minimum qualifications ask for a business tax ID or business license number and state "SSN not accepted." You also need documents that verify your business name and address. U.S. sellers with an EIN file a W-9. In practice, many solo resellers form a business (often an LLC), get an EIN from the IRS, and apply under that business. And not anyone can sell on Walmart: Walmart also looks for a history of success on another marketplace or e-commerce site.

How to become a Walmart seller: the checklist

Walmart lists these minimum qualifications to join Marketplace:

  • A business tax ID (SSN not accepted) or business license number.
  • Documents that verify your business name and address.
  • A history of marketplace or e-commerce success.
  • Products with GS1-issued GTIN/UPC numbers.
  • A catalog that follows Walmart's prohibited products policy.
  • Fulfillment through Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) or another U.S. B2C warehouse that handles returns.
  • An account with Marketplace Wallet or a supported third-party payment provider.

Our step-by-step Walmart seller account guide covers the application itself.

How to sell in Walmart Marketplace once you're approved

After approval you work in Walmart Seller Center. Walmart gives you three ways to get products on Walmart.com: through a solution provider, through the API, or directly in Seller Center using Setup by Match, Single Item setup or Bulk Upload. Resellers usually use Setup by Match, because the product already has a listing and you only add your offer. See Walmart product listings for the details.

If you go live in 2026, check Walmart's New-Seller Savings program. It runs from February 2, 2026 to January 31, 2027 for sellers who go live after February 1, 2026 and opt in on the payments page. It takes 20% off base referral fees on your first $50K in sales, 30% from $50K to $500K and 40% above that (up to $72K in savings), plus up to $2,000 in WFS credits. The Walmart seller fees page has the full fee picture.

Questions

Can anyone sell on Walmart Marketplace?

No. Walmart Marketplace is application-only. Walmart asks for a registered U.S. business with an EIN (a Social Security number is not accepted), a W-9, a U.S. address and bank account, and proof that you already sell online. Walmart also accepts some international businesses.

How long does Walmart seller approval take?

Most complete applications get a decision in about one to four weeks. Delays usually come from a business name or address that doesn't exactly match your EIN paperwork.

Does it cost money to sell on Walmart?

There is no setup fee and no monthly fee. Walmart takes a referral fee when an item sells, 15% in most categories and 6% to 20% overall, plus WFS fees if Walmart ships your orders.

Do I need my own products or UPCs to sell on Walmart?

No. Many sellers resell existing brand-name products by matching an existing Walmart listing. If you create a new listing, Walmart requires a GS1-issued GTIN (UPC) for it.

Is Walmart Seller Center the same as a seller account?

Walmart Seller Center is the dashboard you use after you are approved. It is where you list items, set prices, manage orders and see your payouts and performance.

What sells best on Walmart Marketplace?

Everyday products with steady demand do best: household, grocery, health and beauty, toys, pet supplies and home goods. Before you buy, check the price after fees, how many sellers compete and how often it sells.

How do I sell on Walmart.com?

Apply to Walmart Marketplace at marketplace.walmart.com by clicking Join Marketplace. Once approved, set up payments, shipping and returns in Seller Center, then list items, usually by matching existing listings.

Can I sell on Walmart as an individual?

Not with only a Social Security number. Walmart asks for a business tax ID or business license number and documents verifying your business name and address. Many solo resellers form an LLC and get an EIN before applying.

How do I sell my products in Walmart stores?

Getting into Walmart stores means becoming a Walmart supplier, which has its own qualification process through Retail Link. Walmart Marketplace is for selling directly to shoppers on Walmart.com.

Does it cost anything to apply to sell on Walmart?

Walmart says Marketplace has no setup or monthly fees. You pay referral fees when items sell, and WFS fees if Walmart fulfills your orders.

What is Walmart New-Seller Savings?

A 2026 program for sellers who go live after February 1, 2026 and opt in. It discounts base referral fees by 20 to 40 percent depending on sales (up to $72K in savings) and adds WFS and advertising credits. It runs until January 31, 2027.
