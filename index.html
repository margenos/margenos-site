FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is Margenos for?

Anyone building a business on Walmart Marketplace — from first-time resellers to high-volume sellers and teams. If you buy products to resell, Margenos shows you where the profit is before you spend.

Where do the sales estimates come from?

From the strongest signals first: Walmart's own "sold" badges on the listing, stock changes we've observed over time, and how comparable products sell. When there's nothing reliable to base one on, Margenos says "not enough data" rather than guessing.

How accurate are the fees?

Referral fees follow Walmart's published category rates, and WFS fulfillment and storage are calculated from the product's shipping weight. If the weight isn't known, we ask you for it rather than guessing.

Does the Chrome extension do anything on its own?

No. It reads only the Walmart pages you open yourself. It never clicks, browses, or adds anything to a cart.

Why do I only see variations in Chrome?

Variations come from the Walmart page itself. The extension reads the listing you have open in your own browser, so it can show them. The phone app can't read that page reliably, so variations are in the Chrome extension only.

Is Margenos an alternative to WAMP?

Yes. Margenos covers the same Walmart sourcing job — profit, fees, sellers, stock, buy box and history — in a Chrome extension and an Android app on Google Play (iPhone coming soon), on the same account, with sales estimates built from real Walmart signals. See how they compare.

How many devices can I use?

As many as you like. Your scans are one monthly allowance, shared between the phone app and the Chrome extension.

How does the free trial work?

Every plan starts with 14 days free. You'll add a card to start; nothing is charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel any time before then from your account.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, anytime, from Manage subscription in the app or the extension. Yearly plans save 20% over paying monthly.
