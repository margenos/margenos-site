The Walmart price checker app, and the one resellers use

How to check a Walmart price by scanning a barcode, and how resellers check what an item sells for and what they'd keep on Walmart Marketplace.

There are two kinds of Walmart price checker app. If you're shopping, the regular Walmart app has a barcode scanner that shows Walmart's price for an item at your store. If you're reselling, you need more than the price: what the item sells for on Walmart Marketplace, what Walmart takes in fees, and how much you'd keep. That's what a reseller price checker like Margenos does.

How to check a price with the Walmart app

  1. Open the Walmart app and make sure your store is selected.
  2. Tap the search bar and choose the barcode scanner icon.
  3. Point your camera at the item's barcode. The app shows the item and its price.

Walmart's scanner and Scan & Go are different features; our Walmart scanner app guide explains both and what to do when the scanner won't work.

How resellers check a Walmart price in the aisle

  1. Scan the barcode with the Margenos Android app. If several listings share the UPC, pick the right one.
  2. See the Walmart.com price and Buy Box price, then type the shelf price as your cost.
  3. Read profit, ROI and the most you should pay, after the category's referral fee and the WFS fee for its weight.
  4. Check the sales estimate and the number of sellers before you buy more than one.

On a computer? The Margenos Chrome extension shows the same numbers on any Walmart.com product page. For a quick manual check, use the free Walmart profit calculator.

Questions

Is there a Walmart price checker app?

Yes. The Walmart app has a barcode scanner that shows Walmart's price for an item. For reselling, Margenos is a price checker app that also shows Walmart fees, profit, ROI, sales and competing sellers.

How do I scan a price on the Walmart app?

Open the Walmart app, tap the search bar, choose the barcode scanner icon and point your camera at the barcode. The app shows the item and its price at your selected store.

Is the Walmart price checker app free?

The Walmart app's scanner is free. Margenos, the reseller price checker, is paid with a 14-day free trial on every plan.

Is there a Walmart UPC scanner app?

Yes. The Walmart app scans UPC barcodes for shoppers. Margenos scans UPCs for resellers and finds the matching Walmart.com listing, letting you pick when several listings share one UPC.

Is there a Walmart price checker app for iPhone?

The Walmart app works on iPhone and Android. The Margenos reseller app is on Android now and the iPhone app is coming soon; iPhone users can use the Margenos Chrome extension on a computer.

Can I check Walmart prices without the app?

Yes. Search the item or UPC on Walmart.com, or use the price check kiosks in many stores. Resellers can open any Walmart.com listing with the Margenos Chrome extension.
