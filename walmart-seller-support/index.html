Where Walmart sellers actually get help, from Seller Center cases to Walmart's own guides, and what to put in a case.

Walmart seller support is reached through Seller Center, not a phone line. Log in, click the Help button (the question mark icon) in the top menu bar, choose Contact support, describe your issue in 5 to 200 words and pick the category that fits. If you can't log in, go to the Seller Center login page and select Contact support. Walmart doesn't publish a phone number for Marketplace sellers. Its own FAQ says that, depending on the issue you describe, you may be offered email, live chat or a phone call.

How to contact Walmart seller support

Walmart's Get help quick guide (updated Mar 6, 2026) gives these routes:

  1. Logged in to Seller Center: select the Help button on the right side of the top menu bar and choose a support option.
  2. Describe the issue in at least five and no more than 200 words.
  3. Choose the category that best fits your situation and submit.
  4. Locked out or not logged in: go to the Seller Center login page, select Contact support, pick your market, then a category.

Approved solution providers get support through the Solution Provider Center, and sellers using the API directly can find help in the Walmart Developer Portal.

Walmart partner support cases

Many sellers still call it "Partner Support," and Walmart's Canada guide uses that name. It describes the same flow: you describe the issue, the tool suggests three case categories, shows related FAQs and guides, and lets you create a case if those don't solve it. In the U.S. guide, it's simply Contact support. Either way, keep the case number Walmart gives you so you can follow up on the same case instead of opening duplicates.

Walmart seller support phone number

There isn't a published one. Walmart's FAQ answers "Can I contact Seller Support via live chat or phone call?" with: "Yes. Depending on the situation you described in Seller Center, you may be able to access Seller Support through email or live chat, or by phone." In other words, the phone option, when offered, comes from inside the Contact support flow.

Two cautions. First, Walmart's customer service for shoppers handles Walmart.com orders, not seller accounts, so it can't fix Seller Center problems. Second, be wary of "Walmart seller support" phone numbers posted on third-party sites. Walmart doesn't publish one, and you should never share your Seller Center login or payout details with someone who calls or emails you.

How to write a support case that gets solved

  • One issue per case. Mixed problems get routed to the wrong team.
  • Include the IDs: item ID, SKU, purchase order number, WFS shipment ID or case number, whichever applies.
  • Say what you already tried and the exact error message.
  • Attach screenshots where the form allows it.
  • Pick the closest category rather than "other"; it decides who reads your case.
  • Reply in the same case when you follow up.

Questions

How do I contact Walmart seller support?

Log in to Seller Center, click the Help button (question mark icon) in the top menu bar and choose Contact support. Describe the issue in 5 to 200 words and pick a category. If you can't log in, use Contact support on the Seller Center login page.

Does Walmart have a phone number for seller support?

Walmart doesn't publish one for Marketplace sellers. Its FAQ says that, depending on the issue you describe in Seller Center, you may be offered email, live chat or a phone call.

Does Walmart seller support have live chat?

Sometimes. Walmart says live chat may be available depending on the situation you describe when you contact support in Seller Center.

What is Walmart Partner Support?

It's another name for Walmart's seller support case system, still used in Walmart's Canada guide. In the U.S. you reach it through Help, then Contact support, in Seller Center.

Can I contact Walmart seller support if I'm locked out?

Yes. Go to the Seller Center login page and select Contact support, then choose your market and the category that fits.

Is Walmart customer service the same as seller support?

No. Walmart's customer service helps shoppers with their orders. Seller account problems go through Contact support in Seller Center.

Is Margenos part of Walmart seller support?

No. Margenos is an independent tool by Lemont Holdings LLC and isn't affiliated with Walmart. Its support (support@margenos.com) covers the Margenos app and extension only.
