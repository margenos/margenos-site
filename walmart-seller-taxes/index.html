Walmart seller 1099-K: who gets one

Form 1099-K reports the gross payments you received for Marketplace orders. Walmart's guide (updated Jul 1, 2025) lists the conditions:

  • You're registered with Walmart Marketplace as a W-9 entity (a U.S. taxpayer).
  • You shipped more than 200 orders and had more than $20,000 in gross sales in the calendar year.
  • Your tax profile is filled in within Seller Center.

That matches the federal rule on the IRS's Understanding your Form 1099-K page (updated Oct 28, 2026): payment platforms report when payments "exceeds $20,000 in more than 200 transactions." The IRS adds that you may get a 1099-K below that threshold, and that "whether or not you receive a Form 1099-K, you must still report any income on your tax return." Walmart notes that some states set their own, different filing requirements.

The 1099-K shows gross sales. Walmart's guide says it doesn't subtract credits, discounts, fees or refunds. Your Walmart referral fees, WFS fees and refunds come out of that figure on your return, which is why you need your own records.

How to get your 1099-K from Walmart Seller Center

People search "how to download 1099 from Walmart Seller Center," but Walmart's guide says the form comes from your payment processor. If you qualify, the processor emails you instructions. In detail:

  1. Hyperwallet: log in to your Pay Portal, go to Resources > Tax Documents, find your form under Available Year-End Tax Forms, then select Action > Download.
  2. Payoneer: Payoneer emails you instructions to access the form through tax1099.com. Contact Payoneer Support if you have trouble.
  3. Didn't get one but think you qualify? Select Help in the Seller Center menu bar and contact Seller Support.

Walmart also says it doesn't provide yearly sales reports for tax purposes; managing those is up to you. Pull your payment and settlement reports from Seller Center during the year rather than in April. Our Walmart seller payments guide covers payouts.

Changing your tax ID

You can change your EIN in Seller Center at any time, but Walmart's guide says the tax ID on a 1099-K must be updated before or during the year the sales happened. A change made in the next year won't fix the previous year's form.

Walmart seller tax forms at onboarding

When you open an account, Walmart asks for tax details. Its tax classifications guide (updated Nov 2, 2026) says U.S. sellers must give a business tax ID or business license number, plus documents confirming the company. The form depends on where the business is set up:

FormWho uses it
W-9U.S.-incorporated sellers with an EIN
W-8ECISellers incorporated outside the U.S. that have an EIN
W-8BEN-ESellers incorporated outside the U.S. without an EIN, from listed countries
W-8BENOnly sole proprietors from Mexico or India

Does Walmart collect sales tax for sellers?

In most states, yes. Walmart's sales tax collection guide says Walmart is registered to collect and remit sales and use tax on taxable Marketplace sales in the states it lists, which include the large ones such as California, Texas, Florida and New York. For those orders, Walmart calculates, collects, remits and refunds the tax. This is because of marketplace facilitator laws, which make the marketplace responsible for the tax.

Two caveats from Walmart's own pages:

  • For orders shipped to a state not on Walmart's list, the guide says you receive the tax collected and "you're responsible for remitting the taxes to the tax authorities in those states" until Walmart tells you otherwise.
  • Walmart's tax collection and remittance policy addendum (updated Dec 11, 2025) says sellers must assign and keep accurate tax codes on their items and may be liable for incorrect ones.

Sales tax collected for the buyer isn't your income. Your bookkeeping should keep it separate from sales.

Income tax for Walmart sellers

The 1099-K only reports sales; figuring the income tax is up to you. The IRS basics for small sellers:

  • Schedule C. Sole proprietors use Schedule C (Form 1040) "to report income or loss from a business you operated." It has a section for cost of goods sold, which is where your inventory cost goes.
  • Self-employment tax. The IRS self-employment tax page says you owe it if net earnings from self-employment were $400 or more. The rate is 15.3% (12.4% Social Security, 2.9% Medicare), filed on Schedule SE.
  • Estimated taxes. The IRS estimated taxes page says you generally have to pay during the year if you expect to owe $1,000 or more, using Form 1040-ES.

An LLC or corporation may file differently. This is the part where a tax professional earns their fee.

Sales tax on inventory you buy: resale certificates

Resellers usually pay sales tax when they buy inventory at a store, then Walmart collects sales tax again from the shopper. Many states let registered sellers buy items for resale without sales tax by giving the store a resale certificate. These are state rules, not IRS rules: you generally need a sales tax permit from your state, and each state and each retailer has its own process. Walmart Business runs a tax exemption program for eligible organizations, but its page doesn't say whether it covers resale purchases, so check before you count on it. Ask your state revenue department or a tax professional.

Keep records from the first sale

The 1099-K shows only gross sales, so your deductions depend on what you can show. Keep receipts for inventory, your payout and fee reports, mileage for sourcing trips, and what you pay for supplies and software. Our Walmart reselling guide covers the business side.

Questions

Does Walmart send sellers a 1099-K?

Yes, if you qualify. Walmart's guide says you get one if you're a W-9 entity with a complete tax profile and shipped more than 200 orders with more than $20,000 in gross sales in the calendar year. Some states have different filing rules.

How do I download my 1099-K from Walmart Seller Center?

It comes from your payment processor, not a Seller Center download. Hyperwallet sellers find it in the Pay Portal under Resources > Tax Documents. Payoneer emails instructions to access it through tax1099.com. If you qualify and didn't get one, contact Seller Support from the Help menu.

Do I have to report Walmart income if I don't get a 1099-K?

Yes. The IRS says that whether or not you receive a Form 1099-K, you must still report any income on your tax return.

Does Walmart collect sales tax for Marketplace sellers?

In the states on Walmart's list, yes. Walmart calculates, collects and remits sales tax there under marketplace facilitator laws. For states not on the list, Walmart passes the tax to you and you remit it.

What tax documents does Walmart ask for when you sign up?

A business tax ID or business license number and a tax form. U.S. sellers with an EIN use a W-9. Sellers incorporated outside the U.S. use a W-8ECI, W-8BEN-E or, for some sole proprietors, a W-8BEN.

Do I pay sales tax on inventory I buy to resell on Walmart?

Usually, unless your state lets you use a resale certificate. That's a state process: you generally need a sales tax permit first. Ask your state revenue department or a tax professional.

Is the 1099-K amount my profit?

No. Walmart's guide says the 1099-K shows gross sales without subtracting fees, refunds, credits or discounts. You subtract those, plus your cost of goods and other expenses, on your return.
