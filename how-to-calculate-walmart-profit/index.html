How to calculate Walmart profit

The exact formula for profit after Walmart's fees, plus ROI, margin, breakeven and the most you should pay, worked through on a real example.

Updated September 27, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Walmart profit is simple to write down and easy to get wrong. The formula is:

Profit = Sale price − Referral fee − WFS fulfillment fee − Storage − Your cost − Prep

Everything except your cost comes from Walmart's fee rules. Here's how to work out each part, then turn it into ROI, margin, breakeven and the most you should pay.

1. Referral fee

Look up the item's category in the Walmart referral fee table. Most are 15%. Multiply by the sale price (including any shipping you charge).

2. WFS fulfillment fee

If Walmart ships for you, find the shipping weight (the greater of actual or dimensional weight, plus 0.25 lb, rounded up) and look up the fee. Add $1 if the item sells for under $10, and $0.50 for apparel or hazmat. If you ship it yourself, use your real label and packaging cost instead.

3. Storage

Cubic feet × $0.75 per month for most of the year. For small items this is pennies; for big or slow items, and for October–December, it adds up.

4. Your cost and prep

What you paid for the item, plus anything to get it ready: poly bags, labels, a prep center, and your inbound shipping per unit.

Worked example

You find a board game you can buy for $10.00. It sells on Walmart for $24.97, weighs 1.2 lb in an 8 × 6 × 4 inch box, and you'll use WFS.

ROI tells you how hard your money is working. Breakeven tells you how far the price can fall before you lose money, which matters when other sellers start cutting prices. Max buy cost is the number to know in the aisle: if the shelf price is above it, walk away.

Mistakes that turn "profit" into a loss

  • Using price minus cost. In the example, that says $14.97 profit. The real number is $6.19.
  • Forgetting dimensional weight. A light item in a big box can jump a weight tier.
  • Missing the under-$10 surcharge. Cheap items pay an extra $1 per unit in WFS.
  • Ignoring the Buy Box. If Walmart or a lower-priced seller holds it, you may not sell at the price you planned.
  • Holding through the holidays. October–December storage after 30 days is three times the normal rate.

Questions

How do you calculate profit on Walmart Marketplace?

Profit equals the sale price minus the Walmart referral fee, minus WFS fulfillment and storage fees (or your own shipping cost), minus what you paid for the item and any prep costs.

How do you calculate ROI for Walmart arbitrage?

ROI equals profit divided by your total cost for the item, including prep. For example, $6.19 profit on a $10 item is a 62% ROI.

What is a good ROI on Walmart?

Many resellers look for at least 30% ROI after all fees, but the right target depends on how fast the item sells and how many sellers compete for the Buy Box.

How do I find the maximum I should pay for an item?

Subtract all Walmart fees from the sale price, then divide by 1 plus your target ROI. For a 30% target, divide by 1.30. If the price you'd pay is higher, pass on the item.
