Walmart best seller rank: is there a BSR, and what to use instead

Walmart has no public sales rank. Here's what it does show, how sales estimates fill the gap, and how to find products that actually sell.

No, Walmart doesn't have a public Walmart best seller rank like Amazon's BSR. Walmart.com product pages don't show a sales rank number, and Walmart doesn't publish unit sales for Marketplace items. What you can see are softer signals: a "Best seller" badge on some products, Walmart's Best Sellers pages by department (with no rank numbers), review counts and ratings, how many sellers are on a listing and how their stock moves. Does Walmart have a best sellers rank?

Not one you can see. On Amazon, every product page lists a Best Sellers Rank in its category, and sellers use it as a rough proxy for sales. Walmart.com has nothing equivalent on the product page, and Walmart doesn't publish how it orders its Best Sellers lists. People searching "Walmart BSR" usually want one of two things: which products sell best on Walmart, or how many units a specific item sells. Those are different questions, and Walmart answers neither with a number.

Walmart best sellers: the signals that do exist

Walmart Best Sellers pages

Walmart runs Best Sellers pages with department filters (books, beauty, electronics, home, toys, pets and more), plus department pages such as Best Sellers in Electronics. The lists can be sorted by "Best Seller." They show products, prices, ratings and review counts, but no rank numbers, no sales figures and no explanation of the time window. Use them to see what's popular in a department, not to compare two items.

The "Best seller" badge

Some product tiles and listings carry a "Best seller" label. Walmart doesn't publish the criteria or any way to apply for it. Treat it as a sign of strong demand for the product, not for any single seller's offer. A Best seller item with ten sellers on it may sell well and still sell slowly for you.

Reviews and ratings

Review count is the easiest public signal to check. A product with thousands of reviews has clearly sold in volume at some point. But reviews pile up over years and can be shared across variations, so a high count doesn't tell you what the item sells this month.

Sellers and stock

The number of sellers on a listing, and whether their stock goes down over time, says a lot about current demand. Stock that drops steadily across sellers means units are moving. Our Walmart stock checker and seller lookup pages explain how to read this.

Seller Center tools (approved sellers only)

Inside Seller Center, Walmart's Assortment Growth tool recommends "Customer Favorites," items with strong demand on Walmart or across the market, using Walmart demand signals and external data refreshed monthly. Walmart's Success Hub also suggests trending items to add. These are recommendations, not a ranking you can look up for any item.

How Walmart sales estimates work

Because there's no public rank, every Walmart sales estimator, including ours, is an estimate built from indirect signals. Here's how Margenos handles it:

  • Labeled with the source. Each estimate shows where it came from, such as Walmart's own sales signals or stock movement, so you can judge it.
  • Shown as a floor when less certain. If the data supports "at least" rather than an exact figure, you'll see something like "430+" instead of a made-up precise number.
  • "Not enough data" when it's thin. New listings and slow items often don't have enough history. Margenos says so rather than guessing.
  • Shared demand. An estimate is for the whole listing. Split it by the number of sellers, how often each wins the Buy Box and their stock to get a realistic share for you.

How to find best selling items on Walmart

  1. Start broad with Walmart's Best Sellers pages in a department you can source from.
  2. Open each product and look past the badge: review count, recent price, number of sellers and who has the Buy Box.
  3. Check the sales estimate and its source. Skip items that show "not enough data" unless you're testing a small quantity.
  4. Count the competition. If Walmart itself or many WFS sellers are on the listing, your share shrinks.
  5. Check price history to see whether the price has held or is sliding into a race to the bottom.
  6. Run the profit after Walmart fees at the price you can actually win at.

Best seller vs best for you

The products at the top of Walmart's Best Sellers lists are often the hardest to profit from: big brands, Walmart selling them itself, and many sellers splitting the Buy Box. A mid-demand item with three sellers and a stable price can earn you more than a best seller with twenty. Demand tells you how fast the listing moves; competition tells you how much of it you'll get; price history tells you whether your margin will last.

Questions

Does Walmart have a best sellers rank like Amazon?

No. Walmart.com doesn't show a sales rank number on product pages, and Walmart doesn't publish unit sales for Marketplace items. It has Best Sellers pages and a "Best seller" badge, but no BSR.

What is Walmart BSR?

"Walmart BSR" is usually a search for an Amazon-style Best Sellers Rank on Walmart. Walmart doesn't have a public one, so sellers rely on badges, reviews, seller and stock signals, and sales estimates.

How does Walmart decide what gets a Best seller badge?

Walmart doesn't publish its criteria or any way to apply for the badge. Treat it as a sign of product demand, not of how well any one seller's offer sells.

Where can I see Walmart's best sellers?

On Walmart.com's Best Sellers pages, which you can filter by department, which can be sorted by "Best Seller." The lists don't show rank numbers or sales figures.

How can I tell how many units a Walmart product sells?

Walmart doesn't publish it, so you need an estimate from indirect signals such as Walmart's own sales signals and stock movement across sellers. Good tools label the source and say when there isn't enough data.

Is a Walmart best seller a good product to resell?

Not automatically. Best sellers often have many sellers, big brands or Walmart itself on the listing. Check the number of sellers, Buy Box winner, price history and your profit after fees first.
