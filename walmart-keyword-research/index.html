How Walmart's own keyword reports work

Both reports live in Seller Center under Analytics, then Search Insights, in the Catalog performance section.

Related keywords report

You type a search phrase and pick a time period. According to Walmart's guide, the report returns:

  • Queries: customer searches related to your phrase.
  • Frequency rank: how popular each search is across all of Walmart. Lower is more popular; a rank of 500 means it was the 500th most common search.
  • Top clicked products: the items shoppers clicked most after that search.
  • Competitive benchmarks: the common product types among top clicked items, with their average price, shipping speed, ratings and reviews.

Because it starts from a phrase rather than one of your items, you can use it on a product you don't sell yet. If nothing comes back, Walmart says there may not have been enough searches for that phrase in the period.

Search query report

This one starts from an item in your catalog. Walmart's guide says it shows which searches drive traffic to that item, with impression share, click share, add-to-cart share, the number of competing products, the number one clicked product and a query score. It covers searches on Walmart.com and the Walmart apps only, not other ways shoppers find products.

Walmart SEO: what the search algorithm looks at

Walmart doesn't publish its search algorithm, so be wary of anyone claiming to know the exact formula. Here is what Walmart does say:

  • Its stated advice: both keyword guides say that "to have the best possible performance on search," sellers should focus on Listing Quality recommendations and check that the assigned product type is correct.
  • What it measures: the Search Insights dashboard ranks your item on impressions, clicks, add-to-cart and sales against other items in the same product type, and shows trends with notes where you changed content or price.
  • Listing Quality and visibility: Walmart's Listing Quality page says the score considers content, discoverability, offer, ratings and reviews, and that improving it can bring "an increase in product visibility." It gives no numbers.

Third-party Walmart SEO guides usually add relevance, sales history, price, shipping speed and stock. That lines up with Walmart's Listing Quality categories, but the exact weights are guesses.

Walmart listing quality score

Walmart's Listing Quality and rewards dashboard guide lists five parts of the score:

  • Content quality: your item name, description, key features and images.
  • Price competitiveness: your price compared with similar products on external marketplaces.
  • Shipping: your promised delivery speed across ZIP codes. Items over 50 lb or priced at $10 or less are left out of this part.
  • Published and in stock: how often the item was available to customers over the last seven days.
  • Ratings and reviews: the number of reviews and the average rating.

Each item gets its own score, and your catalog score is the average of your item scores. You'll find it on the Listing Quality page in Seller Center. Walmart doesn't say how the five parts are weighted. For item setup details, see Walmart product listing; Listing Quality also matters for the Pro Seller badge.

Walmart listing optimization: a practical order

  1. Check the product type. Walmart names this as one of the two things to get right for search.
  2. Pull keywords. Run the Related keywords report on your main phrase, and a reverse lookup on the top clicked products if you use a paid tool.
  3. Write the item name and key features around the searches shoppers actually use, in plain language.
  4. Fill in the description and attributes completely, and add clear images.
  5. Price and ship competitively. Listing Quality compares your price with other marketplaces and scores delivery speed.
  6. Stay in stock. The in-stock part of the score looks at the last seven days.
  7. Re-check monthly. Use the Search query report to see which searches convert and adjust.

Questions

What is the best Walmart keyword research tool?

Start with Walmart's own Search Insights in Seller Center: the Related keywords report and Search query report use real Walmart.com search data at no extra cost. Helium 10 (Cerebro and Magnet) and Sellegr8 add reverse lookups on competitors' products; check their sites for pricing.

Does Helium 10 work for Walmart keyword research?

Yes. Helium 10's help center says Cerebro works with Walmart product IDs (labeled beta), showing keywords with organic and sponsored rank and Walmart search volume on a Very Low to Very High scale. Helium 10 also lists Magnet as Amazon and Walmart compatible.

How does the Walmart search algorithm work?

Walmart doesn't publish it. Its guides say the best way to perform in search is to follow Listing Quality recommendations and make sure your product type is correct. Search Insights ranks items on impressions, clicks, add-to-cart and sales within their product type.

What is the Walmart Listing Quality score?

A Seller Center score made of content quality, price competitiveness, shipping speed, published and in-stock rate, and ratings and reviews. Each item has a score, and your catalog score is the average.

How do I rank higher on Walmart?

Use the right product type, write the item name and key features around real search terms, complete every attribute, price competitively, ship fast, stay in stock and build reviews. If you resell an existing listing, focus on winning the Buy Box instead.

Can I do Walmart keyword research without a seller account?

Walmart's Search Insights reports need Seller Center access. Without an account you can use Walmart.com autocomplete or a third-party tool like Helium 10 or Sellegr8.
