Margenos vs WallySmarter: a WallySmarter alternative for resellers

Both tools are built for Walmart. WallySmarter leans toward private label research; Margenos is built for resellers who scan and source.

Updated November 28, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Reselling on Walmart? Margenos scans barcodes in stores and shows fees, profit, sales and every seller on any Walmart.com listing.
Add to Chrome – 14-day free trial

WallySmarter and Margenos are both Walmart-only tools, but they're built for different sellers. WallySmarter, now part of Carbon6, focuses on research for brands and wholesale sellers: a product database, niche finder, keyword and rank tools, a sales estimator, and a Chrome extension on its Professional plan. Margenos focuses on the reseller's buy-or-pass decision: scan a barcode or open a listing, and see Walmart fees, profit, ROI, a sales estimate, every competing seller with stock, and Buy Box and price history.

Margenos is our product, so we kept this comparison to facts from each company's own pricing pages, checked in November 2026.

When WallySmarter is the better fit

  • You sell your own brand and need keyword research, rank tracking and niche ideas.
  • You want a product database to browse ideas from a desk.

When Margenos is the better fit

  • You source in stores and want to scan a barcode and know in seconds if it's worth buying.
  • You want the Chrome extension on the cheapest plan, on any Walmart.com listing.
  • You care about who else is selling, how much stock they have, and who wins the Buy Box.
  • You don't have a Walmart seller account yet. Margenos doesn't need one.

Questions

What is the best WallySmarter alternative?

It depends on your model. For resellers who source in stores or online, Margenos is a Walmart-only alternative with a barcode scanner app, a Chrome extension on every plan, profit after fees, sales estimates and every seller with stock.

How much does WallySmarter cost?

WallySmarter's pricing page lists Basic at $13 a month and Professional at $66 a month billed annually ($152 and $797 a year), with a 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Check carbon6.io for current prices.

Does WallySmarter have a Chrome extension?

Yes, on the Professional plan. WallySmarter's FAQ says the Basic plan doesn't include its Walmart Chrome extension. Margenos includes its Chrome extension on every plan.

Does WallySmarter have a barcode scanner app?

A phone scanner isn't listed on WallySmarter's pricing page. Margenos has an Android barcode scanner app, and the iPhone app is coming soon.

Who owns WallySmarter?

WallySmarter is part of Carbon6, a group of e-commerce seller software companies.

Can I use Margenos and WallySmarter together?

Yes. Some sellers use WallySmarter for keywords and niche research and Margenos for scanning, profit checks and seller competition.
