Walmart seller payment schedule, holds and payout methods

When Walmart pays Marketplace sellers, why payments get held, which payout methods work, and how Walmart's financing options fit in.

Updated November 10, 2026 · By the Margenos team

The Walmart seller payment schedule is biweekly: Walmart's payment processing guide says "generally, payout occurs biweekly (every 14 days) unless alternate arrangements are made," and its payment activities guide says settlement cycles run on alternate Fridays at midnight Pacific time. Orders confirmed after your cycle's cutoff roll into the next cycle, and new sellers have a payment hold on top of that. Walmart itself notes it "can take up to a month or more to receive funds after an order was shipped."

How often does Walmart pay sellers?

  • Payout frequency: generally every 14 days, set when your account is approved.
  • Cutoff: "All shipment notices or delivery confirmations received after the cutoff will be settled in the following payment cycle." Your cutoff is under Payment Information on the Statements page in Seller Center.
  • Balance updates: Walmart's statements guide says balances update about 24 to 48 hours after an order is marked shipped or delivered.

Where to look: Seller Center's Payments dashboard shows each open cycle's balance and scheduled settlement date, and the Transactions tab searches the past 90 days of orders. The Payments Report (also called the Reconciliation Report) downloads from the Statements screen and shows commission, payable amounts and tax per order. If you're looking for a "Walmart seller pay portal," this is it: payments live in Seller Center under Payments.

Walmart new seller payment hold

Every new seller starts with one. Walmart's new seller payment hold policy (updated Sep 24, 2026) calls it a rolling delay, meaning "a portion of your payments will be delayed, or held back by Walmart." It starts with your first shipped or delivered order.

Seller locationHold lengthWhen it ends
United StatesUp to 14 daysOnce 90 days have passed since your first order shipped or was delivered, and you've received $7,500 in payments
Outside the U.S.Up to 21 daysSame two conditions

Both conditions have to be met. A seller with slow early sales can stay on the hold past 90 days until they reach $7,500 in payments.

Why is my Walmart payment on hold?

Besides the new seller hold, Walmart's payment activities guide lists other reasons money can be held back:

  • Order payment holds. The guide says all Marketplace orders are eligible for payment holds, with the timeframe starting after the order ships, and funds release in the next scheduled settlement cycle.
  • Reserves: "a fixed amount of money set aside to cover expected refunds, chargebacks or other risks," shown as one line on the Statements page.
  • Payment suspensions: "Once suspended, you won't receive any funds until the suspension is lifted." They typically last until a review of your account is done, and payments are suspended during an ongoing legal case on the account.
  • Performance problems. The payment processing guide says high return or chargeback rates, or selling counterfeit or illegal items, "may lead to payment freeze, delays or permanent non-payment."

What is a payment-related account review?

When one of these payment activities applies, Walmart's guide says your account "will go into review," and payments wait until the review finishes. Check Seller Center notifications and respond through Help. If your account is suspended, see Walmart account suspension.

Walmart seller payment methods

Walmart doesn't pay out by ACH direct deposit to your bank. The payment processing guide (updated Oct 30, 2026) says you use Marketplace Wallet or an approved payout provider, depending on where your business is incorporated:

Marketplace WalletUnited States, China and Hong Kong

Hyperwallet isn't on Walmart's current list. If an older guide or video tells you to set up Hyperwallet, go by the current list in Seller Center.

What is Walmart Marketplace Wallet (Seller Wallet)?

Marketplace Wallet is Walmart's own payout account, embedded in Seller Center. For U.S. sellers the deposit account is with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., Member FDIC, and Walmart says Wallet accounts are FDIC-insured up to $250,000 per depositor through its bank providers. Walmart says U.S. proceeds appear in the Wallet "within minutes of disbursement," and you then transfer to a linked bank account; its enrollment guide says ACH transfers take two to three business days, while the Wallet page says one to five. You can use either the Wallet or a third-party provider, not both.

To enroll, the guide says U.S. sellers need an IRS tax ID; you pick Set up on the Marketplace Wallet tile during onboarding, the financial controller submits their details and ID to the bank, and you link a bank account whose name matches the Wallet account. After onboarding, the guide says to contact Support through the Help button.

How to change your Walmart payout method

Walmart's payout settings live under Seller Center's payment and financial settings, covered in its "Set up payout information in Seller Center" guide. Because you can only have one payout method, switching means setting up the new provider and updating payout information; for Marketplace Wallet after onboarding, Walmart says to contact Support through Help. Walmart says it isn't responsible for delays caused by incorrect payout details, so double-check names and account numbers, and switch between cycles rather than right before a settlement date.

Faster payouts, financing and funding for Walmart sellers

Walmart's working capital page mentions a "Faster Payout" option that may let eligible sellers access earnings "as soon as the next day," separate from its capital programs. Ask Seller Support whether you qualify.

For funding, Walmart's Walmart Marketplace Capital guide (updated Aug 21, 2026) describes merchant cash advances based on your Marketplace sales and performance. Eligibility includes at least six months of active Marketplace sales, a U.S. business with a U.S. TIN, compliance with Walmart's seller standards, and no active suspensions or payment holds. Repayment is "fully automatic with deductions directly from your Walmart sales proceeds at each settlement cycle." Eligible sellers see offers on the Capital page in Seller Center. Third-party providers listed there include Payoneer, Parafin, PingPong's Lightyear, Slope and others, each with its own terms.

Borrowing against future payouts only makes sense for inventory that sells. Before you fund a big buy, check each product's fees, profit and sales estimate. The Walmart seller fees page covers what comes out of each payout, and taxes on payouts are covered in Walmart seller taxes. To set up an account first, see Walmart seller account.

Questions

How often does Walmart pay sellers?

Generally every 14 days. Settlement cycles run on alternate Fridays, and orders confirmed after your cycle's cutoff are paid in the next cycle.

How long is the Walmart new seller payment hold?

Up to 14 days for U.S. sellers and up to 21 days outside the U.S. It ends once 90 days have passed since your first order shipped or was delivered and you've received $7,500 in payments.

Why is my Walmart seller payment on hold?

Common reasons are the new seller hold, order-level payment holds, reserves for expected refunds or chargebacks, an account review or suspension, or performance problems such as high return or chargeback rates.

What payment methods does Walmart Marketplace use?

Marketplace Wallet or an approved payout provider such as PayPal (U.S.), Payoneer, PingPong or WorldFirst/Zyla, depending on your country. Walmart doesn't pay by ACH direct deposit, and Hyperwallet isn't on its current list.

What is Walmart Marketplace Wallet?

Walmart's built-in payout account in Seller Center. For U.S. sellers the account is with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., and you transfer funds to a linked bank account. It's available to sellers incorporated in the U.S., China or Hong Kong.

Does Walmart offer faster payouts?

Walmart's working capital page mentions a Faster Payout option that may let eligible sellers access earnings as soon as the next day. Check eligibility with Seller Support.

Does Walmart offer financing for sellers?

Yes. Walmart Marketplace Capital offers merchant cash advances to eligible U.S. sellers with at least six months of sales, repaid automatically from settlements. Third-party providers also appear on the Capital page in Seller Center.
