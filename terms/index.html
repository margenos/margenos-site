What Margenos is

A research tool that helps Walmart Marketplace sellers evaluate products: it calculates Walmart's referral and fulfilment fees, shows sales figures where Walmart has published them, and lets you track products you are considering.

What our numbers mean, and what they do not

Margenos does not guarantee that any product will sell or be profitable. Buying inventory is your decision and your risk. Nothing in this app is financial advice.

We take accuracy seriously and we want you to know exactly what each figure is resting on:

  • Sales figures are shown when Walmart itself has published a purchase badge for that product, or when we have measured stock depletion directly. Where neither exists, the app says "not enough data" rather than estimating. It will not guess a number to fill the space.
  • Badge-derived figures are floors extrapolated from one day. A product selling at that rate yesterday may not hold it all month.
  • Slow-seller warnings are modelled, not measured, and are labelled as such in the app. They are right about 96.5% of the time on the products they speak about, and silent on the rest.
  • Fees are calculated from Walmart's published schedule as of the date shown in the app. Walmart can change them without telling us. Confirm against your Seller Center before committing real money.
  • Profit and ROI use the costs you enter. If you leave a cost blank the app shows a dash, not a zero.
