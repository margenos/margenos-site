How to create a Walmart product listing

Match an existing item or build a new one, get the product ID right, then improve the listing until it sells.

A Walmart product listing (or listing for Walmart Marketplace) is the item page shoppers see on Walmart.com plus your offer on it: your price, stock and shipping. You create listings in Walmart Seller Center after your seller account is approved. There are two paths. If the exact product already exists on Walmart.com, you match it and add your offer. If it doesn't, you set up a new item with a valid GTIN, a title, images and attributes.

Most resellers match existing items. Brand owners and private-label sellers create new ones. Not approved yet? Start with how to sell on Walmart.

Matching an existing listing vs creating a new item

Matching (most resellers)

When you resell a brand-name product, someone has almost always listed it already. You match the existing item by its UPC or other product ID, set your price and quantity, and join the other sellers on that page. You then compete for the Buy Box, the Add to cart button most shoppers use. Before you buy stock for a matched listing, check the price, fees, number of sellers and sales speed. The Margenos Chrome extension shows those on the Walmart.com page, and the Walmart UPC lookup helps you confirm you're matching the right item.

Creating a new item

A new item needs a product ID, a correct category and product type, a title, key features, a description, images and the required attributes for that category. You control the content, but you also own the job of making it findable.

GTIN and UPC requirements

Walmart accepts four product ID types: GTIN, UPC (mainly North America), EAN (mainly outside North America) and ISBN (books only). Walmart checks product IDs against the GS1 database, and IDs that GS1 doesn't recognize can be treated as invalid. Walmart recommends getting your IDs directly from GS1. Cheap barcodes from resellers are a common cause of rejected items.

GTIN exemptions

If your products genuinely don't have manufacturer-issued GTINs, such as private label or handmade goods, a Seller Center admin can request a GTIN exemption. Key points from Walmart's guide:

  • Give a specific reason, such as your assortment lacking manufacturer GTINs. "I need it to list" isn't accepted.
  • The brand name on the request must exactly match the brand you use in item setup.
  • Review can take up to three business days. If Walmart asks for more information, reply within five days or the request closes.
  • Once approved, wait about four hours before creating the item.

Walmart listing quality score

Walmart's Listing Quality & Rewards dashboard in Seller Center scores your catalog and each item. The Walmart listing quality score looks at content and discoverability, your offer (price and shipping speed), and ratings and reviews, and it includes post-purchase quality insights. Walmart doesn't publish an exact formula, but the dashboard shows which items need work. Walmart listing optimization mostly means fixing what it flags:

  • Content: a clear title, accurate category, complete attributes and a detailed description.
  • Offer: a competitive price and fast shipping. Using WFS can help; check the WFS fees first.
  • Ratings and reviews: keep quality and delivery consistent so reviews stay positive.

Title, content and image guidelines

Walmart's content guidance for the product detail page:

  • Title: 150 characters or fewer. Clear and descriptive, with size or count where relevant, and no repeated keywords, multiple brands or multiple product types.
  • Key features: three to 10 benefits, each 80 characters or fewer, with no special-character bullets or HTML.
  • Description: one paragraph of at least 150 words (some categories differ), with no bullet lists.
  • Images: square (1:1), recommended 2200 x 2200 pixels, at least 1500 x 1500 for zoom, main image on a seamless white background. JPEG, PNG or BMP up to 5 MB; no GIFs. Walmart recommends at least four images.

Everything you claim, show or describe must match the product the customer receives.

Variation listings

A variant group puts versions of one product, such as sizes or colors, on a single item page. Each version is still its own item with its own SKU and inventory. Every item in the group shares the same variant group ID, uses the same variant attributes (up to three), and has a unique combination of values. A curtain in 2 sizes and 6 colors is 12 items. Each group needs exactly one primary variant, the default version shoppers see. Swatch images are optional but help shoppers tell colors or patterns apart.

Common Walmart product listing errors

  • Invalid or missing product ID: wrong digit count, dashes or spaces, or a barcode GS1 doesn't recognize.
  • Product ID already set up with a different SKU: the barcode is attached to another SKU in your account. Don't create a second SKU for the same ID.
  • Brand mismatch: the brand on an exempt item doesn't match the brand on the approved exemption.
  • Images that don't meet the specs: too small, wrong format or a busy main-image background.
  • Restricted brand or category: some need approval before you can list them.
  • Resubmitting too soon: item feeds take time to process. Wait for the result instead of uploading the same file again.

A clean listing still has to make money. Before you list a product, run it through the Walmart profit calculator or see how to calculate Walmart profit.

Questions

How do I list products on Walmart Marketplace?

Once your seller account is approved, open Seller Center and add items. Match an existing Walmart.com item by product ID and add your price and stock, or set up a new item with a GTIN, title, images and attributes. Use a bulk template or API for many items.

Do I need a UPC to sell on Walmart?

Usually, yes. Walmart accepts GTIN, UPC, EAN or ISBN and checks IDs against the GS1 database. If your products have no manufacturer GTIN, such as private label or handmade goods, you can request a GTIN exemption.

Can I list an item that's already on Walmart.com?

Yes. You match the existing listing and add your own offer. You'll share the page with other sellers and compete for the Buy Box, so check price, fees and competition first.

What is the Walmart listing quality score?

It's a score in Seller Center's Listing Quality & Rewards dashboard, given at the catalog and item level. It considers content and discoverability, your offer, and ratings and reviews, and shows which items to improve.

How long can a Walmart product title be?

Walmart's guidance is 150 characters or fewer. Keep it clear, include size or count where relevant, and avoid repeated keywords or multiple brands.

How long does Walmart item setup take?

It varies. Many items process within hours, but some take longer. GTIN exemption requests can take up to three business days to review. Wait for a feed to finish before resubmitting.

Is there a Walmart listing tool for resellers?

Seller Center handles listing itself. For deciding what to list, Margenos shows fees, profit, sellers, stock and sales estimates on any Walmart.com product page, and you don't need a seller account to use it.

Why is my Walmart listing not showing?

Usually because the item is unpublished. Walmart's unpublished items guide lists common causes such as a missing primary image or price, shipping cost that's too high relative to the item price, a price well above recent prices, a price that looks like an error, or a UPC mismatch. Check the Unpublished Items dashboard in Seller Center for the reason code and fix it there; restricted categories you aren't approved for can also block an item.

Should I pay for Walmart product listing services?

Only if it saves you more than it costs, and only with a provider you can verify. Resellers matching existing listings rarely need one, because matching takes a UPC, price and inventory. Brands creating many new items can find vetted agencies and listing software in Walmart's Solution Provider Hub; add any helper as a Seller Center user rather than sharing your login.
