How to resell Walmart clearance for profit

A step-by-step guide to flipping Walmart clearance: finding deals, checking profit after Walmart's fees, and avoiding the mistakes that eat your margin.

Updated September 27, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Reselling Walmart clearance means buying marked-down items, in stores or on Walmart.com, and selling them for more, often on Walmart Marketplace itself. It works when the discount is big enough to survive Walmart's fees, and the item still sells at a good price online.

Step 1: Get set up before you shop

  • A Walmart Marketplace seller account. Walmart reviews applications, so apply early.
  • A resale certificate if your state offers one, so you aren't paying sales tax on inventory you resell. Rules vary by state.
  • A scanner app that shows Walmart fees and profit, not just the price.

Step 2: Find clearance

In stores, check end caps, clearance aisles and seasonal sections, especially right after holidays. Online, sort categories by price and look for items priced well below what other sellers charge. Toys, home goods, beauty and seasonal items are common clearance categories.

Step 3: Scan and check the numbers

For every item, check four things before it goes in your cart:

CheckWhy it matters
Profit after all feesA 50% markdown can still lose money after the 15% referral fee and WFS fees. See how to calculate Walmart profit.
Max buy costThe most you can pay and still hit your target ROI. If the clearance price is higher, pass.
Who holds the Buy BoxIf Walmart itself sells it for less, you'll struggle to sell at your price.
How many sellers and how fast it sellsLots of sellers on a slow item means price cuts and storage fees while you wait.

Step 4: Watch the traps

  • Items under $10. WFS adds $1 per unit, so cheap clearance needs an even bigger spread.
  • Restricted brands and categories. Some need Walmart approval before you can list them. Check before you buy a stack.
  • Condition and packaging. Items must match the listing and be new. Crushed boxes lead to returns.
  • Holiday storage. WFS storage triples on items held past 30 days in October–December.
  • Buying too many. Buy a few, see how they sell, then buy more.

Step 5: Prep and send to WFS (or ship yourself)

If you use WFS, follow Walmart's packaging and labeling rules. Walmart charges $0.65 per unit to fix a missing or wrong label and $0.80 for a missing or wrong poly bag. Check the WFS fee chart so the fulfillment fee is already in your math.

Example

A toy is on clearance for $10.00 and sells on Walmart.com for $24.97. After the 15% referral fee ($3.75), WFS fulfillment ($4.95) and a month of storage ($0.08), you keep $6.19 per unit, a 62% ROI. At $13.00 on the shelf, it would only make about $3.19, and above $16.19 you'd lose money.

Questions

Can you resell Walmart clearance items?

Yes. Many resellers buy Walmart clearance in stores or online and resell it on Walmart Marketplace or other sites. Check that the item isn't in a restricted brand or category and that it's still profitable after fees.

How do I know if a clearance item is profitable?

Subtract the Walmart referral fee (usually 15%), WFS fulfillment and storage fees, and your cost from the price the item sells for online. A scanner app like Margenos does this when you scan the barcode.

What is the best app to scan Walmart clearance?

Look for an app that shows profit after Walmart fees, ROI, the maximum you should pay, and who holds the Buy Box. The Margenos scanner app and Chrome extension show all of these.

Do I have to pay sales tax on items I resell?

Many states let resellers use a resale certificate to avoid paying sales tax on inventory they resell. Rules vary by state, so check with your state's tax agency.

Why can a clearance deal still lose money?

Walmart's referral fee, WFS fees, the extra $1 fee on items under $10, storage and price drops from other sellers can wipe out the discount. Always check profit after fees before buying.
