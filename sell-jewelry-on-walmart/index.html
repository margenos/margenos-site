Fees, the approval rule for gold, silver and gemstones, what Walmart bans, and how resellers check a piece before they buy it.

To sell jewelry on Walmart, you need an approved Walmart Marketplace seller account and new items that follow Walmart's jewelry policy. Fashion jewelry (non-precious metals, cubic zirconia, glass, acrylic and rhinestones) can be listed without extra approval. Jewelry and watches containing precious metals or natural gemstones are restricted: Walmart's Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Compliance team has to approve you first. Walmart charges a 20% referral fee on the portion of a jewelry sale up to $250 and 5% on the portion above $250. Walmart's referral fee for jewelry

Walmart's published schedule lists Jewelry & Precious Metals at 20% on the portion of the total sales price up to $250, and 5% on the portion over $250. It's one of the highest rates on Walmart, so low-priced fashion jewelry has to be bought cheaply to work. The total sales price includes the item price plus shipping and other charges, and the fee is only charged after a sale. Watches have their own category: 15% on the portion up to $1,500 and 3% above that.

Hypothetical example: a pair of earrings sells for $39.99 with free shipping. The referral fee is 20% × $39.99 = $8.00. A ring that sells for $400 pays 20% × $250 = $50 plus 5% × $150 = $7.50, so $57.50 in total, or about 14.4%. You can check any price with the Walmart referral fee calculator.

Does jewelry need approval on Walmart?

It depends on what it's made of. Walmart's jewelry policy splits it like this:

TypeStatus on Walmart Marketplace
Fashion jewelry: non-precious metals, rhinestones, acrylic or fake stones, cubic zirconia, glassAllowed without extra approval
Jewelry and watches with precious metals or gemstones, loose gems, bullionRestricted: needs approval from Walmart's AML Compliance team
Natural gemstones, moissanite and lab-grown diamondsAllowed with restrictions (approved sellers)
Items with conflict minerals; Russian-origin diamonds or gold; clarity-enhanced, laser-drilled, fracture-filled or non-Kimberley Process diamondsProhibited

Restricted items must also follow Walmart's other prohibited products policies, including its covered goods rules, or they get unpublished. If you're new, start with fashion jewelry and apply for the restricted category once your account has a track record.

Metal and stone claims

Walmart requires jewelry listings to disclose the metal: its type (gold, silver), purity (14K, 925), how it's applied (solid, plated, vermeil) and weight. Don't misstate the metal, purity or amount, or advertise a false weight or value. In practice that means your listing data has to match the stamp on the piece and the supplier's invoice. "Gold" on a gold-plated chain is a fast way to get an item pulled.

What jewelry sells on Walmart

Walmart shoppers lean toward everyday and gift pieces: sterling silver and fashion earrings, birthstone and initial necklaces, men's chains and bracelets, kids' jewelry sets and watches. Demand climbs around Valentine's Day, Mother's Day and the holidays. Because the referral fee is 20% on most of these prices, the winners are usually pieces you can buy for a fraction of the selling price, or higher-priced items where the 5% band above $250 lowers the overall rate.

Risks for jewelry resellers

  • Counterfeits and brand claims. Designer names are heavily copied and enforced. Buy only from sources with invoices, and read Walmart restricted brands first.
  • Returns. Jewelry gets returned for fit, size and "looks different in person". Price that in.
  • Thin margins on cheap pieces. On a $12 bracelet, the 20% fee plus shipping or WFS fees leaves little room.
  • Walmart and big sellers. Check who's on the listing and who usually wins the Buy Box before you buy deep.

Checklist before you buy jewelry to resell

  1. Check the material and whether it needs AML approval.
  2. Confirm the metal and stone details on the invoice match what you'll list.
  3. Run profit after the 20% fee, shipping or WFS, and returns.
  4. Check the sales estimate, the number of sellers and their stock.
  5. Check the price history for holiday spikes you shouldn't count on.

Questions

What is Walmart's referral fee for jewelry?

Walmart's published schedule lists Jewelry & Precious Metals at 20% on the portion of the total sales price up to $250 and 5% on the portion over $250. Watches are 15% up to $1,500 and 3% above.

Do I need approval to sell jewelry on Walmart?

Fashion jewelry made with non-precious metals, cubic zirconia, glass, acrylic or rhinestones doesn't need extra approval. Jewelry and watches with precious metals or gemstones, loose gems and bullion need approval from Walmart's Anti-Money Laundering Compliance team.

What jewelry is banned on Walmart Marketplace?

Walmart prohibits jewelry with conflict minerals, diamonds or gold mined or made in Russia, and diamonds described as clarity-enhanced, laser drilled, fracture filled or deep boiled, or that don't comply with the Kimberley Process.

Can I resell jewelry on Walmart?

Yes, if it's new, accurately described and allowed for your account. Walmart only lets invited sellers list pre-owned or open-box items, so used jewelry isn't allowed for most sellers.
