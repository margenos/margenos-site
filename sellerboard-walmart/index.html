Does sellerboard work for Walmart? And what it doesn't do

Sellerboard tracks the profit of what you've already sold. Margenos tells you what to buy. Here's how the two fit for Walmart sellers.

Updated November 28, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Need to know what to buy, not just what you made? Margenos shows fees, profit, sales and sellers on any Walmart listing before you buy.

Yes, sellerboard supports Walmart, but it's a different kind of tool from Margenos. Sellerboard is profit analytics: it connects to your seller accounts and reports what you earned on orders you've already sold. According to the sellerboard changelog (Aug 17, 2026), it has a Walmart-only Summary Dashboard that combines connected Walmart accounts, flags Walmart MCF/WFS orders so they can be kept out of profit numbers, and a Walmart reports page with XLSX and CSV exports.

Margenos is a sourcing tool. It works before you buy: scan a barcode or open a Walmart.com listing to see the referral and WFS fees, profit, ROI, a sales estimate, every competing seller with stock, and Buy Box and price history. It doesn't need your seller account connected.

Do you need both?

If you already sell on Walmart at volume, a profit tracker like sellerboard helps you see which products actually made money after fees, ads and returns. A sourcing tool like Margenos helps you choose the next products. Many sellers run one of each. If you're just starting, the sourcing side matters first, because a bad buy can't be fixed by better reporting.

Questions

Does sellerboard work with Walmart?

Yes. Sellerboard's changelog describes a Walmart-only Summary Dashboard, handling for Walmart MCF/WFS orders and a Walmart reports page with XLSX and CSV exports.

Is sellerboard a sourcing tool?

No. Sellerboard is profit analytics for orders you've already sold. For deciding what to buy for Walmart, use a sourcing tool like Margenos.

What is a sellerboard alternative for Walmart?

It depends on the job. For profit reporting, other analytics tools exist. For sourcing, Margenos shows fees, profit, sales estimates and competing sellers on any Walmart.com listing before you buy.

Do I need a Walmart seller account for Margenos?

No. Margenos works without connecting a seller account, so you can research products before you're approved.

Can I use sellerboard and Margenos together?

Yes. Use Margenos to choose what to buy and sellerboard to track the profit of what you sold.
