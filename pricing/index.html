How much does Margenos cost? Margenos pricing has three plans: Pro at $24.99 a month, Plus at $34.99 a month and Elite at $44.99 a month. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. A card is needed to start the trial, you aren't charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before then. Yearly billing saves 20%. Prices are in US dollars.

Margenos is a Walmart reseller tool (Chrome extension and Android app) that shows whether a product is worth selling on Walmart Marketplace: fees, profit, ROI, sales estimates, every competing seller with stock, the Buy Box winner and the product's price and Buy Box history. The plans differ mainly in how many products you can check each month. The core research features are the same on all three.

What every Margenos plan includes

You don't lose research features by picking the cheaper plan. Pro, Plus and Elite all include:

  • The Chrome extension and the Android app, on as many devices as you like, on one account.
  • Barcode scan, name search and listing lookup. Variations with price and stock show in the Chrome extension.
  • Profit, ROI, breakeven and max buy cost, with quantity, prep cost and WFS storage months in every calculation.
  • Walmart referral and WFS fees for the product's category and weight.
  • Monthly sales estimates, labeled with their source, or "not enough data" when there's nothing reliable to base one on.
  • Every competing seller with price, stock and whether they use WFS or ship themselves.
  • A Buy Box breakdown showing who usually wins it.
  • Sold-out detection, with the sellers recently on the listing.
  • Price, Buy Box and stock charts with up to a year of history.
  • Push alerts for price moves, stock-outs and sellers joining or leaving.
  • Slow-seller warnings, scan history and saved products.
  • CSV and Google Sheets exports.

Elite adds two tools for heavy sourcing: photo search, for items with no barcode you can scan, and CartIQ, which adds up the profit on everything in your cart while you shop.

How the 14-day free trial works

  1. Pick a plan. Install the Chrome extension or the Android scanner app and choose Pro, Plus or Elite.
  2. Add a card. A card is needed to start the trial. Nothing is charged during the 14 days.
  3. Use the full plan. Scan in stores, open Walmart.com listings, set alerts and export your finds.
  4. Keep it or cancel. If you do nothing, your plan starts when the trial ends. To stop, cancel from Manage subscription in the app or the extension before the 14 days are up.

You don't need a Walmart seller account to use Margenos, so you can try it while your seller application is still being reviewed. See how to open a Walmart seller account if you haven't applied yet.

Which Margenos plan should I pick?

Start by counting how many products you check in a normal week, then multiply by four. Your scan allowance is what you use to check products, so that number tells you roughly which plan fits. You can change plans later, so it's fine to start small.

Pick Pro if you're getting started

Pro suits part-time resellers, people learning Walmart arbitrage, and anyone who mostly researches online in Chrome. 2,000 scans a month is about 65 a day, which covers a store trip or two a week plus some online sourcing. The 60 storefront lookups let you study a couple of competing sellers each day.

Pick Plus if you source full-time

With 10,000 scans a month you can scan whole clearance aisles without rationing, and 300 storefront lookups make seller lookups part of your daily routine. If you ran out of scans on Pro during the trial, this is your plan.

Pick Elite for teams and high volume

Elite gives 20,000 scans and 1,000 storefront lookups a month, and it's the only plan with photo search and CartIQ. It's built for teams sharing one account across devices, high-volume online arbitrage, and in-store sourcing where many items have no barcode or where you want to see a full cart's profit before checkout.

Monthly or yearly?

Yearly plans cost 20% less than paying monthly for twelve months. Pro yearly is $239.90 (instead of $299.88), Plus yearly is $335.90 (instead of $419.88) and Elite yearly is $431.90 (instead of $539.88). Pay monthly while you're testing whether Walmart reselling works for you. Switch to yearly once Margenos is part of how you source every week.

Is Margenos worth the price?

The honest test: one bad buy avoided a month usually covers the subscription. Buying a case of something that turns out to have twelve sellers, months of stock or Walmart itself on the listing can cost more than a year of Pro. Margenos is built to catch that before you pay, with raw numbers rather than "safe" or "risky" verdicts, so you make the call.

It's also fair to say when you might not need it. If you only list a few products a month, Walmart's own Seller Center and our Walmart profit calculator may be enough. WAMP, SellerAmp's Walmart extension, lists a beta plan at $0 a month on its site. Compare the options in best Walmart seller tools and Margenos vs WAMP.

Does Margenos work on iPhone?

Not yet. Margenos runs as a Chrome extension on computers and as an Android app on Google Play. The iPhone app isn't out. If you use an iPhone, you can still use the Chrome extension on a computer during your trial. All plans work on as many devices as you like.
