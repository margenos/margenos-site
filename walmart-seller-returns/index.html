Walmart seller return policy: windows, labels and fees

The return rules Walmart sets for Marketplace sellers, who pays for the label, and how returns change your margin.

The Walmart seller return policy is set by Walmart, not by each seller. Marketplace sellers must accept eligible returns for at least 30 days (the window starts seven days after the ship date), can't charge customers for return shipping, and generally can't charge restocking fees. Walmart prints the return label through its Returns Shipping Service by default and bills the label to the seller. Sellers can choose returnless refunds through Keep It rules. If you don't follow the policy, Walmart can refund the customer for you and charge your account.

Walmart third party seller return policy: the rules

Walmart's returns policy for Marketplace sellers (updated Aug 31, 2026) says you must accept eligible returns "regardless of the customer's reason for the return and the condition of the returned item," apart from exceptions such as Final Sale items. You can't negotiate with customers, discourage a return or delay it.

Shoppers see the same rule from the other side: Walmart's customer return policy says items sold and shipped by a Marketplace seller may be returned within 30 days.

Walmart return shipping labels for sellers

Walmart's Returns Shipping Service (RSS) policy says RSS is the default label for Marketplace returns and is always used for returns started in a Walmart store. Walmart picks the carrier and uses discounted rates, and your business is listed as the shipper of record. A few details matter for your costs:

  • You pay for the label. Return shipping fees you owe Walmart show up in your Payments Report as part of your settlement.
  • Rates use the higher of actual or dimensional weight, and can be adjusted after shipment. Walmart doesn't publish RSS rates, and the Retailer Agreement prohibits sellers from sharing them.
  • You can use your own FedEx or UPS account instead, by adding it in Seller Center. If Walmart can't use your carrier details, RSS is applied.
  • Lost or damaged returns are your claim to file with the carrier; Walmart isn't liable under RSS.

For items with a return exemption (freight, multi-box and luxury items, for example), customers contact you directly and you must send a return label within 48 hours, or Walmart Customer Care may refund the customer and charge you.

Walmart returnless refunds: Keep It rules

Sometimes it's cheaper to let the customer keep the item than to pay for the return label. Walmart's Keep It rules guide explains two options on the Returns page in Seller Center:

  • Keep It rules: a full refund with no return, set by department, item price limit and return order limit.
  • Partial Keep It rules: a partial refund by department and return reason, with a percent off and a maximum amount. They can't be used on items with return exemptions.

Walmart also lets you add Replacement rules for some return reasons, such as a damaged or wrong item. The policy says negotiating other solutions is prohibited unless they come through these pre-approved rules.

Walmart Marketplace returns with WFS

If Walmart Fulfillment Services ships your item, Walmart handles the return. You pay a return processing fee based on shipping weight, starting at $4.70 for 1 lb or less, according to Walmart's WFS fees page. Walmart waives it for returns it caused, such as lost in transit, damaged or late items, and the wrong item sent. Compare fulfillment costs on WFS fees and WFS vs FBA.

Returns and your account health

Walmart's seller performance standards set a Return Rate target of 6% or lower over 60 days (9% for Resold inventory). Running above it is one of the paths to suppression or suspension; see Walmart account suspension. If you think a return is unfair, the policy says you may dispute the charges if the return is eligible for a dispute.

Returns eat margin: check it before you buy

Every return costs you a label (or a WFS return fee), sometimes a full refund on an item you can't resell, and your time. A product with a thin margin can turn into a loss after a few returns. Before you buy, check the numbers. Questions

What is Walmart's return policy for third-party sellers?

Marketplace sellers must accept eligible returns for at least 30 days, starting seven days after the ship date, for any reason, with exceptions such as Final Sale items. They can't charge return shipping and can't refuse, delay or negotiate a return.

Who pays for return shipping on Walmart Marketplace?

The seller. Walmart's Returns Shipping Service creates the label by default and the cost appears in the seller's Payments Report. Sellers can't charge the customer for return shipping.

Can Walmart sellers charge a restocking fee?

Generally no. Walmart allows up to 20% of the item price only for items with an approved return exemption that come back not in original or sellable condition.

Does Walmart offer returnless refunds for sellers?

Yes. Keep It rules let you refund the customer in full without a return, by department, item price limit and return order limit. Partial Keep It rules give a partial refund instead.

Can Marketplace items be returned to a Walmart store?

Yes, for eligible items, based on your return settings. Freight, oversized, luxury, major appliance and fully regulated hazmat items, among others, aren't eligible for in-store return.

Can I dispute a return on Walmart?

Walmart's policy says that, if eligible, you may dispute charges for a customer return. Some returns can't be disputed, such as packages rerouted because your return address was invalid.

Does Margenos handle Walmart returns?

No. Margenos doesn't process returns or refunds. It shows fees, profit, ROI and sales estimates on any Walmart listing so you can check there's enough margin to absorb returns before you buy.

Can you return Pro Seller items to a Walmart store?

Often, yes. A Pro Seller is still a third-party Marketplace seller, so the same rules apply: Walmart's Marketplace help page says items sold and shipped by Marketplace sellers can be returned to a Walmart store within 30 days, without a box or label, subject to restrictions. Eligibility depends on the seller's return settings, and large, heavy or freight items are usually excluded. See what a Walmart Pro Seller is.

Can you return clothes bought from a Walmart Marketplace seller?

Yes, in most cases. Walmart's returns policy for sellers requires Marketplace sellers to accept eligible returns for at least 30 days regardless of the reason, and they can't charge you return shipping. Start the return from your Walmart.com order; many items can also go back to a store, depending on the seller's return settings.
