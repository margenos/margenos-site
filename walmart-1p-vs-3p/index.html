Walmart 1P vs 3P: supplier, drop ship vendor or Marketplace seller

Selling to Walmart and selling on Walmart are different businesses. Here's how each route works and who it fits.

Updated November 10, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Walmart 1P vs 3P is about who sells to the shopper. As a 1P supplier, you sell to Walmart: Walmart buys your products and sells them in its stores, at Sam's Club or on Walmart.com. As a 3P Marketplace seller, you sell to the shopper yourself on Walmart.com, set your own price and pay Walmart a referral fee when an item sells. A drop ship vendor (DSV) sits in between: Walmart.com sells the item, but you ship it straight to the customer. "1P" and "3P" are industry shorthand; Walmart's own pages say "supplier" and "Marketplace seller."

1P: selling to Walmart as a supplier

This is the "get my product on Walmart's shelves" path. Walmart's supplier page describes it as becoming a supplier for Walmart stores, Walmart.com and Sam's Clubs. Its apply page says U.S. businesses should complete the application to be considered, starting with the Supplier Checklist and then creating an account in Retail Link. It also warns that registering "does not guarantee you will be extended an agreement to do business with Walmart." A Walmart merchant still has to want your product.

Walmart vendor requirements

Walmart's supplier requirements page lists, among other things:

  • Federal tax ID for U.S. suppliers, or a current W-8 for non-U.S. suppliers.
  • A GS1 Company Prefix, so your items carry their own GTINs/UPCs.
  • Insurance of the types Walmart requires, kept in force at all times.
  • Audits, inspections and tests of products, facilities and records, depending on the product, such as food safety or product safety audits.
  • Packaging that meets Walmart's supply chain packaging guide.

Walmart supplier portal: Retail Link and Supplier One

New suppliers start qualification by creating a Retail Link account. Walmart says existing suppliers get "exclusive tools and resources through Supplier One," its platform for managing the supplier relationship. Neither is the place to sell as a Marketplace seller; that's Seller Center. Our guide on how to get your product into Walmart covers the supplier route and Open Call in more detail.

Walmart DSV (drop ship vendor)

A Walmart DSV supplies Walmart.com but keeps the inventory and ships orders directly to customers. Walmart's Supplier One help docs say the DSV program is focused on high-volume suppliers and that you must be invited by a Walmart.com category specialist (buyer). You can't just sign up.

The DSV policy guide describes the vendor as "an agent acting on behalf of Walmart.com" and sets strict standards, including:

  • Acknowledge every purchase order line within 4 business hours.
  • Hand at least 99% of orders to the carrier by the expected ship date, with tracking.
  • Ship with the carrier and method Walmart assigns, from the contiguous 48 states.
  • Use plain, unbranded boxes with no packing slips or marketing inserts.
  • Send at least one inventory feed every business day and keep backorders at or below 0.1% a month.

Late shipments and rejected units above the allowances carry $5 chargebacks, and repeated problems can lead to suspension. Don't confuse DSV with "dropshipping" as a Marketplace seller, which has its own rules; see Walmart dropshipping.

3P: the Walmart marketplace

As a 3P seller you list your own offers on Walmart.com, often on the same listing as other sellers and sometimes Walmart itself. Walmart's supplier page says Marketplace is for "select third party retailers," and that Walmart.com has more than 90 million monthly visitors. Walmart's Marketplace site says there are no setup, monthly or hidden fees; you pay a referral fee when an item sells, 15% in many categories and lower or tiered in others. Details are on Walmart seller fees.

To apply, Walmart asks for:

  • A business tax ID or business license number (an SSN isn't accepted) and documents verifying your business name and address.
  • A history of marketplace or ecommerce success.
  • Products with GTINs/UPCs from GS1 company prefixes, and a catalog that follows Walmart's prohibited products policy.
  • Fulfillment through WFS or another U.S. warehouse that handles returns.
  • An account with a compatible payment processor.

Walmart's registration guide says business verification can take from a few minutes to two business days, and new sellers face a payment hold after they start selling. For the full process, see how to open a Walmart seller account.

Which one fits you?

  • You make a product and want store shelves: 1P supplier registration, knowing it can take a long time and isn't guaranteed.
  • You're already a high-volume supplier and a Walmart.com buyer is interested: DSV.
  • You want to sell on Walmart.com soon, or you resell other brands: 3P Marketplace, with or without WFS.

The routes aren't exclusive. Walmart's supplier page notes that many suppliers use Marketplace to complement their broader Walmart business.

Resellers are 3P sellers

If you buy products from stores, clearance, online retailers or wholesalers and resell them, the 1P and DSV routes don't apply to you. Questions

What is the difference between 1P and 3P on Walmart?

1P means you're a supplier selling to Walmart, and Walmart sells to the shopper. 3P means you're a Marketplace seller selling directly to shoppers on Walmart.com and paying Walmart a referral fee on each sale.

What is a Walmart DSV?

A drop ship vendor supplies Walmart.com but keeps the inventory and ships each order directly to the customer. Walmart's Supplier One help docs say the program targets high-volume suppliers and requires an invitation from a Walmart.com category specialist.

Is Walmart DSV the same as dropshipping on Walmart Marketplace?

No. A DSV ships orders for Walmart.com under a supplier agreement. A Marketplace seller who dropships is a 3P seller using a supplier to fulfill their own orders, and must follow Walmart's Marketplace rules.

How do I become a Walmart supplier?

Review Walmart's Supplier Checklist, then create an account in Retail Link to start qualification. You'll need things like a federal tax ID, a GS1 Company Prefix and the required insurance. Walmart says registering doesn't guarantee an agreement.

What is the Walmart supplier portal?

New suppliers start in Retail Link. Existing suppliers use Supplier One, Walmart's platform for supplier tools and resources. Marketplace sellers use Seller Center instead.

Can a reseller become a Walmart supplier?

The supplier route is for businesses Walmart buys from directly, and a Walmart merchant has to want your product. If you resell other brands' products, the practical route is 3P: apply as a Marketplace seller through Seller Center.

Does Walmart charge 3P sellers a monthly fee?

No. Walmart says Marketplace has no setup, monthly or hidden fees. You pay a referral fee when an item sells, plus WFS fees if you use Walmart's fulfillment.
