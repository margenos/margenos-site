Strong seasons, bulky items and a short list of weapons and state rules to know.

To sell sports and outdoors gear on Walmart, you need a Walmart Marketplace seller account and new items in retail packaging. Most of the category is open, the referral fee is 15% (8% for some optics like binoculars), and the main restrictions involve weapons and products some states don't allow. Demand is highly seasonal. Walmart's referral fee for sports and outdoors

Walmart's published schedule lists Outdoors Products and Sports at 15%, except 8% for hunting trail monitors, binoculars, telescopes, spotting scopes and night vision goggles. Outdoor Power Tools are a separate category: 15% at $500 or less and 8% above $500. The fee is a share of the total sales price, including shipping.

Hypothetical examples: a $49.99 camping hammock with free shipping pays 15% = $7.50. A pair of $89.99 binoculars pays 8% = $7.20. A $64.99 trail camera, in the hunting trail monitor exception, pays 8% = $5.20. See Walmart seller fees.

Restrictions in sports and outdoors

Sporting goods aren't on Walmart's general pre-approval list, but several rules apply:

  • Weapons: Walmart has separate prohibited-products policies for air-powered guns, firearms, firearm accessories, ammunition, and knives and other melee weapons. Read them before listing anything in hunting or tactical.
  • State restrictions: Walmart's restricted products policy names some air guns and knives among products that can't be sold in every state, and generally prohibits them unless it lets you set the states where they can ship.
  • Hazardous items: Walmart's hazardous items policy requires shipping under DOT rules and lets Walmart prohibit anything it deems unsafe, which matters for fuel canisters and outdoor chemicals.
  • Ride-ons and e-bikes have their own micromobility policy.
  • Brands: premium outdoor and fitness brands often enforce authorized sellers; see Walmart restricted brands.

What sells and seasonality

This category follows the calendar. Fitness equipment and yoga gear peak in January. Camping, fishing, water sports, pools and bikes rise in spring and summer. Team sports follow their seasons, with back-to-school spikes for sports gear. Hunting optics and trail cameras rise in late summer and fall, and Q4 brings gift sales across the category. Off-season, prices often drop and stock sits.

Sourcing sports and outdoors, and the risks

Retail and clearance: end-of-season clearance on camping, pool and fitness gear is a classic buy-low, hold, sell-in-season play; see Walmart clearance reselling. Only hold if you've priced storage. Online arbitrage: works for lighter accessories; see online arbitrage on Walmart. Wholesale: good for repeatable accessories; see Walmart wholesale.

  • Bulky and heavy items: weights, kayaks, tents and bikes have high shipping and storage costs.
  • Seasonal timing: buying in season at full price often means selling into a falling market.
  • Variations: sizes and colors on apparel and footwear sell unevenly; check the exact variation.
  • Walmart sells a lot of sporting goods itself, including its own brands.
  • Counterfeits in branded apparel and fitness trackers.

Checklist before you buy sports and outdoors gear

  1. Check weapon and state rules for anything hunting, knife or air-gun related.
  2. Confirm the fee rate: 15%, or 8% for the named optics.
  3. Weigh and measure for shipping or WFS.
  4. Check sellers, stock and Walmart's offer, and the exact variation.
  5. Check price history to see what it sold for in and out of season.

WFS notes for sports and outdoors

WFS works well for compact accessories. For big items, check WFS's big and bulky rules and run WFS fees with realistic storage months, since off-season stock can sit. DOT fully regulated hazardous items are prohibited in WFS.

Questions

What is Walmart's referral fee for sports and outdoors?

Outdoors Products and Sports is 15%, except 8% for hunting trail monitors, binoculars, telescopes, spotting scopes and night vision goggles.

Do sporting goods need approval on Walmart?

Most don't. Weapons such as air guns, knives and firearm accessories have their own policies, and some products can't be sold in every state.

Can I sell knives on Walmart Marketplace?

Walmart has a dedicated knives and melee weapons policy, and its restricted products policy names some knives as not sellable in all states. Read both before listing.

When do sports and outdoor products sell best?

Fitness peaks in January, camping, water and pool gear in spring and summer, hunting optics in fall, and gifts in Q4.

Is buying off-season outdoor clearance worth it?

It can be if you account for storage and the price you'll realistically get in season. Check the price history and sales estimate first; Margenos shows both on the listing.
