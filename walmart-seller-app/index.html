The Walmart Seller app: what it does and how to get it

Walmart's free app for running your Marketplace store, what it can't do, and the app you need for choosing products.

Yes, Walmart has an official Walmart Seller app. It's free on iPhone and Android, and it lets approved U.S. Walmart Marketplace sellers run their store from a phone: ship orders, update prices and inventory, answer customers and check sales. It is a store-management app. It doesn't tell you which products are worth buying to sell, which is a different kind of app. This page covers what the Walmart Seller app does, how to download it, and where a product research tool like Margenos fits.

What the Walmart Seller app does

According to Walmart's Marketplace pages, the app covers most day-to-day store tasks:

  • Orders: acknowledge, view and cancel orders, print packing slips, update tracking, mark orders shipped and view returns.
  • Items: add items to your catalog, view item details, and update pricing and inventory.
  • Walmart Fulfillment Services: monitor WFS inventory and track shipments into Walmart's warehouses. See WFS fees for what that costs.
  • Sales: daily, weekly and monthly sales on the home screen, plus a sales tracker.
  • Alerts: push notifications for new orders, customer messages, at-risk orders and out-of-stock items.
  • Customers and support: reply in the Message Center and create or manage support cases.
  • Payments: view payment statements.

How to download the Walmart Seller app

  1. Get approved first. The app is only for existing U.S. Marketplace sellers. If you haven't applied yet, start with our guide to a Walmart seller account.
  2. Log in to Seller Center on a computer. Walmart says you need to be enrolled and to have logged in to Seller Center before using the app.
  3. Install the app. Search for "Walmart Seller" by Walmart in the App Store or Google Play, or use the links on Walmart's Seller App page.
  4. Sign in with your Seller Center details. The app uses the same login as Walmart Seller Center.
  5. Turn on notifications. Order and at-risk alerts are the main reason to have the app on your phone.

The iPhone version needs iOS 16 or later. Walmart's help guide also says Android users can install the app's .apk file directly if they can't use Google Play.

What the Walmart Seller app can't do

  • It can't sign you up. You apply to Walmart Marketplace on Walmart's website first.
  • You can't change user roles in the app; do that in Seller Center.
  • It doesn't manage Marketplace businesses in Canada, Mexico or Chile.
  • It isn't built for product research. It shows your own items and orders, not the profit on a product you're thinking about buying, or how much stock other sellers hold.

Tips for getting the most out of it

  • Act on at-risk order alerts fast. Late shipments and missing tracking hurt the performance metrics Walmart uses to judge sellers and pick the Buy Box winner.
  • Answer messages from your phone. Quick replies keep small problems from turning into returns or cancellations.
  • Watch out-of-stock alerts. When you run out, you drop out of the Buy Box and other sellers take your sales.
  • Do big jobs on a computer. Bulk item uploads, reports and settings are easier in Seller Center.

Questions

Does Walmart have a seller app?

Yes. The Walmart Seller app is Walmart's official app for U.S. Marketplace sellers, on iPhone and Android. It handles orders, items, pricing, inventory, WFS shipments, customer messages and sales tracking.

Is the Walmart Seller app free?

Yes. The app is free to download. You need an approved U.S. Walmart Marketplace seller account to sign in.

Can I sign up for Walmart Marketplace in the Walmart Seller app?

No. The app is for existing sellers. You apply on Walmart's Marketplace website, and once you're approved and have logged in to Seller Center, you can sign in to the app with the same details.

Can I list products from the Walmart Seller app?

Yes. Walmart says you can add items to your catalog, view item details and update pricing and inventory in the app. Larger catalog work is still easier in Seller Center on a computer.

Does the Walmart Seller app show what products to sell?

No. It manages your own store. To check whether a product is worth selling, you need a research tool that shows fees, profit, sales estimates and competing sellers, such as Margenos.

Is Margenos the Walmart Seller app?

No. Margenos is an independent product research tool from Lemont Holdings LLC and is not affiliated with Walmart. Many sellers use both: Margenos to pick products and the Walmart Seller app to manage orders.

Does the Walmart Seller app work in Canada or Mexico?

Walmart's help guide says the app doesn't support managing Marketplace businesses in Canada, Mexico or Chile.
