Walmart seller account suspended? Causes, standards and how to appeal

If your Walmart seller account is suspended, your listings stop being shown and sold, your Seller Center access is limited and Walmart holds your payments. To get reinstated, you find the cause on the Performance page, write a plan of action, and submit it through Help in Seller Center. Most suspensions trace back to missed seller performance standards (late shipments, bad tracking, cancellations) or policy problems such as IP complaints and restricted products. Walmart account suspension: the four levels of action

Walmart's suppression, pause, suspension and termination policy (updated Oct 2, 2026) describes four steps, from mild to permanent:

ActionWhat happens
SuppressionSeller-fulfilled listings can't be bought and may show as out of stock. Walmart-fulfilled (WFS) listings stay live.
PauseBoth seller-fulfilled and WFS listings can't be bought and may show as out of stock.
SuspensionListings can't be bought or found on Walmart.com, Seller Center access is restricted and payments are held.
TerminationAll listings are removed, selling privileges and Seller Center access are permanently revoked, and payments are held.

Walmart notifies you in Seller Center and by email, and you can't unsubscribe from those notices. The policy also says an account that stays suspended past "a reasonable threshold" without a successful appeal may be terminated, so don't sit on it.

"Walmart seller account deactivated" usually means one of two things. If a banner says seller fulfillment is deactivated, that's a suppression, and you appeal it from the Performance page with Start appeal. If the whole account is shut off, it's a suspension or termination.

Walmart seller performance standards

These are the targets on Walmart's Seller performance standards page (updated Jul 28, 2026). Walmart evaluates them over the last 30 or 60 days and says missing them "may result in suppression, suspension or termination."

MetricWalmart's standardWhat it measures
On-Time Delivery Rate90% or higherOrders delivered by the Expected Delivery Date
Valid Tracking Rate99% or higherOrders with valid tracking
Late Shipment Rate5% or lowerOrders shipped after the Expected Ship Date
Cancellation Rate2% or lowerOrders you cancel after receiving them
Seller Response Rate95% or higherCustomer messages answered within 48 hours
Negative Feedback Rate2% or lowerOrders in the past 60 days rated one or two stars
Return Rate6% or lower (9% for Resold)Delivered orders returned in the past 60 days
Item Not Received Rate2% or lowerOrders expected in the past 60 days that weren't delivered

If you use Walmart Fulfillment Services, Walmart covers most of these metrics for those orders, except Negative Feedback Rate. That's one reason sellers who struggle with shipping move inventory to WFS; compare the costs on WFS fees first.

Common reasons for a Walmart seller account suspension

  • Shipping and tracking misses. Walmart's performance notifications warn you about orders at risk of auto-cancellation, orders unshipped by the Expected Ship Date and missing carrier scans. Under the shipping and fulfillment policy, an order with no tracking by the Expected Ship Date plus four calendar days is auto-canceled.
  • Prohibited fulfillment. The same policy bans buying from another retailer and having that retailer ship straight to your customer, shipping in a competitor's box, and marking orders as shipped before they ship. Walmart says a single violation can lead to suppressed listings, selling limits and withheld funds.
  • IP complaints and counterfeits. Walmart's IP claims page says repeat infringers can have listings removed and their relationship with Walmart suspended or ended. Counterfeit items can lead to termination under the IP policy.
  • Restricted or prohibited products. Listing gated items without approval gets them unpublished, and repeat problems can escalate. See Walmart restricted brands.
  • Pricing problems. Walmart's pricing rules unpublish offers priced far above recent prices, and shipping costs above the allowed limit can be rejected and put the account at risk.

How to reinstate a Walmart seller account

Walmart's Appeal an account suspension guide lays out the process:

  1. Review your performance. Open the Performance page in Seller Center and find which standard or policy you missed, and on which orders.
  2. Write a plan of action. Describe the violation, the steps you'll take to fix it and, if you broke the Marketplace Retailer Agreement, how you'll prevent it from happening again.
  3. Gather supporting documents. If Walmart asks, include current warehouse photos, supplier or distributor invoices less than two months old, or intellectual property documents. Missing documents can get the appeal denied.
  4. Submit through Help in Seller Center. For a suppression, use Start appeal on the Performance page instead.

Walmart handles appeals in the order it receives them and contacts you with a decision. It doesn't publish a response time, and an appeal doesn't guarantee reinstatement. Terminations can't be appealed.

How to write a Walmart plan of action

Walmart only lists what the plan must contain. These habits make it easier to read and harder to reject:

  • Name the root cause. "Our carrier pickups were missed during a staffing gap" beats "we had some issues."
  • List what you've already fixed. Orders shipped, refunds sent, listings removed, tracking corrected.
  • List what changes going forward. Lag time settings, a second carrier, daily order checks, supplier changes, a rule against unauthorized brands.
  • Attach proof. Invoices that match your business name, less than two months old, from real suppliers. Retail receipts are weaker than distributor invoices for IP or authenticity cases.
  • Keep it short and factual. Don't blame Walmart or the customer, and don't argue the policy.

Prevention: check products before you buy

Many suspensions start at the sourcing stage: a brand that files IP claims, a gated category, or a product so slow that you're tempted to cut corners on shipping. Before you buy, look at who already sells the item. The Margenos seller view lists every seller on a listing with stock counts and shows whether Walmart sells it too. A listing where only the brand sells can be a sign the brand controls its resellers. Questions

Why was my Walmart seller account suspended?

The most common causes are missing the seller performance standards (on-time delivery, valid tracking, cancellations and others), IP or counterfeit complaints, listing restricted products, and breaking the shipping policy, for example drop-shipping from another retailer. Check the Performance page and your Seller Center notifications for the exact reason.

How do I reinstate my Walmart seller account?

Review the Performance page, write a plan of action describing the violation and the steps you'll take to fix and prevent it, attach any documents Walmart requests, and submit it through Help in Seller Center. Walmart reviews appeals in the order received.

What are Walmart's seller performance standards?

On-time delivery 90% or higher, valid tracking 99% or higher, late shipments 5% or lower, cancellations 2% or lower, response rate 95% or higher within 48 hours, negative feedback 2% or lower, returns 6% or lower (9% for Resold) and item not received 2% or lower.

How long does a Walmart suspension appeal take?

Walmart doesn't publish a timeframe. It says appeals are handled in the order received and that it will contact you with the decision.

Can a terminated Walmart seller account be reinstated?

Walmart's appeal guide says it isn't required to give a reason for termination and that appeals won't be granted. Suspensions and suppressions can be appealed.

Do I get paid while my Walmart account is suspended?

Walmart's policy says payments are held during suspension and termination.

Can Margenos help with a Walmart suspension?

No. Margenos doesn't file appeals or manage your account. It helps before you buy, by showing fees, profit, sales estimates and every competing seller on a listing, so you can skip risky or thin-margin products.

Do I need a lawyer for a Walmart seller suspension?

Usually not. Walmart's appeal guide has you submit a written plan of action through Help in Seller Center, with supporting documents such as recent supplier invoices if requested, and it doesn't require legal representation. A lawyer can make sense for intellectual property disputes, large held balances or contract issues, but no lawyer or consultant can guarantee reinstatement.
