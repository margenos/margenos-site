Selling on Walmart vs Amazon

Fees, approval, competition and traffic side by side, and how to decide which marketplace fits your business.

Updated September 30, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Amazon is the biggest online marketplace in the U.S. and Walmart.com is the second. Many resellers sell on both. The right question isn't which one is better, but which one fits your products, budget and experience right now.

Why sellers add Walmart

  • Less competition. Fewer sellers on each listing means a better shot at the Buy Box.
  • No monthly subscription. You only pay when you sell.
  • Diversification. If something goes wrong with one marketplace account, you still have sales on the other.
  • Same inventory, two channels. Many products you already sell on Amazon can be matched to existing Walmart listings.

Why some sellers stay on Amazon

  • More traffic. More shoppers usually means more total sales per product.
  • Easier to start. Amazon doesn't require an application for most sellers.
  • More tools and data. Amazon's public sales rank makes research easier, while Walmart sellers have to estimate sales.

Which should you choose?

  • Brand new with no sales history? Amazon is easier to start on. Walmart looks for an e-commerce track record.
  • Already selling on Amazon? Apply to Walmart. Your sales history helps approval, and the lower competition can add real profit. Start with our guide on how to sell on Walmart Marketplace.
  • Selling low-competition, everyday items? Walmart can be the better fit, especially with WFS.

Questions

Is it better to sell on Walmart or Amazon?

It depends. Amazon has more traffic and is easier to join. Walmart has fewer sellers per listing, no monthly fee and similar referral fees, but you must apply and be approved. Many resellers sell on both.

Are Walmart seller fees lower than Amazon's?

Walmart has no monthly subscription, while Amazon's Professional plan is $39.99 a month. Referral fees are similar on both, mostly 15% depending on the category.

Is it harder to get approved on Walmart than Amazon?

Yes. Walmart Marketplace requires an application, a registered U.S. business with an EIN, and proof of e-commerce experience. Amazon lets most sellers sign up without approval.

Can I sell the same products on Walmart and Amazon?

Usually yes. Many products can be matched to existing listings on both marketplaces, as long as you're allowed to sell the brand and category on each.

Does Walmart have a sales rank like Amazon?

No. Walmart does not publish a sales rank, so sellers rely on sales estimates, reviews, sales badges and competitor stock changes to judge demand.
