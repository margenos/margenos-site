Walmart WFS fees 2026, explained

What Walmart Fulfillment Services really charges per unit: the fulfillment fee chart by weight, the surcharges most resellers miss, monthly storage, and a real example.

Updated September 27, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) charges two main fees: a fulfillment fee per unit sold, set by shipping weight, and a monthly storage fee per cubic foot. There's no signup or monthly subscription fee. Your Walmart referral fee is charged on top of these on every sale.

Step 1: Find your item's shipping weight

WFS doesn't use the weight on the box. It uses a shipping weight, worked out like this:

  • Under 1 lb: start with the unit weight.
  • 1 lb to 150 lb: use whichever is greater, the unit weight or the dimensional weight.
  • Dimensional weight = length × width × height (in inches, with packaging) ÷ 139.
  • Then add 0.25 lb for packaging materials and round up to the next pound.

So a 1.2 lb item in an 8 × 6 × 4 inch box has a dimensional weight of 192 ÷ 139 = 1.38 lb. That's more than 1.2 lb, so Walmart uses 1.38 lb, adds 0.25 lb (1.63 lb) and rounds up to a 2 lb shipping weight. The 0.25 lb matters: a 1.8 lb item becomes 2.05 lb and bills at 3 lb.

Step 2: WFS fulfillment fee by shipping weight

These are Walmart's standard fulfillment fees per unit, effective since April 2025:

Standard items are up to 150 lb, with the longest side up to 108 inches and longest side + girth up to 165 inches. Anything bigger ships as big & bulky by freight: $155 + $0.80 per lb over 90 lb, up to 500 lb.

The under-$10 surcharge is the one resellers miss most. A $9.97 item pays $1 more per sale than a $10.00 item, so cheap items need a bigger spread to work.

Step 4: Monthly WFS storage fees

Storage is charged per cubic foot per month. Cubic feet = length × width × height (inches) ÷ 1,728.

When / how longStorage fee per cubic foot per month
January–September$0.75
October–December, first 30 days$0.75
October–December, after 30 days$0.75 + $1.50 ($2.25 total)
Stored 366–450 days (from June 30, 2026)$2.25
Stored over 450 days (from June 30, 2026)$7.50

Our 8 × 6 × 4 inch example is 0.11 cubic feet, so storage is about $0.08 a month most of the year. Storage only really hurts on big, slow items and on stock sitting through October–December.

Other WFS fees to know

  • Prep chargebacks: $0.65 per unit for a missing or wrong label, $0.80 for a missing or wrong poly bag.
  • Returns: if a customer returns for a reason that isn't Walmart's fault (wrong size, no longer wanted), you pay a return processing fee, starting at $4.70 for items 1 lb or less.
  • Removal and disposal: disposal starts at $0.35 per unit; removal adds $0.40 per lb of shipping.

Questions

How much does WFS charge per item?

WFS fulfillment fees start at $3.45 per unit for items with a shipping weight of 1 lb or less, $4.95 for 2 lb and $5.45 for 3 lb. From 4 to 20 lb it's $5.75 plus $0.40 for each pound over 4. Surcharges apply for items under $10, apparel, hazmat and oversize items.

How is WFS shipping weight calculated?

Walmart starts with the unit weight, or for items from 1 to 150 lb the greater of the unit weight or the dimensional weight (length x width x height in inches divided by 139). It then adds 0.25 lb for packaging and rounds up to the next pound.

How much are WFS storage fees?

WFS storage is $0.75 per cubic foot per month from January to September. From October to December, items stored more than 30 days pay an extra $1.50 per cubic foot. From June 30, 2026, items stored 366 to 450 days pay $2.25 and items over 450 days pay $7.50 per cubic foot per month.

Is there a monthly fee to use WFS?

No. Walmart says WFS has no signup or monthly subscription fees. You pay fulfillment fees per unit sold and storage fees for the space your inventory uses, plus your normal Walmart referral fee on each sale.

Does WFS charge extra for cheap items?

Yes. Items with a retail price under $10 pay an extra $1 per unit on top of the base fulfillment fee.
