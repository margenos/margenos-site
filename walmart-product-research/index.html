Questions

What is the best way to do Walmart product research?

Check four things for every item: the Walmart selling price, the fees Walmart takes, how many units sell, and how many sellers you would compete with. Margenos shows all four for any barcode, name or listing.

Is Margenos a Walmart product finder?

Yes. You can scan items in a store, search by name, or browse Walmart listings in Chrome, and Margenos tells you which ones are profitable at your cost.

Does it work for wholesale sourcing?

Yes. Type the UPC and your wholesale cost to see profit and ROI per unit before you place an order.
