How the Walmart Seller Center login works

Seller Center is where approved sellers list items, ship orders, track payments and contact Walmart. Your login is created when you sign up to sell, and you finish onboarding inside Seller Center before you can publish items, according to Walmart's onboarding guide. If you haven't applied yet, start with our Walmart seller requirements guide.

  1. Go to seller.walmart.com. Use a bookmark you created yourself or type the address. Don't sign in from links in unexpected emails or texts.
  2. Enter your email and password for your Seller Center user (not your Walmart.com shopping account).
  3. Enter your verification code from your authenticator app, email or text message. Then you land on your Seller Center dashboard.

Walmart seller center sign in: 2-step verification

Walmart requires 2-step verification for Seller Center. Its 2-step verification guide says you set it up during onboarding, and each user on the account sets up their own method. There are three choices:

MethodHow it works
Authenticator appAn app such as Google or Microsoft Authenticator shows a six-digit code that changes every 30 seconds.
EmailA one-time code is sent to the email on the account and is valid for 10 minutes.
Text messageA one-time code is sent to the phone number on the account and is valid for 10 minutes.

An authenticator app is the most reliable choice if you travel or share an inbox, because it doesn't depend on email delivery or cell signal.

Common Walmart seller login problems and fixes

Forgot your password

On the login page, select Forgot your Password?, enter the email on your account and open the reset email. Walmart's password reset guide says the reset link is valid for seven days. If the email doesn't arrive, check spam and make sure you used the email address tied to your Seller Center user.

The verification code doesn't work

  • Email or text codes expire after 10 minutes. Request a new one instead of reusing an old code.
  • Authenticator codes change every 30 seconds. If they keep failing, check that your phone's date and time are set automatically.
  • New phone? An authenticator app on an old phone won't move over by itself. If you can't get a code at all, contact Seller Support.

Walmart also has a guide to troubleshooting one-time verification code errors.

The page won't load or keeps looping

Seller guides such as 8th & Walton suggest typing the URL instead of using an old bookmark, not relying on saved browser passwords, and clearing your cache and cookies. Trying a private window or another browser is a quick way to rule out an extension or stale cookie.

You're signing in to the wrong account

Your Walmart.com shopping account and your Seller Center user are separate. If you were added by someone else's business, use the email they invited, and ask that account's admin if your access is missing.

Your account is suspended

A suspension can limit what you can do in Seller Center. See Walmart account suspension for what each status means and how appeals work.

How to add users to Walmart Seller Center

Don't share one login across your team. Walmart's add a new user guide gives these steps:

  1. Open People and permissions in Seller Center.
  2. Select Add User in the top-right corner.
  3. Enter the person's name, email and role, then select Add. Walmart emails them an invitation to create their own login and password.

Only users with admin access can add users. The roles are:

  • Admin: full access, including API credentials, requests for special approval categories, adding users and accepting binding agreements for the business.
  • Read and Write: day-to-day work such as setting up items and viewing reports.
  • Read Only: can view data and download reports but can't change anything.

Walmart notes that you're responsible for what your users do, so keep Admin to people who can legally commit your company.

Where to get help with your Walmart seller login

Seller help runs through Walmart, not through third-party sites. Walmart's guides point to the Seller Support page on seller.walmart.com, and Marketplace Learn has the written guides linked above. For more on support options and what's inside Seller Center, see our Walmart Seller Center guide and Walmart seller support.

Be careful with anyone who offers to "fix" your login for a fee or asks for your password and codes. Walmart staff and real tools don't need your verification codes.

Questions

What is the Walmart seller login URL?

It's seller.walmart.com, the Walmart Seller Center. Type it yourself or use your own bookmark rather than links in emails.

Is Walmart Seller Central the same as Seller Center?

People often say "Seller Central" because that's Amazon's name. Walmart's dashboard is called Seller Center, and its login is seller.walmart.com.

Does Walmart Seller Center require 2-step verification?

Yes. Each user sets up an authenticator app, email or text message. Email and text codes are valid for 10 minutes; authenticator codes change every 30 seconds.

How do I reset my Walmart seller password?

Select "Forgot your Password?" on the Seller Center login page and enter your account email. Walmart says the reset link is valid for seven days.

Can I use my Walmart.com shopping account to log in to Seller Center?

No. Seller Center uses its own user logins tied to your seller account. Your shopping account is separate.

How do I give my VA or employee access to Seller Center?

An admin opens People and permissions, selects Add User, and picks Admin, Read and Write, or Read Only. The person gets an email invite to set their own password, so you never share yours.

Does Margenos need my Walmart seller login?

No. Margenos is not affiliated with Walmart and never asks for your Seller Center login or codes. It reads public Walmart.com product pages, so it works even if you don't have a seller account yet.

Is Walmart Seller Center down right now, and how can I check?

Walmart doesn't publish a public status page for the Seller Center website, so first rule out your side: type seller.walmart.com directly, try another browser or a private window, and clear cache and cookies. API users can check Walmart's API Platform Status service, which reports outages and degradation. If the site still won't work, use the Contact support link on the Seller Center login page, which works without signing in.

What are the Walmart seller password requirements?

Walmart doesn't publish exact password rules for Seller Center. Its password reset guide only notes that an "insecure" password can be rejected and that the reset link is valid for seven days, so use a long, unique password you don't use anywhere else. 2-step verification is also required for every Seller Center user.

Is the Walmart vendor or Supplier One login the same as the Seller Center login?

No. Seller Center (seller.walmart.com) is for Marketplace sellers who sell their own inventory on Walmart.com, while Walmart's supplier site says existing suppliers (vendors who sell to Walmart wholesale) use Supplier One, and new suppliers register through Retail Link. The accounts and logins are separate. See Walmart 1P vs 3P for how the two roles differ.
