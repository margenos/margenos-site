Walmart WFS fees calculator and Walmart ROI: how seller fees work

Every sale on Walmart Marketplace pays a referral fee: a percentage of the sale price set by category. Most categories are 15%, some are price-tiered (apparel is 5% under $15, grocery and beauty are 8% under $15 or $10), and a few, like consumer electronics, are 8%.

If Walmart fulfills the order through Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS), you also pay a fulfillment fee based on shipping weight, plus monthly storage. Items under $10 carry an extra $1.00, and apparel an extra $0.50.

Questions

How much does Walmart take per sale?

Walmart charges a referral fee on each sale, set by category. Most categories are 15% of the sale price; some are lower or price-tiered, such as apparel at 5% under $15 and consumer electronics at 8%. There is no monthly seller subscription fee.

How are WFS fees calculated?

WFS fulfillment fees are based on the item's shipping weight, which is the unit weight plus packaging, rounded up to the next pound. Items under $10 add $1.00 and apparel adds $0.50. Storage is charged monthly by cubic foot and rises in October through December.

Is this Walmart profit calculator free?

Yes. The calculator on this page is free to use. The Margenos app and Chrome extension fill in the category, weight, price and competing sellers for you from the listing.

What is a good ROI for Walmart arbitrage?

Many resellers aim for at least 30% ROI after all fees, but the right target depends on how fast the product sells and how much competition is on the listing. Margenos shows sales estimates and the competing sellers next to your ROI for that reason.
