Walmart liquidation pallets: where to buy and what to check

How Walmart's official liquidation auctions work, what's really in a returns pallet, and how to price a manifest before you bid.

Updated November 10, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Walmart liquidation pallets are sold officially through Walmart Liquidation Auctions, which Walmart's own liquidation site describes as "an official liquidation channel for Walmart.com and Walmart Stores, Inc." and part of the B-Stock Sourcing Network. Lots range "from single pallets up to truckloads," sold by auction to approved business buyers, mostly Walmart.com customer returns. Walmart doesn't run a liquidation store you can walk into. Other liquidators and local bin stores also sell pallets they describe as Walmart, but those aren't Walmart's official channel.

Does Walmart sell liquidation pallets? Is there an official Walmart liquidation store?

Yes, through the auction site, not a store. Walmart Liquidation Auctions sells excess inventory, store returns and refurbished bulk lots, and its about page says "almost anything found at a Walmart may be available in bulk," including TVs, electronics, furniture, bikes, appliances and apparel. Sales are "exclusive to B-Stock Sourcing Network approved buyers."

"Walmart liquidation store" searches usually turn up independent bin stores and pallet resellers. Some buy from liquidation auctions and resell; they aren't Walmart stores. If one claims an official Walmart connection, ask what lot it came from and check their site.

How Walmart Liquidation Auctions on B-Stock work

From the Walmart Liquidation Auctions FAQ:

TopicWhat the FAQ says
Who can buyOnly buyers in the United States. A state-issued reseller's certificate is required for approval (except in Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon); an EIN can't replace it. Review takes up to one business day.
Cost to join"There is no fee for applying or for being an approved buyer."
What's sold"Walmart.com customer returns, unless stated otherwise." Sold as-is; images may be stock photos; no inspection before bidding.
BiddingAuction format with proxy bids. Bids are binding and can't be cancelled once submitted.
PaymentWire transfer only, due within 2 business days of auction close. No credit cards, personal checks or Western Union.
ShippingSome locations are buyer-arranged (you can pick up with a dock-height truck); others are seller-arranged, with shipping shown on the auction page and paid to B-Stock.
Damage claimsThe FAQ says it "does not accept order claims for merchandise that arrives damaged." Other disputes go through "Report an Issue."

Missing a payment can trigger reinstatement fees, so only bid on what you can pay for within two business days.

Is Walmart Liquidation Auctions legit?

Yes. The site runs on a walmart.com subdomain (liquidations.walmart.com) and describes itself as Walmart's official channel. Legit doesn't mean low-risk: you're buying unseen customer returns, as-is, with binding bids and wire-only payment. That's normal for liquidation, and it's why per-item research matters.

What's in a Walmart returns or overstock pallet?

  • Customer returns can be new and unopened, opened, used, missing parts or broken. The FAQ warns that images may be stock photos.
  • Overstock and excess inventory is more likely to be new, when a lot says so.
  • Manifested vs unmanifested. A manifest lists the items (often with UPCs and retail prices). Unmanifested lots don't, so you're guessing. The Walmart FAQ doesn't describe its manifests; read each listing.
  • Condition grades are set per listing. Read the listing's condition notes, not just the title.

Walmart's U.S. FAQ doesn't publish typical lot prices. Pricing is set by bidding, and the about page only says lots "typically sell at a fraction of retail MSRP." Retail value isn't resale value.

Are Walmart pallets profitable?

Sometimes. Profit depends on what you can actually resell, at what price, after shipping, fees, damaged units and your time testing and listing. A pallet with a big total retail value can still lose money if most items are broken, slow sellers, or listed by many other sellers. Nobody can tell you an average return on a pallet honestly, so we won't.

How to check per-item profit on a manifest before you bid

  1. Download the manifest and sort by retail value. A handful of items usually make or break a lot.
  2. Look up the top items by UPC. Use a Walmart UPC lookup to find the Walmart.com listing for each.
  3. Check the real resale price and fees. Margenos shows the current price, referral and WFS fees, profit, ROI and max buy cost on each listing, with quantity and prep cost in the math.
  4. Check demand and competition. Look at the sales estimate (or "not enough data"), every competing seller with stock, and who wins the Buy Box. If Walmart itself sells it, you'll compete on price.
  5. Discount for condition. Assume some units are unsellable as New. Add up only what you're confident in, subtract shipping, and set a maximum bid.

The Walmart profit calculator helps with the math, and Walmart product research explains how to read sales estimates and competition.

Can you resell liquidation items on Walmart Marketplace, eBay or Amazon?

On Walmart Marketplace, condition is the hurdle. Walmart's item condition guide defines New as "brand new, unused, unopened, undamaged and in its original packaging," and says the other condition types are tied to invite-only programs. Walmart's Resold program for Pre-Owned and Restored items "is by invitation only and not available to all Marketplace sellers." So opened returns usually can't go on Walmart as New. eBay and Amazon have their own condition rules for used and open-box items; check each marketplace's policies before you list.

Other places to buy Walmart pallets, and how to avoid scams

Online liquidators and local pallet sellers also offer lots they label as Walmart returns. We haven't verified those sources, so check their site for where the stock comes from, whether lots are manifested, and their return terms. Common warning signs:

  • "Walmart mystery pallets" with no manifest at a fixed retail-style price.
  • Social media sellers who want payment by gift card, app transfer or crypto before you see anything.
  • Claims of guaranteed profit or "brand-new Walmart overstock" with no lot details.

Can you buy Walmart liquidation in Canada?

The U.S. site is open only to U.S. buyers. B-Stock runs a separate Walmart Canada Liquidation Auctions marketplace that says "only Canadian buyers will be allowed to bid," requires a GST/HST/QST business number from the Canada Revenue Agency, takes payment in Canadian dollars, and sells customer returns as truckloads or less-than-truckloads shipping from Mississauga, ON. Margenos checks Walmart.com (U.S.) listings only.

Questions

Where can I buy Walmart liquidation pallets?

Officially, at Walmart Liquidation Auctions (liquidations.walmart.com), part of the B-Stock Sourcing Network. Lots range from single pallets to truckloads and are sold by auction to approved buyers.

Does Walmart have a liquidation store?

Not a physical one. Walmart's official liquidation channel is the online auction site. Local liquidation and bin stores are independent businesses, even if they sell items that came from Walmart.

Is Walmart Liquidation Auctions legit?

Yes. It runs on liquidations.walmart.com and describes itself as an official liquidation channel for Walmart.com and Walmart stores. Items are sold as-is, bids are binding, and payment is by wire only.

What do I need to buy from Walmart Liquidation Auctions?

You must be in the United States, and most U.S. buyers need a state-issued reseller's certificate; an EIN alone isn't enough. There's no fee to apply or to be an approved buyer.

What's in a Walmart return pallet?

Usually Walmart.com customer returns, unless the listing says otherwise. Condition varies from new to broken, images may be stock photos, and you can't inspect before bidding.

Are Walmart liquidation pallets profitable?

They can be, but there's no reliable average. Price the top items on the manifest one by one at their real resale price after fees, discount for damaged units, and set a maximum bid.

Can I sell Walmart returns on Walmart Marketplace?

Only as New if they're brand new, unused, unopened, undamaged and in original packaging. Pre-Owned and Restored listings go through Walmart's Resold program, which is invitation only.
