Walmart seller fees 2026: referral fees by category

How much Walmart takes from each sale, category by category, with the tier rules that trip up resellers and real examples.

Updated September 27, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Selling on Walmart Marketplace has no monthly subscription fee and no setup fee. Walmart makes its money from a referral fee, a percentage of each sale that depends on the item's category. Most categories are 15%; electronics, cameras and a few others are lower. If you use Walmart Fulfillment Services, WFS fees are charged on top.

The fee is taken from the total sales price, which includes the item price plus shipping, handling, gift wrap and other charges.

Two ways the tiers work

Read the tiers carefully, because Walmart uses two different styles:

  • Whole-price tiers (Apparel, Baby, Beauty, Grocery, Outdoor Power Tools): the price decides one rate for the whole sale. An $18 shirt pays 10% on all $18, which is $1.80.
  • "Portion" tiers (Compact Appliances, Electronics Accessories, Furniture, Jewelry, Watches): each part of the price has its own rate. A $150 phone case bundle pays 15% on the first $100 ($15.00) plus 8% on the other $50 ($4.00), which is $19.00.

What else can cost money on Walmart?

  • WFS fulfillment and storage if Walmart ships for you. See the full WFS fee chart.
  • Your own shipping if you fulfill orders yourself.
  • Advertising, only if you choose to run Walmart Sponsored Search ads.
  • Returns on WFS orders that aren't Walmart's fault.

Questions

How much does Walmart take per sale?

Walmart charges a referral fee on each sale, based on the item's category. Most categories are 15% of the total sales price. Some are lower, such as 8% for consumer electronics, 6% for personal computers and 12% for automotive.

Does Walmart Marketplace have a monthly fee?

No. Walmart Marketplace has no setup fee or monthly subscription fee. You pay a referral fee only when an item sells, plus WFS fees if you use Walmart Fulfillment Services.

Is the Walmart referral fee charged on shipping?

Yes. Walmart calculates the referral fee on the total sales price, which includes the item price plus shipping and handling, gift wrap and other charges.

What is the Walmart referral fee for toys?

Toys & Games has a 15% referral fee on Walmart Marketplace.

Why is the fee lower on cheap beauty, baby and grocery items?

Walmart charges 8% on Beauty, Health & Personal Care and Baby Products items with a total sales price of $10 or less, and on Grocery items of $15 or less. Above those prices, the rate is 15%.
