A BrickSeek alternative built for Walmart resellers

BrickSeek tells you where a deal is. It doesn't tell you if the item will sell on Walmart Marketplace. Here's how the two jobs differ.

Updated November 28, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Found a clearance deal? Scan it with Margenos to see Walmart fees, profit, ROI, sales and every seller before you buy the whole shelf.

BrickSeek and Margenos do two different jobs. BrickSeek is a local inventory and price checker: you look up an item at stores like Walmart, Target and Lowe's to find clearance deals near you. Margenos answers the next question a reseller has: if I buy this, will it make money on Walmart Marketplace? It shows Walmart referral and WFS fees, profit, ROI, a sales estimate, every competing seller with stock and who wins the Buy Box.

Many resellers use both: BrickSeek (or the Walmart app) to find the deal, and a profit tool to decide whether to buy it. If you only want one tool for reselling on Walmart, the profit check is the one that stops bad buys.

Why local store stock isn't enough

A $5 clearance item isn't a deal if it sells for $12 on Walmart.com and fees take $6. And it isn't a deal if 30 other sellers have stock on the same listing. Before you buy, you need three numbers BrickSeek doesn't give you:

  1. Profit after Walmart's fees. Walmart takes a referral fee by category, and WFS charges by weight. See Walmart seller fees and WFS fees.
  2. How fast it sells. Walmart has no public sales rank, so you need an estimate. See the Walmart sales estimator.
  3. Competition. How many sellers have stock and who holds the Buy Box. See how the Walmart Buy Box works.

A sourcing flow that uses both

  1. Find clearance or markdowns: BrickSeek, the Walmart app, or a store walk. Our guides on hidden clearance and reselling clearance cover where to look.
  2. Scan the barcode with Margenos in the aisle. Type the shelf price as your cost.
  3. Check profit, ROI, the sales estimate and the seller count. Buy only what clears your target.
  4. Track the item and get an alert if the price drops or sellers sell out.

Questions

What is the best BrickSeek alternative for Walmart resellers?

If you resell on Walmart Marketplace, pair a deal finder with a profit tool. Margenos scans a barcode and shows Walmart fees, profit, ROI, a sales estimate, every competing seller and the Buy Box, which BrickSeek doesn't show.

Does BrickSeek still work for Walmart?

BrickSeek still has a Walmart inventory checker, but some users report Walmart stock showing as unknown for many items. Treat its results as a lead, and confirm in the Walmart app or in store.

Is BrickSeek free?

BrickSeek has a free tier with limited results and a paid membership called BrickSeek One. Check brickseek.com for current plans and prices.

Is there an app like BrickSeek?

BrickSeek has its own app. The Walmart app also shows store availability. For reselling, Margenos is an Android scanner app that shows whether an item will make money on Walmart Marketplace.

Can Margenos check Walmart store inventory?

No. Margenos shows Walmart.com Marketplace sellers and their stock, not stock at your local store. Use the Walmart app or a store checker for that, then scan with Margenos to check profit.
