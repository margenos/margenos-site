Walmart wholesale: what it means for resellers

Walmart doesn't run a wholesale program for resellers, but there are four real things people mean when they search for one.

Walmart doesn't sell wholesale to the public. There's no Walmart wholesale account that gets you case pricing on Walmart.com items. When people search for Walmart wholesale, they usually mean one of four things: Sam's Club, Walmart's own warehouse club; Walmart liquidation pallets; selling wholesale to Walmart as a supplier; or buying wholesale from brands and distributors to resell on Walmart Marketplace. Here's what each one is and when it makes sense.

Does Walmart do wholesale?

Not in the way a distributor does. Walmart is a retailer. These are the closest options:

OptionWhat it isWho it fits
Sam's ClubA membership-only warehouse club owned by Walmart, selling bulk packsSmall businesses buying supplies; some resellers
Walmart BusinessWalmart's buying site for businesses and nonprofitsCompanies buying supplies for their own use
Walmart Liquidation AuctionsReturns, overstock and refurbished lots sold by auction through B-StockApproved resellers who can test, sort and grade goods

Can you buy wholesale from Walmart?

Not at true wholesale prices. Sam's Club bulk packs and Walmart Business accounts are built for businesses buying things they use. Bulk retail pricing is usually too close to the Walmart.com selling price to leave room for fees and profit.

Buying Walmart's retail or clearance stock to resell is arbitrage, not wholesale. It can work on clearance markdowns (see how to resell Walmart clearance), but you're buying one item at a time at retail-level prices.

Walmart wholesale pallets and liquidation

Walmart wholesale pallets almost always means liquidation. Walmart sells excess inventory, store returns and refurbished goods through Walmart Liquidation Auctions, part of the B-Stock Sourcing Network. Lots range from single pallets to truckloads and are usually well below retail. Buying is limited to approved B-Stock buyers.

Before you bid, know what you're getting:

  • Condition varies. Returns can be used, damaged or missing parts. Budget for testing, cleaning and items you can't sell.
  • Freight is extra. Pallet shipping can eat a big share of a cheap lot.
  • Walmart Marketplace condition rules apply. Used or open-box goods can't be listed as new. Check what conditions your account and category allow before you plan to sell there.
  • Mixed lots need product research. Check what each item actually sells for before you value the pallet.

Be careful with resellers advertising "Walmart pallets" outside the official auction site. Ask where the goods came from and what condition they're in.

How to become a Walmart supplier vs a Marketplace seller

If you make or own a brand, you can apply to sell wholesale to Walmart as a supplier. Walmart buys your product and sells it in stores or online. U.S. businesses apply through Walmart's supplier application, starting with its supplier checklist. Registering doesn't guarantee an agreement, and the process is slower and stricter than Marketplace. Suppliers manage the relationship in Walmart's supplier tools, such as Retail Link.

A Walmart Marketplace seller keeps ownership of the inventory and sells straight to Walmart.com shoppers, setting their own price. You apply at marketplace.walmart.com. Walmart reviews each application, and you pay referral fees when you sell, plus WFS fees if Walmart ships for you. For resellers, this is the path that matters. See how to sell on Walmart.

Finding wholesale suppliers for Walmart Marketplace

Real wholesale for a Walmart reseller means buying cases from a brand or an authorized distributor and selling them on Walmart Marketplace. It gives you repeatable stock instead of one-off finds.

  1. Pick products first. Use Walmart product research to find items that sell steadily and don't have Walmart on the listing.
  2. Find who distributes the brand. Check the brand's website for a wholesale or retailer page, or contact the brand directly.
  3. Apply as a business. Most suppliers want an EIN, a resale certificate and sometimes a website or store link.
  4. Ask for invoices. Brand-name items on Walmart can need proof of authenticity. Keep the paperwork.
  5. Start with one case. Test sell-through before you place larger orders.

Steer clear of "Walmart wholesale suppliers" lists that promise Walmart-approved sources. Walmart doesn't certify wholesale suppliers for resellers.

Checking wholesale profit before you order

Wholesale margins are thinner than clearance arbitrage, so the math has to be exact. For each item, you need the Walmart.com sell price, the referral fee for the category, WFS fulfillment and storage if you use it, inbound shipping and prep, and your case cost divided by units.

Questions

Does Walmart sell wholesale?

No. Walmart is a retailer and doesn't sell wholesale to resellers. Its warehouse club, Sam's Club, sells bulk packs to members, and Walmart Business serves companies buying supplies, but neither offers true wholesale pricing for resale.

Is there a Walmart wholesale account?

Not for resellers. Walmart Business offers business accounts for buying supplies, and Sam's Club has memberships. If you want to sell on Walmart, you need a Walmart Marketplace seller account instead.

How do I buy Walmart wholesale pallets?

Walmart sells returns, overstock and refurbished goods through Walmart Liquidation Auctions, which is part of the B-Stock Sourcing Network. You need to be an approved B-Stock buyer to bid.

What's the difference between a Walmart supplier and a Walmart seller?

A supplier sells products wholesale to Walmart, which resells them. A Marketplace seller keeps ownership of inventory and sells directly to Walmart.com shoppers, paying referral fees on each sale.

Where do I find wholesale suppliers for Walmart Marketplace?

Go to brands and their authorized distributors. Find products that sell first, then contact the brand or check its wholesale page. Expect to need an EIN and a resale certificate, and keep invoices.

Can I resell items bought from Sam's Club or Walmart?

Buying retail stock to resell is arbitrage. It's common, but margins are usually thin unless the item is on clearance. Check brand restrictions and the profit after Walmart fees before you buy.

How do I know if a wholesale product is profitable on Walmart?

Subtract the referral fee, WFS fees if used, inbound shipping, prep and your unit cost from the Walmart.com price. Margenos does this from a UPC and your cost, and shows sellers, stock and sales estimates.
