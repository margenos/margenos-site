Walmart's referral fee for health and personal care

Health sits in Walmart's Beauty, Health and Personal Care fee category: 8% when the total sales price is $10 or less, 15% when it's over $10. The rate applies to the whole price, and shipping counts toward the total.

Hypothetical examples: a $7.49 travel-size pain reliever with free shipping pays 8% = $0.60. A $14.99 bottle of vitamins pays 15% = $2.25. A 3-pack of the same vitamins at $39.99 pays $6.00. Multipacks raise the fee in dollars but often cut shipping per unit. See Walmart seller fees.

Approval and restrictions for health products

Walmart's prohibited products overview lists ingestible products, topical products and Class II or restricted medical devices as needing pre-approval, and names select OTC drugs, dietary supplements, medical devices and personal care product types as prohibited outright. For supplements, Walmart's policy adds:

  • Manufacturing: products must be made under FDA current Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP).
  • Labeling: listings need the statement of identity, net quantity, Supplement Facts panel, full ingredients, directions and manufacturer or distributor information. Structure/function claims need the FDA disclaimer; disease claims are prohibited.
  • Prohibited: expired supplements, products not in tamper-evident packaging, opened or repackaged items, and time- or temperature-sensitive products such as liquid vitamins and probiotics.
  • Claims: weight-loss products making disease or drug-type claims are prohibited, and claims need competent and reliable scientific evidence.

Products that need a prescription or medical supervision aren't allowed. Brand authorization matters here too; see Walmart restricted brands.

What sells and seasonality

Health is mostly replenishment: daily vitamins, oral care, shaving, first aid and household medicine-cabinet staples sell year-round. Cold and flu products and immune supplements peak in fall and winter, allergy products in spring, and fitness and weight-management products in January.

Sourcing health products and the risks

Wholesale is the safest route because distributor invoices support approval and authenticity; see Walmart wholesale. Retail and clearance deals on supplements are often clearance for a reason: short dating. Check the expiration before you check the price. Online arbitrage works for some personal care, but you can't inspect dates before buying.

  • Expiration dates are the biggest risk. Expired stock can't be sold, and WFS rejects short-dated items.
  • Counterfeit supplements exist and can be dangerous. Buy from verifiable sources.
  • Tamper evidence: broken seals make an item unsellable.
  • Temperature: heat can damage gummies and softgels in transit and storage.
  • Walmart sells most national health brands itself, often at low prices.

Checklist before you buy health products

  1. Confirm approval for ingestibles or topicals and the brand.
  2. Check the expiration date and how long it leaves to sell.
  3. Check the label for required panels and no disease claims.
  4. Look at sellers, stock and Walmart's offer on the listing.
  5. Run profit after the fee tier and fulfillment.

WFS notes for health products

WFS requires expiration or best-by dates in an accepted format (MM-DD-YYYY, YY-MM-DD or YYYY-MM-DD). Items expiring within 60 days won't be received, and items that reach 30 days or less are removed from inventory and returned or disposed of at your expense without reimbursement. Liquids need leak-proof packaging. See Walmart WFS fees.

Questions

Can you sell supplements on Walmart Marketplace?

Yes, with pre-approval for ingestible products. Supplements must be made under FDA cGMP, carry compliant labels, be sealed in tamper-evident packaging and be in date. Some supplement types are prohibited.

What is the Walmart referral fee for health and personal care?

It's in the Beauty, Health and Personal Care category: 8% when the total sales price is $10 or less and 15% when it's over $10.

Can I sell OTC medicine on Walmart?

Select OTC drug product types are prohibited, and ingestibles and topicals need pre-approval. Products that require a prescription aren't allowed. Check Seller Center for your account.

How much shelf life do health products need for WFS?

WFS won't receive items expiring within 60 days and removes items with 30 days or less remaining, at the seller's expense.
