How to win the Walmart Buy Box

What Walmart looks at when it picks the seller on the Add to cart button, and six ways to win more of it.

Updated September 30, 2026 · By the Margenos team

On Walmart.com, most shoppers click the big Add to cart button without comparing sellers. The seller attached to that button holds the Buy Box, and on a shared listing they get most of the sales. If you don't win the Buy Box, you usually don't sell much, no matter how good your price looks in the "more sellers" list.

How Walmart picks the Buy Box winner

Walmart doesn't publish an exact formula, but its seller guidance points to the same main factors:

FactorWhat Walmart looks at
Total priceItem price plus shipping. The lowest total price usually has the edge.
Delivery speedFaster promised delivery, especially 2-day, helps.
Seller performanceOn-time delivery, valid tracking, cancellations and refunds.
In-stock inventoryYou need stock available to be eligible.
Price competitivenessPrices far above other retailers can lose the Buy Box or be flagged.

How to win the Walmart Buy Box

  1. Price to the total, not the item. Compare item price plus shipping against the current Buy Box winner.
  2. Use Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS). WFS items get fast, reliable delivery badges, which can help you win at a slightly higher price. See the WFS fee chart to make sure it still pays.
  3. Keep your metrics clean. Ship on time, upload valid tracking and avoid cancellations. Walmart's targets include roughly 90%+ on-time delivery, 99% valid tracking and under 2% cancellations.
  4. Stay in stock. Running out drops you from the Buy Box and gives competitors your sales history.
  5. Know when Walmart is on the listing. When Walmart sells an item itself, it often wins. Unless you can match its price and speed, pick a different product.
  6. Don't race to the bottom. Set a minimum price from your real costs so a price war never turns into a loss.

Check the Buy Box before you buy inventory

The best time to think about the Buy Box is before you source a product. Look at who wins it now, who usually wins it, how often it changes hands, and at what price. If one seller or Walmart holds it almost all the time, your share of sales will be small.

Questions

What is the Buy Box on Walmart?

The Buy Box is the main Add to cart button on a Walmart.com product page. When several sellers offer the same item, the seller attached to that button gets most of the sales.

How does Walmart decide who wins the Buy Box?

Walmart weighs total price including shipping, delivery speed, seller performance and in-stock inventory. It does not publish an exact formula.

Does WFS help win the Buy Box on Walmart?

It can. WFS orders get fast, reliable delivery, which Walmart favors. Make sure the item is still profitable after WFS fees.

Can I win the Buy Box if Walmart sells the item?

It's hard. Walmart often wins its own listings with low prices and fast shipping. Many resellers skip products where Walmart is a seller.

Why did I lose the Walmart Buy Box?

Common reasons are a competitor with a lower total price, slower delivery than other sellers, running out of stock, a price that's high compared with other retailers, or weaker seller performance metrics.
