Walmart price tracker: how to check price history

Walmart.com shows today's price only. Here's how to see where a price has been, and why resellers watch it before they buy.

Walmart.com doesn't show price history. A product page tells you today's price, and sometimes a "was" price or a Rollback or Clearance tag, but not a chart of what the item sold for last month. To see that, you need a Walmart price tracker: a browser extension, app or website that records the price over time and shows it as a chart, with alerts when it drops.

Shoppers use a price tracker for Walmart to time a purchase. Resellers use one for a different reason: to know whether the price they're about to sell at will still be there when their inventory arrives. This page covers both.

Does Walmart show price history?

No. Walmart doesn't publish a price history chart on its product pages or in its app. The labels you do see are snapshots, not history:

  • Rollback and Reduced price tags say the price is lower now, but not for how long or how often it happens.
  • A "was" price shows one earlier price, not the full trend.
  • Clearance means the item is being marked down to clear, and prices can differ between stores. See how to resell Walmart clearance.

On a listing with many sellers, the price you see is also just the current Buy Box offer. The seller behind that price can change several times a day.

How to check Walmart price history

Because Walmart doesn't keep a public record, any Walmart price history check depends on a tool that has been taking its own snapshots of the item. Your options:

  1. Use a Walmart price tracker extension. Extensions add a price chart to Walmart.com product pages. Waltrack is one shopper-focused example on the Chrome Web Store; resellers usually want a tool that also shows sellers and fees.
  2. Use a Walmart price tracker app. Phone apps let you scan a barcode or paste a link and follow the item. Good for checking in the store.
  3. Track it yourself. Save the link and write down the price each time you check. It works for one item, but you'll miss short drops.

Whatever you use, history only starts when the tool started watching that item. If a tracker has no data, that's not proof the price never moved.

Why resellers track Walmart item price history

If you resell on Walmart Marketplace, Walmart item price history answers three questions before you put money into inventory:

  • Is today's price normal? A product that sells at $40 today but sat at $28 for most of the last few months is likely to fall back. Do your profit math at the lower price.
  • Is there a price war? Frequent small drops usually mean sellers keep undercutting each other. Your margin shrinks every time.
  • What happens when Walmart joins? When Walmart itself sells an item, the Buy Box price often drops and stays there.

Pair the price chart with a sales estimate and a stock check. A good price on an item that barely sells, or one where sellers are sitting on deep stock, is still a risky buy.

Questions

Does Walmart show price history?

No. Walmart.com shows the current price and sometimes a "was" price, Rollback or Clearance tag, but no price history chart. You need a third-party Walmart price tracker to see past prices.

How do I check Walmart price history?

Use a Walmart price tracker extension or app that has been recording the item's price. Open the product page or scan the barcode and the tool shows a chart of past prices. History only goes back to when the tool started tracking that item.
