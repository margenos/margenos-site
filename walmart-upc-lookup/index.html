How do I look up a Walmart UPC code?

A UPC is the 12-digit number printed under the barcode on a product's package. If you have the item in your hand, that number is the fastest Walmart UPC lookup there is. If you want Walmart's price for it, scan the barcode with the scanner in the Walmart app (it's in the search bar and on the Services tab) and the app shows the price and offers.

Going the other way, from a Walmart.com listing to its UPC, is harder. The code isn't always shown on the visible product page. Sellers often find it in the page's source code: open the listing, view the page source, and search for "upc". If it shows nothing or "null", Walmart.com doesn't provide it for that listing, and you'll need the code from the physical package.

Walmart UPC number vs Walmart item number

These two numbers get confused a lot:

  • UPC (GTIN): the manufacturer's barcode number. It's the same at every store that carries the product, which is why a UPC lookup works on items you find at Target or a drugstore.
  • Walmart item number: the ID Walmart gives a listing on Walmart.com, the number at the end of the product page address. It only means something on Walmart.

One UPC can also match more than one Walmart listing, for example a single item and a multipack, or an old and a new listing for the same product. That's why a plain UPC reader isn't enough for reselling: you need to know which listing you would actually sell on.

Walmart UPC codes for sellers

If you plan to sell on Walmart Marketplace, UPCs matter for listing too. Walmart's minimum qualifications ask for products with GS1-issued GTIN/UPC numbers. When you resell an existing brand-name product, you match the listing that's already on Walmart.com, so you use the product's existing UPC. When you create a brand-new product, you need your own GS1 barcodes.
