Tactical Arbitrage is bulk online arbitrage software, and it now has Walmart features in beta. According to a Threecolts blog post on Walmart tools, Tactical Arbitrage 2 (Beta) includes a Product Finder for Walmart that scans retail websites, compares their prices with Walmart Marketplace, and estimates profit after fees, plus Quick Picks product ideas from its database. Its Tactical Arbitrage page says it's part of Threecolts' Seller 365 bundle of 10 apps for $69 a month.

Margenos works differently: instead of scanning thousands of products in bulk, it checks one product at a time, fast, in a store or on a Walmart.com listing, and shows the details that decide a buy: fees, profit, ROI, a sales estimate, every competing seller with stock, and Buy Box and price history.

Questions

Does Tactical Arbitrage work for Walmart?

Threecolts says Tactical Arbitrage 2 (Beta) includes a Product Finder for Walmart that compares retail site prices with Walmart Marketplace and estimates profit after fees.

How much does Tactical Arbitrage cost?

Threecolts lists Tactical Arbitrage as part of Seller 365, a bundle of 10 apps for $69 a month. Check threecolts.com for current pricing.

What is a Tactical Arbitrage alternative for Walmart?

For item-by-item checks in stores or on Walmart.com, Margenos shows fees, profit, sales estimates and every competing seller. For bulk scanning, Tactical Arbitrage and similar OA tools generate lead lists.

Is Margenos online arbitrage software?

Margenos supports online arbitrage through its Chrome extension on any Walmart.com listing and retail arbitrage through its Android barcode scanner. It doesn't bulk-scan other retailers' sites.

Can I use Tactical Arbitrage and Margenos together?

Yes. Some sellers use bulk software to find leads and Margenos to double-check each lead's sellers, Buy Box and sales before buying.
