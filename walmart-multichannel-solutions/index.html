Walmart Multichannel Solutions: how MCS works and what it costs

Use your WFS inventory to fulfill Amazon, eBay, Shopify and other orders: eligibility, shipping speeds, fees and limits.

Walmart Multichannel Solutions (MCS) lets WFS sellers use the same inventory to fulfill orders from other sales channels. Walmart's Multichannel Solutions basics guide (updated Aug 20, 2026) names Amazon, eBay, Shein, Temu and TikTok Shop, and says any channel works if orders and returns follow that site's terms. Orders ship in unbranded packaging, and Walmart's MCS fees guide (updated Aug 5, 2026) says there are no referral fees on multichannel orders.

Who can use MCS

  • You must use Walmart Fulfillment Services.
  • You need a default billing method on file for WFS fees. Walmart currently accepts only U.S.-based ACH, and bank accounts take 2–3 business days to verify.
  • Items must meet WFS product requirements. Multi-box and big-and-bulky items aren't eligible, and items over 150 lb in unit or dimensional weight aren't eligible for multichannel.

Items list on Walmart.com by default, but Walmart's guide says you can choose to sell some only on other channels.

How orders get to Walmart

  1. Add a sales channel in Seller Center for each storefront (for example your Shopify site or eBay). Each channel gets a unique ID so you can see where orders came from.
  2. Send orders manually in Seller Center, through Walmart's APIs, or through a solution provider that automates it.
  3. Walmart picks, packs and ships from your WFS inventory in unbranded poly bags or brown boxes with plain tape.

Shipping speeds and limits

Expedited2 business daysNot available for items 31–150 lb
Standard3–5 business daysAvailable for all eligible items

Orders ship only to U.S. addresses in the 50 states and Puerto Rico, not to P.O. boxes. Walmart says speeds aren't guaranteed during peak (October 1–December 31) or sales events.

MCS fees

  • Fulfillment fee per order, based on weight and shipping speed. Walmart publishes the table in Seller Center's fee estimator and its MCS fees guide. Weight is the greater of unit weight or dimensional weight (L × W × H ÷ 139) for items over 1 lb, plus 0.25 lb for packaging.
  • Storage: the same as WFS standard storage fees.
  • Referral fee: none on multichannel orders.
  • Oversize fee: extra per unit if the longest side is over 48", the median side over 30", or longest side plus girth over 105", including packaging.

Walmart's marketing pages say MCS costs about 15% less on average than other marketplace fulfillment providers. Compare your own items against your current fulfillment costs before you move inventory; our WFS vs FBA guide shows how.

Is MCS worth it for resellers?

It's most useful if you already sell the same items on Walmart and another channel and want one inventory pool. Slow-moving or aging stock can sell wherever it moves fastest. The catch is that the inventory has to clear WFS's requirements first, and you pay WFS storage while it waits. Questions

What is Walmart Multichannel Solutions?

A WFS program that fulfills orders from other sales channels, such as Amazon, eBay, Shein, Temu, TikTok Shop and your own website, using the inventory you store with Walmart Fulfillment Services.

Does Walmart charge referral fees on MCS orders?

No. Walmart's MCS fees guide says there are no referral fees for multichannel orders. You pay a fulfillment fee per order and WFS storage fees.

How fast does Walmart MCS ship?

Expedited is 2 business days and standard is 3–5 business days. Items weighing 31–150 lb aren't eligible for expedited, and speeds aren't guaranteed during peak season.

Does Walmart MCS use Walmart-branded boxes?

No. Walmart says it ships multichannel orders in unbranded poly bags or brown boxes with plain tape and dunnage.

Can I use MCS without selling on Walmart.com?

You need to be a WFS seller, but Walmart's guide says you can choose to sell some items only on other channels instead of Walmart.com.
