Walmart seller verification: steps, documents and timing

What Walmart checks during business verification, the documents to have ready, how long it takes and the deadlines that close accounts.

Walmart seller verification is the first step of onboarding in Seller Center: Walmart confirms your business and the person applying for it. Walmart's seller registration guide says business verification "can vary from a few minutes to two business days." You'll enter your business details, upload a photo ID and, if asked, business documents. And if Walmart requests a live identity check, you have seven days to finish it or your account is closed.

The verification steps

From Walmart's business verification guide (updated Jun 15, 2026):

  1. Get started. Select Begin on the Seller Center home page and enter your country or region of incorporation, state business registration or business license number, business phone number and entity type.
  2. Primary contact. Choose the business role that describes your position.
  3. Identity document information. Enter your details exactly as they appear on your ID, especially your residential address. Walmart warns that inaccurate entries can delay your application.
  4. Upload documents. Each file must be under 5MB.
  5. Save or submit. Save as a draft or submit to Walmart.
  6. Verify your identity (only if asked). A third-party service uses your device camera to scan your ID and face. You must finish within seven days of the notification, "or your account will be closed."

Documents to have ready

  • Photo ID (required): front and back, clear and well-lit, as PNG, JPG or PDF.
  • Business document (if asked): a business license or registration certificate showing the company name, address and a date. Turn off the camera flash to avoid glare.
  • Proof of address (if asked): for example a utility bill addressed to the business, showing the company name, address and a date.
  • Tax details: U.S. sellers with an EIN use the W-9 classification, per Walmart's tax classifications guide.

Document requirements depend on your country of incorporation. Sellers outside the U.S. are asked for more, such as a certificate of incorporation and a utility bill or bank statement.

Why verification gets stuck

  • Name or address mismatches. Enter your legal business name and address exactly as they appear on your IRS EIN letter and state documents.
  • An unsupported entity type. Walmart's U.S. list is C-Corp, S-Corp, Partnership, LLC taxed as a C-Corp or S-Corp. See can you sell on Walmart as an individual.
  • Unreadable documents. Blurry photos, glare or cropped edges.
  • A missed identity check. The seven-day window is strict.

Some sellers in Walmart's forum report waiting weeks or months in "verifying" status. Walmart's replies there say some verification steps can take 7–10 business days and the overall process longer if more review is needed. If you're stuck, check the Business verification section of Seller Center for requested actions, then contact Seller Support.

After verification: the rest of onboarding

Walmart's registration guide lists five onboarding steps: verify your business, choose your payout method, add market details (display name and customer service info), manage fulfillment (shipping and returns) and set up your catalog. Account setup can take up to four hours after you add inventory. New sellers also go through a New Seller Payment Hold after they start selling; see Walmart seller payments.

Questions

How long does Walmart seller verification take?

Walmart's registration guide says business verification can take from a few minutes to two business days. Walmart forum replies say some steps can take 7–10 business days, and longer if extra review is needed.

What documents does Walmart need to verify my business?

A photo ID (front and back) is required. Walmart may also ask for a business document such as a license or registration certificate, and proof of address such as a utility bill, each showing the company name, address and a date.

What happens if I miss the identity verification?

If Walmart asks you to verify your identity, you must finish within seven days of the notification or your account will be closed, according to Walmart's business verification guide.

Why is my Walmart seller account stuck on verifying?

Common causes are name or address mismatches with your EIN letter, an unsupported entity type, or unreadable documents. Check the Business verification section in Seller Center for requested actions, then contact Seller Support.
