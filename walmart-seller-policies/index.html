The Walmart Marketplace seller agreement is officially the Comprehensive Walmart Marketplace Program Retailer Agreement, the contract every seller accepts. It makes you the seller of record, lets Walmart take a referral fee from each sale, requires you to sell only authentic products you have the right to sell, and lets Walmart remove listings, hold payments or end your account at its discretion. The agreement points to a set of "Retailer Policies" that carry the detailed rules. Walmart keeps all of them on one index page, the Walmart Marketplace seller retailer policies (updated Sep 4, 2026). This page summarizes them and links to our deeper guides.

Where to find the current Walmart seller policies

  • Retailer Agreement: linked as a PDF from the policy index page. The version linked in November 2026 is dated July 20, 2024, and covers the Marketplace program, Walmart Fulfillment Services and Walmart Ad Center.
  • Seller policies: the same index page lists 16 "seller agreement policies" (code of conduct, performance standards, returns, customer care, prohibited products, shipping, taxes, multiple accounts and more) and dozens of additional rules.
  • Seller privacy notice: the Walmart Global Marketplace Seller Privacy Notice (last updated Jul 24, 2026) explains what Walmart collects about sellers, including contact, identity, financial and transaction data, and how it uses it for onboarding, fraud prevention and account management.
  • Check the date: policies change often, so look at the "last updated" line on each guide before relying on it.

The Walmart Marketplace Program Retailer Agreement in plain English

Article II of the Retailer Agreement (Rev. July 2024) applies to every seller. The parts resellers should know:

SectionWhat it means for you
Walmart.com's roleEvery sale is between you and the customer, and you're the seller of record. Walmart provides the site and tools.
Legal right to sellYou can sell a product if you're an authorized reseller, bought it from an authorized reseller, or otherwise have a legal right to sell it. On request you must provide proof, such as authenticity certificates or confirmation from the brand or supplier.
AuthenticityNo counterfeit, stolen, illegal or infringing products, and you must keep processes to check that what you sell is authentic.
U.S. onlyProducts must be legal to sell and ship in all U.S. states.
Removal requestsIf Walmart asks you to remove a product or content, you make commercially reasonable efforts to do it within 24 hours.
Referral fees and payoutsWalmart's fee is a percentage of gross proceeds, including shipping and gift wrap. Payouts cover each 14-day period, sent within 7 days of its end. New sellers face an initial holding period, and Walmart can withhold payments during investigations.
FulfillmentYou're responsible for shipping and its problems (damage, loss, late delivery) unless you use WFS. Orders not shipped on time can be canceled automatically.
Pricing errorsWalmart can require you to honor a pricing mistake.
Returns and refundsYour return policy can't be less favorable than on your own site, and must follow Walmart's Returns Policy. You reimburse Walmart for refunds it gives.
Customer dataWalmart owns order data. You can't use it for marketing or contact customers to sell them something elsewhere.
AuditsWalmart can request compliance records within 30 days and audit your books, at your cost.
TerminationWalmart can terminate the agreement "at any time in its sole discretion," and can suspend you or remove listings if you violate it.

The Walmart Marketplace Seller Code of Conduct

The Marketplace Seller Code of Conduct (updated Aug 18, 2026) has six principles:

  1. Act fairly and with respect. No misleading item info, review manipulation, raising prices after an order, or sending materially different items.
  2. Provide accurate information in Seller Center, using your legal business name. No account switching or using other accounts to dodge enforcement.
  3. Don't divert customers to outside websites or storefronts.
  4. Communicate responsibly. Only order-related messages, sent through Seller Center.
  5. Meet Seller Performance Standards: cancellation rate, on-time delivery, valid tracking, seller response rate and negative feedback.
  6. Follow all Retailer Policies.

It also bans agreements that restrict competition and sharing competitively sensitive information, such as future prices, with other sellers. Breaking it "may result in removal of listings, account suspension or account termination."

Walmart seller policies resellers break most often

These come up again and again for resellers. Each has its own guide; this is the short version.

Pricing rules

Walmart's pricing rules unpublish offers priced egregiously higher than recent prices on Walmart.com or competing sites, and can make overpriced offers lose the Buy Box. High shipping fees count too. See our Walmart repricer guide.

Authenticity and brand restrictions

Selling a brand that doesn't allow resellers, or that files IP claims, is the fastest way to collect complaints. See Walmart restricted brands and Walmart Brand Portal and IP complaints. Keep invoices, because the agreement lets Walmart ask for proof of your right to sell.

Condition

New items only, unless Walmart has invited you to its Resold program. Walmart's resold products policy (updated Feb 12, 2026) prohibits misrepresenting condition, so opened or used retail returns can't go up as new.

Prohibited and pre-approval products

Some products are banned and some categories need approval first. The prohibited products policy says Walmart will automatically unpublish prohibited items. See Walmart prohibited items.

Walmart seller expiration date policy

For WFS, Walmart's prepare and pack guide (updated Jul 24, 2026) says items with an expiration or best-by date within 60 days won't be received, and items within 30 days are removed from inventory, returned or disposed of at your expense. Dates must be printed in an accepted format, and perishable items without a date are rejected. Check the date before you buy short-dated grocery or health items.

Shipping, returns and multiple accounts

  • Shipping: late shipments and invalid tracking hurt performance metrics. See Walmart shipping templates.
  • Returns: Walmart sets the return rules, not you. See the Walmart seller return policy.
  • Multiple accounts: Walmart's multiple seller accounts policy (updated Dec 10, 2025) says it "prohibits sellers from owning multiple accounts," with exceptions only at Walmart's discretion for a significant business reason.

What happens if you violate a Walmart seller policy?

It depends on the policy and how serious it is. Walmart says prohibited items are automatically unpublished. Walmart's trust and safety policy adds restricted sales, suspension or termination of selling privileges. Account-level enforcement runs from suppression to pause, suspension and termination, and payments can be held. Our Walmart account suspension guide explains each level and how to appeal.

Questions

What is the Walmart Marketplace seller agreement?

It's the Comprehensive Walmart Marketplace Program Retailer Agreement, the contract every seller accepts. It covers your role as seller of record, product rules, referral fees and payouts, fulfillment, returns, customer data, audits and termination.

Where can I read the Walmart Marketplace Retailer Agreement?

It's linked as a PDF from Walmart's Marketplace seller retailer policies page on Marketplace Learn. In November 2026 the linked version is dated July 20, 2024.

What does the Walmart Seller Code of Conduct say?

Six principles: act fairly and with respect, provide accurate information, don't divert customers off Walmart, only send necessary customer messages through Seller Center, meet performance standards, and follow all Retailer Policies.

Can I sell used items on Walmart Marketplace?

Only if Walmart has invited you to its Resold program. Otherwise Walmart's resold products policy limits you to new items, and misrepresenting condition is prohibited.

What is Walmart's expiration date policy for sellers?

For WFS, Walmart won't receive items with an expiration or best-by date within 60 days and removes items within 30 days, at the seller's expense. Dates must be printed in an accepted format.

What happens if I break a Walmart seller policy?

Depending on the violation, Walmart can unpublish items, restrict sales, hold payments, or suppress, pause, suspend or terminate your account.

Can I have more than one Walmart seller account?

Generally no. Walmart's multiple seller accounts policy prohibits owning multiple accounts, with exceptions only at Walmart's discretion for a significant business reason.
