Walmart prohibited items: what you can't sell

The categories Walmart bans or restricts, the rule that trips up most resellers, and how to check before you buy inventory.

Walmart's prohibited products policy lists what you can't sell, or can only sell with extra requirements, on Walmart Marketplace. It covers whole groups such as alcohol, drugs, tobacco and vaping, firearms and ammunition, recalled products, hazardous items, digital goods and software, live animals and anything that infringes someone's intellectual property. For resellers, the rule that matters most is the resold products policy: unless Walmart invites you to its Resold program, you can only list items in new condition. Prohibited vs restricted

Walmart uses one policy for two different things. Some products are prohibited outright. Others are allowed with restrictions: you need approval, specific documents (like a children's product certificate) or accurate claims in the listing. Brand and category approvals are covered separately in Walmart restricted brands and approval categories.

Walmart also bans products made or sourced in Iran, Cuba, North Korea and certain regions of Ukraine, and products made with forced labor.

Can you sell used items on Walmart?

Usually not. Walmart's resold products policy says selling items that aren't in new condition is prohibited unless Walmart invites you to its Resold program. Invited sellers can list Restored, Pre-Owned, Open Box or Remanufactured items in allowed categories, with disclosure rules. Some categories are closed to resold items even then, including cosmetics, food, hazardous materials and batteries, medical devices, pet supplies, tobacco and weapons.

For retail and clearance resellers, "new" means sealed, unused, in its original retail packaging. A returned or open-box item from a store shelf doesn't qualify. See Walmart clearance reselling and retail arbitrage for what does.

What happens if you list a prohibited item

Walmart says it will automatically unpublish an item that doesn't meet its category requirements and can remove any listing that breaks the law or its policies. Repeated or serious violations can lead to your seller account being suspended or terminated, and Walmart can remove a product category without notice. Unpublished items also hurt if you've already shipped stock to WFS, which has its own prohibited products list.

How to check a product before you buy it

  1. Check the category rules in Walmart's prohibited products policy for anything consumable, electrical, for kids or with batteries.
  2. Check brand and category approval for your account in Seller Center.
  3. Make sure it's new in original packaging, with an invoice or receipt.
  4. Look at the listing: who sells it, how much stock they have and whether it sells.
  5. Run the profit after Walmart's fees before you buy.

Questions

What items are prohibited on Walmart Marketplace?

Walmart's prohibited products policy covers alcohol, drugs and drug paraphernalia, tobacco and vaping, firearms and ammunition, recalled and hazardous products, digital goods and software, live animals, items that infringe intellectual property, and products from Iran, Cuba, North Korea or made with forced labor. Many other categories, like supplements, cosmetics and children's products, are allowed only with conditions.

Can I sell used items on Walmart?

Not unless Walmart invites you to its Resold program. Its resold products policy prohibits selling items that aren't in new condition; invited sellers can list Restored, Pre-Owned, Open Box or Remanufactured items in allowed categories.

What's the difference between prohibited and restricted on Walmart?

Prohibited products can't be listed at all. Restricted products can be listed if you meet extra requirements, such as category or brand approval, safety documents or accurate product claims.

What happens if I list a prohibited item on Walmart?

Walmart automatically unpublishes items that don't meet its requirements and can remove listings that violate law or policy. Serious or repeated violations can lead to suspension or termination of your seller account.

How do I check if I can sell a product on Walmart?

Check the category in Walmart's prohibited products policy, your brand and category approvals in Seller Center, and that the item is new. Then check demand and profit; Margenos shows sales estimates, sellers, stock and profit on the Walmart.com listing.
