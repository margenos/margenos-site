What Helium 10 does for Walmart sellers

Helium 10's help center describes four Chrome extension tools on Walmart.com. Xray opens on Walmart search results and shows data such as estimated revenue, average prices on Walmart and Amazon, and the share of products with free shipping; Helium 10 says it often returns up to 500 products. Supplier Finder, inside Xray, previews Alibaba suppliers with prices, order minimums and lead times. Demand Analyzer shows preliminary Amazon search results with estimated search volume. A Listing Quality Score widget rates a Walmart listing's content, price and ratings.

Beyond the extension, Helium 10 Profits pulls in Walmart financial data once you connect your seller account, and Helium 10 Ads (powered by Pacvue) manages Walmart Connect campaigns with bid rules, dayparting and automated bidding for auto campaigns. Its pricing page says Platinum and above include full Chrome extension access for Amazon, TikTok Shop and Walmart; the free plan has limited extension access.

What Margenos does for Walmart sellers

Margenos answers a narrower question in more depth: is this product worth buying to resell on Walmart? On any product page, the Chrome extension shows Walmart referral and WFS fees, profit, ROI, the most you can pay and your breakeven, with quantity, prep cost and WFS storage months included. It shows a sales estimate with its source (or "not enough data"), every competing seller with stock counts, who wins the Buy Box, and price and Buy Box history. The Android app scans barcodes in stores, and tracked products send push alerts.

Walmart.com has no public sales rank or price history, so Margenos builds its history from its own daily price and stock snapshots. It shows raw numbers, not "risky" or "safe" labels. You don't need a Walmart seller account.

Questions

Does Helium 10 work with Walmart?

Yes. Helium 10's Chrome extension runs Xray on Walmart search results and shows a Listing Quality Score on Walmart product pages. Its Profits tool connects to a Walmart seller account, and Helium 10 Ads supports Walmart Connect.

Is Helium 10 good for Walmart resellers?

It's built mainly for brands and Amazon-first sellers. Its Walmart help article doesn't list per-item Walmart fee math, competing seller stock or Buy Box history, which resellers rely on. Margenos is built for that job.

What is the best Helium 10 alternative for Walmart?

For reselling on Walmart, Margenos shows fees, profit, ROI, sales estimates, every competing seller with stock and Buy Box history on any Walmart.com product, with an Android barcode scanner. WAMP is another Walmart-specific option.

How much does Helium 10 cost for Walmart?

Helium 10's pricing page lists Platinum at $129/month ($99/month billed yearly) and Diamond at $359/month ($279/month billed yearly), with full Chrome extension access for Walmart on Platinum and above. A free plan has limited extension access. Check their site for current prices.

Does Margenos do keyword research or Walmart ads?

No. Margenos focuses on whether a product is worth reselling: fees, profit, sales estimates, sellers, stock and Buy Box history. For keyword and ad work, a suite like Helium 10 fits better.

Can I use Margenos and Helium 10 together?

Yes. They are separate Chrome extensions. A common split is Margenos for sourcing decisions and Helium 10 for ads, listings or accounting.

Is there a Keepa for Walmart?

Walmart.com doesn't publish price history or sales rank, so there's no built-in equivalent. Margenos charts a product's price and Buy Box history from its own daily snapshots.
