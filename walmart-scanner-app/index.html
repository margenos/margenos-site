Yes. Margenos is a Walmart reseller app for iPhone and Android: scan a product's barcode to see its Walmart listing, fees, profit, ROI, sales estimate, competing sellers and buy box.

Does the app work with the Chrome extension?

Yes. One Margenos account works on your phone and in Chrome, and your scans are one allowance shared between them.

No. Margenos works for anyone researching products to sell on Walmart Marketplace.
