Why resellers need a Walmart-specific scanner

Most reseller scanner apps were built for Amazon. They show Amazon fees and Amazon sales rank, which don't apply to Walmart Marketplace. Walmart has its own referral fees by category, its own WFS fulfillment fees, no public sales rank, and a different Buy Box. A Walmart scanner app needs to use Walmart's numbers, or the profit it shows is wrong.

Margenos is built only for Walmart. When you scan a barcode, it finds the matching Walmart.com listing (and lets you choose when several listings share one UPC), applies the right referral fee for the category and the WFS fee for the weight, and shows what you actually keep.
