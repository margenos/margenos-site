Where is the barcode scanner in the Walmart app?

If you just want to check a price, the barcode scanner at Walmart is built into the regular Walmart app. Walmart says the scanner icon is in the app's search bar and on the Services tab. Tap it, point your camera at the barcode, and the app shows the item's price along with offers. You don't need a separate Walmart scan app for that, and the Walmart app is available for Android and iPhone.

  1. Download the Walmart app and sign in or create an account.
  2. Tap the scanner icon in the search bar, or open it from the Services tab.
  3. Point your camera at the barcode on the item or shelf tag.
  4. Read the price, offers and availability the app shows.

Two other app features often get mixed up with the price scanner. Scan & Go lets you scan items as you shop and pay on your phone; Walmart launched it as a Walmart+ member benefit. Store mode shows in-store savings and aisle locations for items on your list. Both are built for buying, not for reselling.

What the Walmart app scanner doesn't tell a reseller

The Walmart app answers one question: what does Walmart charge for this item? If you plan to resell on Walmart Marketplace, you need a different set of answers: what Walmart takes in fees, what you keep, how fast it sells and who you would be competing with. That is the gap a Walmart scanner app for resellers fills.

Is there a Walmart scanner app for resellers?

Yes. The Margenos app is live on Google Play for Android (iPhone coming soon): scan a product's barcode to see its Walmart listing, fees, profit, ROI, sales estimate, competing sellers and buy box.

Do I need a Walmart Seller account?

Can I use an Amazon scanner app for Walmart?

Not accurately. Amazon scanner apps use Amazon's fees and sales rank. Walmart has different referral fees, WFS fees and no public sales rank, so a Walmart-specific scanner like Margenos gives you the real profit.

Does the Margenos scanner work in any store?

Yes. Scan a product's UPC in any store and Margenos finds the matching Walmart.com listing, so you can check clearance at Walmart, Target, Walgreens and other retailers.

Does the Walmart app have a price scanner?

Yes. The Walmart app has a built-in barcode scanner. Walmart says the scanner icon is in the search bar and on the Services tab. Scan an item's barcode and the app shows its price and offers.

How do I use the barcode scanner on the Walmart app?

Open the Walmart app, sign in, tap the scanner icon in the search bar (or on the Services tab), and point your camera at the barcode. The price and offers appear on screen.

What is the difference between Walmart Scan and Go and the price scanner?

The price scanner checks what an item costs. Scan and Go lets you scan items as you shop and pay on your phone, and Walmart offers it as a Walmart+ member benefit. Neither shows reseller numbers like fees or profit.

Is there a Walmart scanner app for Android?

Yes. Shoppers can use the Walmart app on Android. For reselling, the Margenos app on Google Play scans a barcode and shows the Walmart listing's fees, profit, ROI, sales estimate, competing sellers and Buy Box.

Can I use the Walmart app scanner to find items to resell?

It tells you Walmart's price, but not Walmart's seller fees, your profit, how fast the item sells or how many sellers you would compete with. A reseller scanner like Margenos adds those numbers.

Why is the Walmart app scanner not working?

Usually it's a permissions or setup issue. Make sure the Walmart app has camera access, you're signed in, location sharing is on, and the app is updated; then hold the barcode steady in good light. Walmart's Check a price page has troubleshooting for the scanner and for location sharing on iPhone and Android. If you're scanning to resell, the Margenos Android app is a separate scanner that also shows fees, profit and sellers.

Does the Walmart scanner app work in Canada?

The U.S. Walmart app's price scanner is built for Walmart.com and U.S. stores; Walmart Canada runs its own separate site and app at walmart.ca. Margenos also works only with Walmart.com (U.S.) listings, so it won't show Walmart Canada prices or fees.
