Is retail arbitrage legal?

Short answer: yes. Longer answer: the law is on your side, but marketplaces, brands and stores have rules of their own.

Yes, retail arbitrage is legal in the United States. Buying products at a store and reselling them for more is protected by the first sale doctrine: once a brand sells a genuine item, the buyer can resell it without the brand's permission. The US Supreme Court confirmed this for copyrighted goods in Quality King v. L'anza (1998) and Kirtsaeng v. John Wiley & Sons (2013), and trademark law follows the same principle for genuine goods. What can get a reseller in trouble isn't the law against reselling; it's selling fakes, misdescribing products, or breaking a marketplace's or store's own rules. What the first sale doctrine covers

The first sale doctrine means a brand's control over a specific item ends after that item's first authorized sale. You can buy a toy on clearance at Walmart, Target or Walgreens and sell it on Walmart Marketplace, eBay or Amazon. A brand can't sue you for trademark infringement just for reselling its genuine, unaltered product, and it can't stop you with a "do not resell" sticker alone.

It doesn't cover everything. Reselling becomes infringement when the product is materially different from what the brand sells (repackaged, altered, damaged, expired, missing its warranty, or a gray-market version with different features), when it's counterfeit, or when your listing suggests you're the brand or an authorized dealer when you're not.

What can still get you in trouble

RiskWhat it means for resellers
Marketplace rulesWalmart, Amazon and eBay can restrict brands and categories even when resale is legal. Walmart only allows new items unless you're in its Resold program. See restricted brands and prohibited items.
CounterfeitsSelling fakes is illegal even if you bought them in good faith. Keep receipts and buy from real retailers.
Material differencesDamaged, expired, repackaged or bundle-broken items can count as different from the genuine product.
Store policiesStores can set purchase limits and refuse sales. Coupon and price-match terms often say "no resellers"; breaking them isn't a crime but can get you banned.
Sales tax and incomeProfit from reselling is taxable income. Many states let resellers buy tax-free with a resale certificate and collect tax on the resale; marketplaces like Walmart collect sales tax in most states.
Recalls and safety rulesSelling recalled products is prohibited, and kids' products need safety certificates on Walmart. See selling toys on Walmart.

Is it legal to resell Walmart products?

Yes. Buying from Walmart stores or Walmart.com and reselling is legal under the same doctrine. Two Walmart-specific points: Walmart can limit how many units you buy, and dropshipping straight from Walmart.com to another marketplace's buyer breaks other marketplaces' rules; see dropshipping from Walmart. Selling on Walmart Marketplace itself needs an approved Walmart seller account.

How to do retail arbitrage the right way

  1. Sell only new, genuine items in original packaging, and keep every receipt.
  2. Check brand and category restrictions on the marketplace before you buy.
  3. Describe items accurately and never imply you're an authorized dealer.
  4. Track your income and sales tax from day one.
  5. Check demand and profit before you buy, so legal inventory is also sellable inventory.

Questions

Is retail arbitrage legal?

Yes. In the US, the first sale doctrine lets you resell genuine products you bought without the brand's permission. It's illegal to sell counterfeits or materially different products, and marketplaces and stores can set their own rules.

Can brands stop me from reselling their products?

Not through the law alone, for genuine and unaltered products. Brands can restrict who sells on a marketplace through brand approval programs, enforce rules on authorized dealers, and file complaints when products are damaged, expired or misdescribed.

Is it legal to buy from Walmart and resell?

Yes. Reselling products bought at Walmart is legal. Walmart can limit quantities, and having Walmart.com ship directly to your buyers on another marketplace breaks those marketplaces' dropshipping rules.

Do I need a license to do retail arbitrage?

There's no special reselling license, but you may need a state sales tax permit, and you owe income tax on profits. Many states let registered resellers use a resale certificate to buy inventory without paying sales tax.

Can I sell used or open-box items on Walmart?

Only if Walmart invites you to its Resold program. Otherwise Walmart Marketplace only allows new items.
