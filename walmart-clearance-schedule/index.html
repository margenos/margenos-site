Is there a Walmart clearance schedule?

What's known (and not) about when Walmart marks down clearance, the seasonal times resellers watch, and how to check a markdown before you buy.

Walmart doesn't publish a clearance or markdown schedule. Markdowns are set by Walmart's systems and vary by store, department and item, so any "Walmart clearance day" you read online is a pattern people noticed, not an official rule. What you can rely on is the seasonal rhythm of retail and the fact that clearance prices step down over time until an item sells out.

How Walmart clearance works

  • Prices step down. Clearance items are marked down, often more than once, until they're gone. Unlike a rollback, clearance doesn't go back up.
  • Tags can lag the system. The register price can be lower than the shelf tag, which is why resellers scan items with the Walmart app. See hidden clearance.
  • It varies by store. The same item can be on clearance in one store and full price in the next.

Questions

Does Walmart have a clearance schedule?

No. Walmart doesn't publish a clearance or markdown schedule. Markdowns vary by store, department and item.

What day does Walmart mark down clearance?

There's no official markdown day. Resellers share patterns online, but they vary by store and change, so scan items with the Walmart app to see the current price.

When is the best time to find Walmart clearance?

After major holidays and at the end of each season, when stores clear seasonal items, plus during toy and electronics resets.

How low do Walmart clearance prices go?

Clearance prices step down over time until an item sells out. How far depends on the item and store; Walmart doesn't publish markdown percentages.

Can I resell seasonal clearance on Walmart Marketplace?

Often, but some seasonal products, such as Halloween items, need Walmart's pre-approval. Check profit after fees and whether the item will sell before next season.
