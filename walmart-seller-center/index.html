Walmart Seller Center: login, support and how it works

Where to log in, what each part of the Walmart seller dashboard does, and how to reach Walmart seller support when something breaks.

Walmart Seller Center is the dashboard approved Walmart Marketplace sellers use to list products, manage orders, track payments and contact support. Many people search for "Walmart Seller Central" because that's Amazon's name for its version, but Walmart's is called Seller Center. The official login is seller.walmart.com. You can only sign in after Walmart approves your seller application.

Walmart Seller Center login

Every version of this search, whether "Walmart seller login," "Walmart marketplace login" or "Walmart seller portal," ends up at the same place: seller.walmart.com. Type it in yourself or use a bookmark, and don't sign in through links in unexpected emails. Phishing pages that copy seller logins are common on every marketplace.

  • Not a seller yet? You create your Seller Center login when you apply, then finish onboarding inside Seller Center before you can sell (see Walmart seller login help). Start with our guide to opening a Walmart seller account.
  • Forgot your password? Use the reset link on the login page.
  • Can't get in at all? The login page has a Contact support link that works without signing in.
  • Using WFS? There's no separate WFS login. Walmart Fulfillment Services is managed inside Seller Center.

Margenos is not affiliated with Walmart and never asks for your Seller Center login. It works on public Walmart.com product pages.

What's inside Walmart Seller Center

The exact menus change as Walmart updates the platform, but the main areas of the Walmart seller hub stay the same:

AreaWhat you do there
Items / catalogAdd items by matching existing listings or creating new ones, update prices and inventory, fix listing errors.
OrdersAcknowledge, ship and cancel orders; handle returns and refunds.
WFSCreate inbound shipments to Walmart warehouses and track fulfilled inventory.
PerformanceTrack on-time delivery, cancellations, response rate and your Pro Seller progress.
PaymentsSee payouts, reconciliation reports and fees.
SettingsShipping templates, return settings, tax settings, users and permissions.
AdvertisingRun Walmart Sponsored Search campaigns if you choose to.

Two areas matter most for resellers. Performance decides whether you keep selling: falling short of Walmart's standards can lead to suppressed listings or a suspension. It's also where you track the metrics behind the Walmart Pro Seller badge. Payments is where you check what you actually earned after the referral fees and WFS fees.

How to contact Walmart Seller Support

Walmart seller support runs through Seller Center, not a public phone line. Walmart's own guide gives these steps:

  1. Logged in: click the Help button in the top menu bar and choose Contact support.
  2. Describe the issue in 5 to 200 words.
  3. Pick the category that fits best, then submit your case.
  4. Locked out? Go to the Seller Center login page, select Contact support, pick your market and a category.

Seller Center also has Marty, Walmart's AI assistant, for quick questions, and a search bar that pulls up Marketplace Learn guides.

How to call Walmart seller support

There's no published direct phone number for seller support. Seller guides report that you can request a callback from the Help menu for some issue types, and that live chat is offered during business hours. Walmart's regular customer service line is for shoppers, not sellers, so it can't fix Seller Center problems.

Walmart Seller Academy and seller help

Walmart's official training lives on Marketplace Learn (marketplacelearn.walmart.com). It has written help guides, FAQs and the Seller Academy, which offers short video tutorials and certification courses such as Getting Started on Walmart Marketplace, Listing Your Products, Getting Started with Walmart Fulfillment Services, and Optimizing Your Listings for Pro Seller Success. When a policy question comes up, Marketplace Learn is the first place to check because it's Walmart's own wording.

Walmart seller tax documents

Tax settings and tax forms are handled in Seller Center:

  • Sales tax: Walmart is a marketplace facilitator. It calculates, collects and remits sales tax on orders shipped to most states, including Texas, California, Florida and New York. For any state not on Walmart's list, you're still responsible unless Walmart tells you otherwise.
  • Form 1099-K: Walmart reports seller payments to the IRS; your 1099-K is emailed by your payment processor, per Marketplace Learn's Retrieve Form 1099-K guide. See Walmart seller taxes.
  • Your tax info: the W-9 (or W-8) you submitted at sign-up must match your business's legal name and EIN.

Keep your own records too. Payout reports show what Walmart paid you, but not what you paid for inventory. Our guide to calculating Walmart profit shows how to put both together.

Walmart seller forums and Reddit

Walmart runs an official Seller Forum on Marketplace Learn, where moderators answer questions. Sellers also gather in Reddit communities about e-commerce and reselling, Facebook groups and YouTube. Those are useful for real-world experience: how long approvals took, which categories need approval, how a suspension appeal went. Treat them as stories, not policy. Rules change, and a post from two years ago may be wrong today. Check anything important against Marketplace Learn or a Seller Support case.

Questions

Is it Walmart Seller Central or Seller Center?

Walmart calls it Seller Center. "Seller Central" is Amazon's name, but people use both terms for Walmart's seller dashboard.

What is the Walmart Seller Center login URL?

The official login is seller.walmart.com. You can only sign in once Walmart has approved your seller application.

Does Walmart have a phone number for seller support?

There's no published direct phone line for sellers. Open a case in Seller Center by clicking Help, then Contact support; seller guides report callbacks are available for some issue types.

Does Walmart collect sales tax for sellers?

Yes, in the states on its marketplace facilitator list, which covers most states with sales tax. Walmart calculates, collects and remits the tax on those orders.

Where do I find my Walmart 1099-K?

Your payment processor emails it to you, per Walmart's Retrieve Form 1099-K guide on Marketplace Learn. You get one if you're a W-9 entity with more than 200 orders and more than $20,000 in gross sales for the year.

What is Walmart Seller Academy?

Walmart's official training area on Marketplace Learn, with video tutorials and certification courses on listing, shipping, WFS, pricing and advertising.

Is there a Walmart seller forum?

Yes. Walmart runs an official Seller Forum on Marketplace Learn (marketplacelearn.walmart.com/forum), where moderators answer questions. Sellers also talk on Reddit, Facebook groups and YouTube; check anything important against Marketplace Learn.

Where do I find sales reports and analytics in Walmart Seller Center?

In the Reports dashboard (seller.walmart.com/reports/overview). Walmart's reports guide says it covers sales at the account, item and department level, plus catalog, pricing and Buy Box, orders, fulfillment and assortment reports, which you can schedule, save or re-download. Those reports cover your own store only; to research products you don't sell yet, use a tool like the Margenos sales estimator.

Is there a Walmart seller Discord or Facebook group?

Not an official one. Walmart runs its own Seller Forum on Marketplace Learn, where Walmart moderators answer questions, while Discord servers, Facebook groups and Reddit communities are run independently by sellers. Unofficial groups can be useful for tips, but confirm policy answers on Walmart's own pages and never share your login or payout details there.
