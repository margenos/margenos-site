Can you sell Walmart products on Amazon?

Buying at Walmart and reselling on Amazon or eBay is allowed if you own the inventory. Here's where it goes wrong, and how to check the numbers.

Can you sell Walmart products on Amazon? Yes, if you buy them first. Buying products at Walmart, in a store or on Walmart.com, and reselling inventory you own on Amazon or eBay is retail or online arbitrage. The platforms allow it, within their brand, category and condition rules. What they don't allow is listing an item and then ordering it from Walmart to ship straight to your buyer. That's retail-to-retail dropshipping, and Amazon and eBay both prohibit it. Walmart can also limit quantities or refuse sales to resellers.

Amazon's policy also requires you to be the seller of record: no packing slips, invoices or packaging showing another seller's name. A Walmart box or receipt in your buyer's package breaks that. eBay does allow dropshipping from a wholesale supplier, just not from a retailer. Arbitrage vs. dropshipping: the difference that matters

In arbitrage you pay for the product up front, hold it, inspect it, and ship it yourself or send it to Amazon FBA. You carry the inventory risk, but the buyer gets your package. In retail dropshipping you never touch the product, and the buyer gets Walmart's package. Only the first is a business the platforms accept. Walmart arbitrage and online arbitrage on Walmart cover sourcing from both directions.

Walmart receipts vs. invoices for Amazon ungating

This is the biggest practical problem with Walmart-to-Amazon flips. Many brands and categories on Amazon need approval before you can list them, and Amazon decides those requests. Sellers commonly report that retail receipts, including Walmart's, get rejected and that Amazon wants invoices from a manufacturer or distributor. We couldn't confirm Amazon's current document rules on a public Amazon page; they're shown inside Seller Central when you request approval. A receipt may also do little for you if a brand files an authenticity complaint.

The safe approach: before you buy, check each product in Seller Central to see whether you can list it. Skip items that need approval you don't have. Keep every receipt anyway, because it proves you bought genuine goods.

Best Walmart items to sell on Amazon and eBay

No list stays current, because prices move daily. Resellers tend to look for the same patterns:

  • Clearance and rollbacks on items that hold their price elsewhere. See Walmart clearance reselling.
  • Seasonal and holiday items (toys, decor) bought before demand peaks.
  • Walmart-exclusive or discontinued products that other stores no longer carry.
  • Items out of stock elsewhere, where the other marketplace's price has jumped.

Avoid brands you're not approved for, hazmat and fragile items, and low-priced items where fees and shipping leave nothing. On eBay, collectibles and bundles you assemble yourself are common. On Amazon, a restricted brand can strand inventory you already paid for.

When selling on Walmart Marketplace is the better move

If you already have a Walmart seller account, the same item might sell better on Walmart.com itself: the brand may be gated on Amazon but not on Walmart, or the Walmart listing may have fewer sellers. How to sell on Walmart and Amazon from one inventory

Once you sell on both, the risk is overselling the same unit twice. Three common setups:

  • Ship it yourself from one stock and keep quantities synced with a listing or inventory tool.
  • Walmart Fulfillment Services: Walmart's WFS page says its Multichannel Solutions program fulfills orders from your website, Amazon, eBay, Shopify, TikTok Shop and more.
  • Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment: Amazon says MCF fulfills orders from non-Amazon channels and ships them in unbranded packaging by default. Walmart's packaging policy allows multi-channel fulfillment in neutral packaging and unbranded delivery vehicles.

Whichever you choose, the buyer should receive a package that identifies you, not another retailer.

Questions

Can I sell Walmart products on Amazon?

Yes, if you buy the products first and ship inventory you own, yourself or through FBA. Amazon's brand and category approvals still apply. Listing an item and having Walmart ship it to your Amazon buyer breaks Amazon's drop shipping policy.

Can you sell Walmart products on eBay?

Yes, as arbitrage: buy the item, then ship it to your eBay buyer yourself. eBay's drop shipping policy doesn't allow buying from another retailer or marketplace that ships directly to your customer.

Can I dropship from Walmart to Amazon?

No. Amazon prohibits buying from another online retailer and having it ship directly to customers, and requires you to be the seller of record on every package. eBay has the same rule for retailers.

Will Amazon accept a Walmart receipt for ungating?

Amazon decides each approval request. Sellers commonly report that retail receipts are rejected and that Amazon asks for manufacturer or distributor invoices. Check the requirements shown in Seller Central before you buy gated products.

Does Walmart limit how much I can buy to resell?

It can. Walmart.com's Terms of Use say Walmart may limit quantities per person, household or order, may prohibit sales to dealers or resellers, and may refuse or cancel orders.

Is Walmart to Amazon arbitrage legal?

Reselling genuine products you bought is generally legal in the U.S., but each marketplace's brand, category and condition rules still apply. This isn't legal advice; see our page on whether retail arbitrage is legal.
