Walmart's built-in Repricer

Walmart calls its Repricer a "free automated pricing tool." According to Walmart's Repricer overview:

  • You accept the Automate Pricing terms first, which authorize Walmart to adjust prices for you. Strategies live on the Automate pricing page in Seller Center.
  • Every item needs a minimum and maximum price before it can be assigned to a strategy. Walmart suggests min and max prices to help.
  • It changes the item price only, not shipping.
  • Allow up to four hours for prices to adjust after you assign an item to a strategy.
  • A dashboard shows strategy performance over 7, 30, 60 or 90 days, including GMV from repricing, price competitiveness, Buy Box win rate and conversion rate.
  • Simulations let you preview how a strategy would perform before turning it on.

Walmart also says using the Repricer can help you qualify for referral fee discounts, under a separate incentive offer with its own terms and conditions on the Repricer page.

Amazon and Walmart repricer: one tool for both?

If you sell the same items on both marketplaces, an Amazon and Walmart repricer like Aura, Seller Snap or Informed keeps your rules in one place. Watch for tools that simply copy Amazon prices to Walmart. Walmart's fees, Buy Box and competitors are different, so the best Walmart price isn't always your Amazon price. Walmart pricing rules every repricer must follow

Whatever tool you use, Walmart's pricing rules still apply, and a bad min or max can get you in trouble:

  • Reasonable price: offers priced "egregiously higher" than prices currently or recently offered on Walmart.com or a competing website are unpublished automatically. Item reports show the reason "Reasonable Price Not Satisfied."
  • Buy Box ineligibility: an offer priced substantially higher than recent prices, but not high enough to unpublish, loses its Add to Cart button. Shoppers can still find it under More Seller Options.
  • Egregious shipping: very high shipping fees can unpublish an offer or make it Buy Box ineligible. Walmart limits shipping cost edits to 10 per template per day; going over locks the template for 24 hours.

Walmart reviews both the item price and the shipping fee, so set your repricer's maximum with total cost to the shopper in mind.

Walmart price parity

"Walmart price parity" usually means keeping your Walmart price in line with other sites. Walmart's pricing rules page doesn't state a rule requiring identical prices across channels. In practice, though, the reasonable price rule and Buy Box eligibility compare your offer to prices on competing websites, and Walmart's Repricer can price against external retailers directly. Pricing far above other sites tends to cost you the Buy Box. For how it's decided, see how the Walmart Buy Box works.

Questions

Does Walmart have a free repricer?

Yes. Walmart's Repricer is a free tool in Seller Center. You set a minimum and maximum price per item and assign it to a strategy based on the Walmart Buy Box price, external retailers' prices, or both.

What is the best Walmart repricer?

Start with Walmart's own free Repricer. If you also sell on Amazon or need more rules, compare third-party tools such as Aura, Seller Snap, Informed and Repricer.com on their own sites; most offer a free trial.

Is there a repricer for both Amazon and Walmart?

Yes. Aura, Seller Snap and Informed each say they support both. Repricer.com is Amazon-first and pushes Amazon prices to Walmart through its Multichannel feature.

How fast does Walmart's Repricer change prices?

Walmart says to allow up to four hours for prices to adjust after you add an item and select a strategy.

Can a repricer get my Walmart listing unpublished?

It can if your maximum is too high. Walmart unpublishes offers priced egregiously higher than recent prices on Walmart.com or competing websites, and offers priced substantially higher can lose the Buy Box.

Does Walmart require price parity with Amazon?

Walmart's pricing rules don't state a requirement for identical prices across channels, but they compare your price with competing websites when deciding whether it's reasonable and Buy Box eligible.

Does Margenos reprice my Walmart listings?

No. Margenos doesn't change prices or connect to your seller account. It shows price and Buy Box history, competing sellers and your profit so you can decide whether a product is worth buying before you ever need a repricer.
