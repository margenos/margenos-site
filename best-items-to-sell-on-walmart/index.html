What sells best on Walmart Marketplace

The categories that sell, the five checks every product must pass, and how to find items that make money after Walmart's fees.

Updated September 30, 2026 · By the Margenos team

The best items to sell on Walmart Marketplace are everyday products that sell steadily, have room for profit after Walmart's fees, and aren't already crowded with sellers. There's no single "hot list" that works for everyone, because a product that's profitable today can flood with competition next month. What works is a repeatable way to check any product before you buy it.

What makes a product worth selling

Before you buy any product for Walmart, check these five things. If it fails one, pass.

  • Profit after all fees. Subtract the referral fee, WFS fees if you use them, and what you paid. Many resellers want at least $3–5 profit per unit.
  • ROI. A 30% or better return on your cost gives you room if the price drops.
  • Sales speed. Walmart has no public sales rank, so use a sales estimator. Products that sell several times a week turn your money over faster.
  • Competition. Count the sellers on the listing and how much stock they have. If ten sellers have 50 units each and it sells 20 a month, you'll wait a long time.
  • Whether Walmart sells it. When Walmart itself is on the listing at a low price, it usually wins the Buy Box.

Where to find products to sell

  • Retail clearance. Walmart, Target and drugstore clearance aisles. See how to resell Walmart clearance.
  • Online deals. Discounted items on retail websites. See Walmart arbitrage.
  • Wholesale. Buying cases from authorized distributors gives you repeatable stock and fewer brand problems.
  • Competitor storefronts. Look at what other successful sellers carry, then check whether you can source it cheaper. Margenos's seller lookup shows a seller's products with profit for each.

Products to avoid

  • Restricted brands and categories you aren't approved for. Walmart can remove listings or suspend accounts.
  • Anything that looks counterfeit or comes without a receipt or invoice you can show Walmart.
  • Oversized or heavy items unless the price is high enough to cover fulfillment.
  • Items under about $10 unless they sell fast, since fixed fees eat most of the margin.
  • Listings with a price war, where the Buy Box price keeps dropping every week.

Questions

What is the best thing to sell on Walmart?

There is no single best product. The best items are everyday products with steady sales, at least a few dollars of profit after Walmart's referral and WFS fees, and few competing sellers. Check every product's profit, sales speed and competition before you buy.

What sells the most on Walmart Marketplace?

Household, health and personal care, beauty, grocery, toys, pet supplies and home goods are consistently strong categories, because Walmart shoppers buy them often.

How do I know if a product will sell on Walmart?

Walmart doesn't publish a sales rank, so use a sales estimator, check the product's reviews and sales badges, and watch how quickly competing sellers' stock goes down.

How much profit should I make per item on Walmart?

Many resellers aim for at least $3 to $5 of profit per unit and a 30% or better return on cost, after Walmart's referral fee and any WFS fees.

Can I resell brand-name products on Walmart?

Often yes, if you bought them legitimately and can show receipts or invoices. Some brands and categories are restricted and need Walmart's approval first.
