Can you sell on Walmart as an individual?

The short answer, the business types Walmart accepts, and what to set up before you apply.

No, not in the U.S. Walmart Marketplace is for registered businesses. Walmart's seller registration guide lists a Business Tax ID or Business License Number as a requirement and says Social Security numbers are not accepted. And the U.S. entity types in Walmart's business entity type guide (updated Oct 8, 2025) are C-Corporation, S-Corporation, Partnership, LLC taxed as a C-Corp and LLC taxed as an S-Corp. There is no "individual" or "sole proprietor" option.

What Walmart accepts

During business verification you pick an entity type from a fixed list. Walmart's guide defines the U.S. (W-9) options:

Entity typeWhat it is (Walmart's definition, summarized)
C-CorporationA corporation taxed separately from its owners; generally files Form 1120
S-CorporationA corporation that elected to pass income and losses to shareholders; generally files Form 1120-S
LLC – C-CorpAn LLC that elected to be taxed as a C-Corporation
LLC – S-CorpAn LLC that elected to be taxed as an S-Corporation
PartnershipA business owned by two or more people or entities; generally files Form 1065

Outside the U.S., Walmart's tax classifications guide says it supports the W-8BEN classification only for sole proprietors from Mexico or India, and Mexican sole proprietors can onboard only to the Mexico market.

What about a single-member LLC?

This is the most common question, and Walmart's list doesn't name it. A single-member LLC is taxed like a sole proprietorship by default. In Walmart's own Marketplace Learn forum, sellers have asked for a "single-member LLC" option, and Walmart's replies say the types in Seller Center are the only options and that a business that doesn't fit them isn't currently accepted. Walmart's advice is to confirm your entity type with a tax or legal professional before you register. Some LLC owners file an S-Corp election with the IRS, which would make "LLC – S-Corp" accurate; whether that makes sense for you is a question for your accountant.

What you need before you apply

  1. A supported business entity from the list above, registered with your state.
  2. An EIN. The IRS issues them free. Walmart's business verification guide (updated Jun 15, 2026) also asks for your state business registration or business license number.
  3. Photo ID and business documents. Walmart asks for front and back of a photo ID, and may ask for a business document (license or registration certificate) and proof of address showing your company name, address and a date.
  4. Products with GS1 UPCs (or a GTIN exemption) and a catalog that follows Walmart's prohibited products policy.
  5. A way to ship and handle returns: WFS or your own U.S. warehouse with returns capability.

Questions

Can I sell on Walmart with my Social Security number?

No. Walmart's seller registration guide says SSNs are not accepted. You need a Business Tax ID (an EIN for U.S. businesses) or a business license number.

Does Walmart accept sole proprietors?

Not in the U.S. Walmart's U.S. entity list is C-Corporation, S-Corporation, Partnership, LLC taxed as a C-Corp and LLC taxed as an S-Corp. It supports sole proprietors only from Mexico or India, under the W-8BEN classification.

Can a single-member LLC sell on Walmart?

Walmart's list doesn't include a single-member or disregarded-entity option. Walmart's forum replies say businesses that don't fit the listed types aren't currently accepted. Ask a tax professional whether an S-Corp election fits your situation before you apply.

Do I need a seller account to research Walmart products?

No. Tools like Margenos show fees, profit, sales estimates and competing sellers on any Walmart.com listing without a seller account.

How long does Walmart business verification take?

Walmart's registration guide says business verification can take from a few minutes to two business days.
