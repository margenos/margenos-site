Walmart seller performance standards are the thresholds every seller-fulfilled account is held to. Walmart's seller performance standards guide (updated Jul 28, 2026) lists eight metrics, evaluated over the last 30 or 60 days. Miss one and Walmart says you "must take immediate action," because failure to improve "may result in suppression, suspension or termination of your account." Using WFS covers most of them for you, except the Negative Feedback Rate.

What each one means and how to protect it

Cancellation Rate

Orders you cancel after Walmart sends them. The usual cause is selling stock you don't have. Keep inventory counts accurate, and don't list items you can't restock quickly.

On-Time Delivery and Late Shipment

Delivery on or before the expected delivery date, and handing the package to the carrier on time. Walmart's guide says you're accountable for late hand-over, carrier delays and a carrier delivery estimate later than promised; weather and carrier exceptions aren't counted against you. Set realistic lag times in your shipping templates.

Valid Tracking Rate

Orders with valid tracking. Invalid tracking URLs, misleading tracking and non-integrated carriers count against you. Only share tracking after the package is tendered to the carrier.

Seller Response Rate

Customer messages answered within 48 hours.

Return Rate, Negative Feedback and Item Not Received

These reflect what arrives: wrong or damaged items, misleading listings, lost packages. Accurate titles and photos and good packaging help all three. Walmart's returns rules also apply.

How WFS changes the picture

Walmart's guide says that if you use Walmart Fulfillment Services, "most performance metrics will be met for you, with the exception of the Negative Feedback Rate." For resellers who struggle with shipping times, that's a real argument for WFS. Weigh it against the fees in our WFS fees guide or run your numbers in the WFS fee calculator.

Metrics and the Buy Box

Performance also shapes who wins sales. Strong metrics feed into eligibility for the Pro Seller badge, and shipping speed matters in the Buy Box. Questions

What are Walmart's seller performance standards?

Walmart lists eight: Cancellation Rate 2% or below, On-Time Delivery 90% or above, Valid Tracking 99% or above, Seller Response 95% or above, Negative Feedback 2% or below, Return Rate 6% or below (9% for Resold), Item Not Received 2% or below, and Late Shipment 5% or below.

What happens if I don't meet Walmart's performance standards?

Walmart says you must take immediate action to improve, and failure to do so may result in suppression, suspension or termination of your account. It notifies you by email and in Seller Center.

Does WFS count toward my performance metrics?

Walmart says that if you use WFS, most performance metrics will be met for you, with the exception of the Negative Feedback Rate.

What is Walmart's valid tracking rate requirement?

99% or above. Invalid tracking URLs, misleading tracking and non-integrated carriers count against it.

How long do I have to answer Walmart customer messages?

Walmart measures the Seller Response Rate as inquiries answered within 48 hours, and the standard is 95% or above.
