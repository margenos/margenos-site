Choosing a Walmart sourcing tool

WAMP and Margenos both help resellers decide what to buy for Walmart Marketplace. The right one depends on how you source. Ask yourself these questions:

  • Do you source in stores? You'll want a phone app with a barcode scanner. Margenos has one; WAMP doesn't list barcode scanning.
  • Do you have a Walmart seller account yet? WAMP requires one. Margenos works without one, so you can research while your application is reviewed.
  • Do you want alerts? Margenos can alert you when a tracked product's price moves, a seller sells out, or a new seller joins.
  • What's your budget? WAMP says it's free during beta. Margenos starts at $24.99 a month with a 14-day free trial.

SellerAmp for Walmart

SellerAmp is best known for SellerAmp SAS, its Amazon sourcing tool. WAMP is its Walmart product: SellerAmp launched it after acquiring the data company Bizmetrica, and describes it as giving Walmart sellers historical sales data and trend insights. So "SellerAmp Walmart" and "WAMP Walmart" are the same thing, compared in detail above.

Helium 10 for Walmart

Helium 10 says its Chrome extension's Xray works on Walmart search results, with filtering, sorting and export, and that a Listing Quality Score widget shows on Walmart product pages. Its Demand Analyzer extends research to Amazon. Helium 10 leans toward brands and private label sellers researching keywords and listings, more than resellers checking an item's profit at a shelf.

sellerboard for Walmart

sellerboard is a profit analytics dashboard for Walmart sellers: sales, refunds, fees, cost of goods, ad costs and inventory planning for the products you already sell. Walmart lists it as a solution provider. It answers "how much did I make?" rather than "should I buy this?"

Jungle Scout for Walmart

Jungle Scout is built mainly around Amazon. If Walmart research is your main need, check Jungle Scout's site for what it currently covers on Walmart before you subscribe.

Sourcing tools vs seller-account tools

The easiest way to sort these is by when you use them. Sourcing tools like Margenos and WAMP help before you buy inventory. Analytics tools like sellerboard work after you sell. Many sellers use one of each. If you also sell on Amazon, keep in mind that Amazon tools use Amazon fees and sales rank; on Walmart you need Walmart's referral fees, WFS fees and a Walmart sales estimate. Questions

Is WAMP free?

WAMP says it is free during its beta, with Pro pricing not yet announced, and it requires a Walmart Seller account. Margenos has a 14-day free trial, works without a seller account, and has a phone barcode scanner on Google Play for Android (iPhone coming soon).

Is Margenos a good WAMP alternative?

Margenos covers the same Walmart sourcing job as WAMP (profit and fees, sales estimates, competing sellers and stock, buy box and history charts) and adds push alerts, with a phone barcode scanner on Google Play for Android (iPhone coming soon) on the same account.

Do I need a Walmart Seller account to use Margenos?

No. You can research any Walmart product with Margenos without connecting a Walmart Seller account.

Can I use Margenos and WAMP together?

Yes. They are separate Chrome extensions. Many resellers compare tools side by side before choosing one.

What is WAMP for Walmart?

WAMP is a Chrome extension from SellerAmp for researching products on Walmart Marketplace. It shows profit, fees, sales estimates and competing sellers, and it requires a Walmart seller account.

What is the best WAMP alternative?

If you source in stores or don't have a Walmart seller account yet, Margenos is a strong WAMP alternative: it adds push alerts, works without a seller account and has a phone barcode scanner on Google Play for Android (iPhone coming soon), on one account with its Chrome extension.

Does SellerAmp work for Walmart?

Yes, through WAMP. SellerAmp's main SAS tool is for Amazon; WAMP is its separate product analysis tool for Walmart sellers.

Does Helium 10 work for Walmart?

Helium 10 says its Chrome extension's Xray works on Walmart search results and that its Listing Quality Score widget shows on Walmart product pages. It's aimed more at brands and private label sellers than at resellers sourcing in stores.

Is sellerboard available for Walmart?

Yes. sellerboard offers profit analytics for Walmart sellers, tracking sales, fees, cost of goods and ad costs on products you already sell. It isn't a sourcing tool.

Does Jungle Scout work with Walmart?

Jungle Scout is built for Amazon only (its help center says it supports Amazon marketplaces only) sellers. Check its website for current Walmart features. For Walmart sourcing, a Walmart-specific tool like Margenos or WAMP uses Walmart's own fees and data.
