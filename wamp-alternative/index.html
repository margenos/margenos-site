Choosing a Walmart sourcing tool

WAMP and Margenos both help resellers decide what to buy for Walmart Marketplace. The right one depends on how you source. Ask yourself these questions:

  • Do you source in stores? You'll want a phone app with a barcode scanner. Margenos has one; WAMP doesn't list barcode scanning.
  • Do you have a Walmart seller account yet? WAMP requires one. Margenos works without one, so you can research while your application is reviewed.
  • Do you want alerts? Margenos can alert you when a tracked product's price moves, a seller sells out, or a new seller joins.
  • What's your budget? WAMP says it's free during beta. Margenos starts at $24.99 a month with a 14-day free trial.
