Walmart shipping templates: methods, costs and 2-day setup

How Walmart's shipping templates, methods and fast-delivery programs work, and how shipping cost changes your profit.

Updated November 10, 2026 · By the Margenos team

Walmart shipping templates are the rules in Seller Center that tell Walmart how you ship each item: which shipping methods you offer, to which regions, with what transit time and at what shipping cost. Every account starts with a Default Shipping Template that can't be deleted, and any SKU you don't assign elsewhere uses it. You can add custom templates for different products, such as a paid template for heavy items or a TwoDay template for fast movers. You manage them under Shipping Profile > Shipping Templates.

Walmart shipping templates: the types

From Walmart's Shipping Templates overview (updated Sep 1, 2026):

TemplateWhat it's for
DefaultOne per account, can't be deleted, only customized. Unassigned SKUs use it.
CustomUp to 60 templates for seller-fulfilled items, each with its own methods, regions and costs.
Third-party (3P)Up to 30 more templates for items a provider such as Deliverr (Flexport) fulfills.
Paid StandardFor items without free shipping, with 3–5 day transit.
FreightFor large, bulky items shipped less-than-truckload; items in a freight category or over 150 lb.
InternationalFor inventory shipped from outside the U.S., with longer transit limits by country.

Walmart also offers Simplified Shipping Settings, which work out delivery times from your fulfillment settings, as an alternative to building every rule by hand.

Walmart seller shipping methods

Walmart's shipping methods overview lists five methods. Sellers must support at least one, unless they use WFS.

MethodTransitCan you charge shipping?
Value6–7 daysNo. Value is on by default in the Default template.
Standard3–5 daysYes, or free. Walmart says items with a shipping fee aren't eligible for search boost or Buy Box prominence.
TwoDay2 daysNo
OneDay1 dayYes, or free
FreightLonger, for LTL shipmentsYes, or free

To stop offering free Value shipping on an item, Walmart says to create a Paid Standard template and move the SKUs to it.

How to set up a shipping template

Walmart's add settings guide (updated Sep 1, 2026) walks through it:

  1. Open Shipping Templates. In Seller Center, go to Shipping Profile and select Shipping Templates.
  2. Create a template. Choose manual shipping settings for items shipped from the U.S., or international settings for items shipped from abroad.
  3. Add shipping rules. For each method and region, pick a transit time and set the rate, then save.
  4. Assign SKUs. Map your items to the template. If you just added a fulfillment center, wait four hours first, or the items fall back to the Default template.

Two limits catch people out. Walmart allows one change per hour per template and up to 10 edits per day; go past that and the template locks for 24 hours. And shipping costs are subject to the Egregious Shipping Cost rule: Walmart's pricing rules say very high shipping fees can get an offer unpublished or made ineligible for the Buy Box, and a template rate above the allowed limit is rejected and can put your account at risk of suspension.

Walmart 2-day shipping for sellers

Walmart's expedited delivery program (updated Feb 4, 2026) lets seller-fulfilled items show OneDay or TwoDay delivery. Walmart says these listings get more search visibility, a Buy Box advantage, a fast-shipping badge and preference in sales events. Enrollment is automatic once you qualify. Over the last 30 days you need:

  • An active account (not suspended or terminated)
  • On-time delivery rate above 90%
  • Valid tracking rate above 95%
  • Cancellation rate below 1.5%
  • More than 90 days as a seller, or at least 20 qualifying orders in the last 30 days

If your performance drops, Walmart removes you from the program automatically and re-enrolls you seven days after you qualify again. Walmart suggests a lag time of zero for expedited offers, a 5 p.m. local cutoff and weekend delivery where your carrier supports it, and it offers a 25% discount on Ship with Walmart labels. On Walmart's expedited delivery page, ThreeDay is open to everyone: choose Standard with a 3-day transit time in your template.

Walmart fulfillment options

  • Seller fulfilled: you ship from your own location using your templates and carriers.
  • Third-party logistics: a provider ships for you, using 3P templates where supported.
  • Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS): Walmart stores and ships your stock. Walmart says TwoDay is automatically available for WFS items. See WFS fees and WFS vs FBA.
  • Multi-channel fulfillment: allowed under Walmart's shipping policy if it uses neutral packaging and unbranded delivery vehicles.

Whichever you pick, the shipping policy still applies: ship within one operational day unless Walmart approves a longer lag time, upload valid tracking, and don't have another retailer ship directly to your customer. Missing those can lead to an account suspension.

Who pays the Walmart seller shipping fee?

You do. You buy the label, and the customer only pays shipping if your template charges a rate on a method that allows it. On Value and TwoDay, the customer pays nothing for shipping, so that cost comes out of your margin. On WFS, the fulfillment fee replaces your label cost.

Questions

What are Walmart shipping templates?

They're the rules in Seller Center that set your shipping methods, regions, transit times and shipping costs for each item. Every account has a Default template, and you can add up to 60 custom templates for seller-fulfilled items.

How do I change shipping settings on Walmart Marketplace?

In Seller Center, go to Shipping Profile, then Shipping Templates. Edit a template's methods, regions, transit times and rates, save it, and assign SKUs. Walmart limits edits to one per hour per template and 10 per day.

Can Walmart sellers charge for shipping?

Yes, on Standard, OneDay and Freight. Not on Value or TwoDay. Walmart says Standard items with a shipping fee aren't eligible for search boost or Buy Box prominence, and very high shipping fees can get an offer unpublished.

How do I get Walmart 2-day shipping as a seller?

Enrollment is automatic when you qualify: an active account, on-time delivery above 90%, valid tracking above 95% and cancellations below 1.5% over 30 days, plus more than 90 days selling or at least 20 qualifying orders in 30 days. WFS items get TwoDay automatically.

What happens to SKUs not assigned to a shipping template?

They use the Default Shipping Template's settings, including its transit times and shipping cost rules.

Do I pay for shipping on Walmart Marketplace?

Yes. Sellers pay for their own labels or carrier service, and the customer only pays if your template charges a rate on a method that allows it. With WFS, you pay Walmart's fulfillment fee instead.
