Walmart international seller: selling on Walmart.com from outside the U.S.

Which countries Walmart accepts, the documents and tax forms you need, how you get paid, and how to get inventory to U.S. customers.

Yes, you can be a Walmart international seller on Walmart.com without living in the U.S. Walmart accepts businesses incorporated outside the U.S. and lets companies from 15 countries and regions onboard without a U.S. tax ID, including China, India, the U.K., Canada, Mexico and Vietnam. Businesses from other countries may need a U.S. tax ID. Every seller needs a valid U.S. return address and a way to deliver within Walmart's shipping times, usually Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) or a U.S. warehouse. There is no Walmart Marketplace in the U.K. or Europe.

Walmart seller eligible countries

Walmart's tax classifications guide (updated Nov 2, 2026) sorts sellers by where the business is incorporated and whether it has a U.S. Employer Identification Number (EIN):

Tax formWho it's for
W-9Businesses incorporated in the U.S. with an EIN.
W-8ECIBusinesses incorporated outside the U.S. that have an EIN. For some countries of incorporation, Walmart doesn't accept the local tax ID and you may need a U.S. tax ID.
W-8BEN-ENon-U.S. businesses without an EIN, from China, Hong Kong, the U.K., Japan, Canada, Mexico, India, Singapore, South Korea, Taiwan, Germany, Vietnam, Thailand, Chile and Turkey.
W-8BENSole proprietors from Mexico or India only. Mexican sole proprietors can only sell in the Mexico market.

Walmart doesn't publish a single "approved countries" list, so that W-8BEN-E list is the clearest public signal. Walmart's onboarding guide adds that non-profits and sole proprietorships (except in India) can't sell on the U.S. marketplace.

Selling on Walmart from India, Pakistan, Nigeria or the U.K.

India

India is one of the best-supported countries. Walmart's Sell from India page says you can register with Indian documents and no U.S. tax ID: GSTIN for sole proprietors, plus a certificate of incorporation or articles of association for partnerships and companies. Hindu Undivided Families can't register. Payouts can go to an Indian bank account in INR through Payoneer or PingPong. You still need a U.S. return address, you're responsible for duties and import taxes, and Walmart isn't the importer of record.

United Kingdom

U.K. companies can onboard without an EIN under W-8BEN-E, with a certificate of incorporation, a utility bill or bank statement from the past three months, and a letter of authorization if applicable. Walmart's international FAQ has a separate application link for U.K.-incorporated businesses.

Pakistan and Nigeria

Neither is on Walmart's W-8BEN-E list. Walmart's guides don't say businesses from unlisted countries are refused outright, but they say some countries of incorporation must provide a U.S. tax ID. Sellers in that position often form a U.S. company with an EIN; get legal and tax advice first, because Walmart says you can't change your entity type after onboarding. Walmart also verifies the person behind the business: its business verification guide says it may require photo ID and facial recognition, completed within seven days. Stay away from anyone selling ready-made seller accounts.

China and Hong Kong

Both are supported, with extra documents: Chinese companies submit a business license and a letter of authorization stamped with the company chop, and Hong Kong businesses need a notarized letter of authorization and the director's contact details.

Is there a Walmart Global or international seller program?

Not as a separate marketplace. International sellers apply through the same Walmart Marketplace application, choosing their country of incorporation; Walmart's international seller FAQ links applications for businesses incorporated in the U.K., Canada and Mexico. Walmart's registration guide says international sellers may be asked for documents such as a company PAN card, GST certificate, certificate of incorporation and a utility bill or bank statement. Once you're live in the U.S., you can add the Canada, Mexico or Chile markets from the same account. Our Walmart seller account and seller requirements pages cover the rest of the application.

Do non-U.S. sellers need a U.S. company, EIN or bank account?

  • EIN: not if your country is on the W-8BEN-E list. Walmart's FAQ says it accepts different tax classifications for certain international sellers. W-8BEN-E and W-8ECI forms must be renewed every three years.
  • U.S. bank account: payouts go through the payment processor you choose. Walmart's FAQ lists Payoneer, Hyperwallet and PingPong, plus options such as LianLian, Airwallex and WorldFirst for sellers in China and Hong Kong.
  • Payment hold: international sellers' payments are delayed up to 21 days per order until 90 days have passed since the first shipped order and they've received $7,500 in payments.
  • Importing: Walmart's India page says a U.S. entity with an EIN should act as ultimate consignee, or you must name another business.

For U.S. tax questions, talk to a tax professional; Walmart seller taxes covers the basics.

Is there a Walmart Marketplace in the UK or Europe?

No. Walmart completed the sale of Asda, its U.K. business, on February 16, 2021, and Walmart's registration guide names its marketplaces as the U.S., Mexico, Chile and Canada. A U.K. or German company can still sell to U.S. customers on Walmart.com.

Walmart Mexico and Walmart Canada seller centers

They're markets inside the same Seller Center, not separate accounts. Walmart's FAQ says you add a market by selecting the flag icon in Seller Center, and that both Canada and Mexico offer WFS in their own countries. Walmart's WFS page also says eligible U.S. inventory can ship to Mexico through Walmart Cross Border: Exports. Canada details are on Walmart Canada seller.

How overseas sellers fulfill Walmart.com orders

Walmart's FAQ lists the shipping methods you must support at least one of: Value (6 to 7 day transit), Standard (3 to 5 days), TwoDay, OneDay and Freight (6 to 10 days). Shipping from overseas per order rarely fits, so most international sellers use one of these:

OptionHow it works
WFSWalmart stores, ships and handles returns. WFS doesn't accept international inbound shipments; inventory must arrive from within the U.S., usually via a freight forwarder. Units need GTIN or UPC labels.
Walmart Cross Border: ImportsOcean freight from select ports in China, India and Vietnam (Cambodia via Ho Chi Minh City) to WFS. You must use WFS, be in good standing and have a continuous U.S. customs bond. Walmart estimates 35 to 60 days from cargo ready date (guide).
U.S. 3PLYou stay seller-fulfilled and a U.S. warehouse ships orders and receives returns.

Returns need a valid U.S. address: no P.O. boxes, and not in Hawaii, Alaska or U.S. territories. Walmart can't export returns from Cross Border Imports back to Asia. Read how to send inventory to WFS before your first shipment, and compare what Walmart allows for dropshipping.

Questions

Can I sell on Walmart from India?

Yes. Walmart lets Indian businesses register with Indian documents and no U.S. tax ID, including sole proprietors with a GSTIN. You need a U.S. return address, and payouts can go to an Indian bank account in INR through Payoneer or PingPong.

Can I sell on Walmart from Pakistan or Nigeria?

Neither country is on Walmart's list for onboarding without a U.S. tax ID, and Walmart says some countries of incorporation must provide one. Sellers there often form a U.S. company with an EIN; get legal and tax advice first.

Can I sell on Walmart from the UK?

Yes, on Walmart.com. U.K. companies can onboard without an EIN using a certificate of incorporation and a recent utility bill or bank statement. There's no Walmart marketplace in the U.K. itself.

Do I need a US EIN to sell on Walmart?

Not if your business is incorporated in one of the 15 countries and regions Walmart supports under W-8BEN-E, such as China, India, the U.K., Canada or Vietnam. Other non-U.S. businesses may need a U.S. tax ID.

Is there a Walmart Marketplace in Europe?

No. Walmart's marketplaces are the U.S., Canada, Mexico and Chile, and Walmart sold its U.K. business, Asda, in 2021. European companies can sell to U.S. customers on Walmart.com.

How do international sellers get paid by Walmart?

Through a payment processor such as Payoneer, Hyperwallet or PingPong. Payments for each order are held up to 21 days until 90 days have passed since your first shipped order and you've received $7,500.
