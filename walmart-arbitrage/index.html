Questions

What is Walmart arbitrage?

Walmart arbitrage means buying products for less, in stores, online or wholesale, and reselling them on Walmart Marketplace for a profit after fees.

Is there a Walmart arbitrage scanner app?

Yes. Margenos is a Walmart arbitrage scanner for Android and iPhone (coming soon), with a Chrome extension for online arbitrage on the same account.

How do I know if an arbitrage deal is profitable?

Enter your cost and Margenos subtracts Walmart referral fees and, if you use WFS, fulfillment fees. It shows profit, ROI and the maximum you should pay.
