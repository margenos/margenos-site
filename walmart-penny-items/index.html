Walmart penny items: what's real and what isn't

Why some Walmart items scan at a penny, whether stores will sell them, how people find them, and what to check before reselling one.

Walmart penny items are products that people report ringing up at $0.01, usually because they've been pulled from sale or discontinued. Walmart doesn't advertise penny pricing, doesn't publish a penny list, and doesn't have a penny program that we could find on Walmart.com or corporate.walmart.com. Many penny finds are items the store isn't supposed to sell at all, and a cashier or manager can refuse the sale. So: occasionally real, never guaranteed, and not something to build a business on.

Does Walmart have penny items? Are they real?

Reports of Walmart penny items come from shoppers, Reddit threads and Discord groups, not from Walmart. The stories are consistent: an item scans at a penny in the app or at a price checker, often in clearance or seasonal areas, and sometimes the store sells it and sometimes it doesn't. We can't verify any specific penny list, and neither can the people selling them.

Why do Walmart items ring up for a penny?

The usual explanation in reseller communities is that a penny price marks stock that's been discontinued or pulled and should be removed from the shelf, rather than a real sale price. Walmart hasn't published how it marks items for removal, so treat that as the community's explanation, not Walmart policy. What it means in practice: a penny scan is a sign the store may not want to sell the item, which is why refusals are common.

Walmart penny items policy: will a cashier sell it?

Walmart has no public penny-item policy. Stores can decline to sell merchandise that's been pulled, and on pricing questions Walmart's price match page says "the Walmart store manager on duty has the final decision." So "does Walmart honor penny items" comes down to the store and the associate. If you try:

  • Be polite and don't argue. A refusal is within the store's discretion.
  • Don't hide items, swap tags or pull stock from the back. That isn't deal hunting, and it's the fastest way to lose a store's goodwill.
  • Don't buy huge quantities if the store does sell. Taking a whole shelf is how stores end up tightening up for everyone.

Walmart penny items vs Home Depot and Dollar General

People search "does Walmart have penny items like Home Depot" because penny hunting is better known at Home Depot and Dollar General. The idea reported at all three is the same: a penny price marks stock the store wants off the shelf, and the sale isn't guaranteed. In each case the information comes from shopper communities, not from published retailer policies we could verify. Claims that one chain's penny items are more predictable than another's are anecdotal, so don't plan trips around them.

How people find Walmart penny items

  1. Scan with the Walmart app. Walmart's Check a price page says the app's scanner shows "the price of any item just by scanning it." Set your store first.
  2. Look where pulled stock ends up. Clearance sections, seasonal aisles, end caps and items sitting out of place.
  3. Check community posts as leads. Reddit and Discord penny threads point at categories or items; prices still vary by store.
  4. Confirm at a price checker or with an associate before you get to the register.

Is there a Walmart penny list app, or a list for today?

There's no official Walmart penny list. Unofficial "penny list today" posts are compiled from other shoppers' scans, so they may not match your store. Be wary of paid apps or groups that promise penny items: nobody has access to Walmart's internal removal lists, and a penny scan in one store says little about another. Third-party inventory checkers such as BrickSeek look up prices and stock by SKU or UPC; for what they currently show for Walmart, check their site.

The free tool that matters is the Walmart app scanner. Our page on Walmart hidden clearance covers the same scan-and-compare method for regular markdowns, which turn up far more often than pennies.

Does Walmart Canada have penny items?

We couldn't find anything from Walmart Canada about penny pricing either. Walmart Canada's pricing policy says clearance items are excluded from price matching and price-drop refunds. Margenos works on Walmart.com (U.S.) listings only.

Can you resell penny items on Walmart Marketplace?

Yes, if you bought them legitimately and they meet Walmart's listing rules. Three checks first:

  • Condition. Walmart's item condition guide defines New as "brand new, unused, unopened, undamaged and in its original packaging," and says other condition types are tied to invite-only programs. Shelf-worn penny items may not qualify as New.
  • Brand and category rules. Some brands need approval; see Walmart restricted brands.
  • Real resale value. Discontinued items can still sell, but some were pulled because nobody wanted them. A penny cost doesn't help if the item doesn't sell.
