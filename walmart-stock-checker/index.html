Walmart stock checker for Marketplace sellers

Before you buy, check how much stock the other sellers on a listing have. Margenos shows units left when Walmart shows them, flags sold-out listings, and charts stock over time.

Units left per seller

When a listing says "Only N left", Margenos shows that number next to the seller. When it is not shown, you see a question mark, never a made-up number.

Sold-out detection

Listings where sellers have run out, along with the sellers recently on them. A sold-out listing can be an opening.

Stock history

Stock changes charted over time, so you can see how fast units move.

Why competitor stock matters

A listing with three sellers who each have two units left is a different buy from one with a seller holding hundreds. Stock tells you how long you would wait for your turn to sell.

  • Competing sellers with price, fulfillment and stock
  • Sold-out alerts by push notification
  • Monthly sales estimates alongside stock
  • Charts up to a year of history

How it works

  1. Scan or open a listing.

Questions

How can I see a seller's stock on Walmart?

Margenos shows the units-left count Walmart displays for a seller. When Walmart does not show a count, Margenos shows a question mark instead of guessing.

Does Margenos track stock over time?

Yes. Stock changes are charted, and push alerts can tell you when a listing sells out.

Does the Chrome extension show stock?

Yes. The extension loads every seller on the Walmart page, with their stock where Walmart shows it.
