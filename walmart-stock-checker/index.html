Walmart in-stock checker for shoppers: use the Walmart app

If you want to know whether your Walmart store has something on the shelf, the free option is Walmart's own app or website. Set your store, search for the item or scan its barcode, and Walmart shows the price and whether it's available. In the app, Walmart also lists aisle locations for items on your shopping list. Store counts change through the day, so if you're driving across town for one item, call the store first.

How to read competitor stock before you buy

Stock only means something next to sales. A quick check: divide total stock across sellers by the monthly sales estimate. If the sellers on a listing hold about four months of supply, your units will wait in line behind theirs, and if you use WFS, you pay storage while they do. If the listing is sold out or close to it, you may get the sales to yourself for a while.

Then look at who holds the stock. One seller with hundreds of units and the Buy Box is a harder fight than five sellers with a few units each. You can look up any seller's storefront with the Walmart seller lookup, and the Walmart price tracker shows whether the price has held as stock came and went. Track an item and Margenos sends a push alert when a listing sells out or sellers join or leave.
