How to open a Walmart seller account

Requirements, the sign-up steps, how long approval takes, and what to do if your account is denied, suspended or needs a break.

To get a Walmart seller account, you apply at marketplace.walmart.com with a registered U.S. business (EIN), a U.S. address and bank account, product details with valid UPCs, and a way to ship fast. Approval isn't instant, and Walmart doesn't publish a set review time (seller guides commonly report one to four weeks; see the full Walmart seller requirements). Once approved, you log in to Seller Center to finish setup and list products. This page covers the whole life of the account, from sign-up to approval, suspensions, taking a break and closing it.

Walmart seller account requirements

Walmart Marketplace is application-only, not open sign-up. These are the Walmart marketplace seller requirements that sellers and agencies consistently report:

  • A registered business. An LLC, corporation or other legal entity. Walmart does not accept individuals applying with a Social Security number.
  • A U.S. business tax ID (EIN) whose legal name matches your application exactly, plus a W-9 (or W-8 form if your company is incorporated outside the U.S.).
  • A verified U.S. business address and a U.S. bank account for payouts.
  • E-commerce history. Sales on Amazon, eBay, Shopify or your own site help a lot. Walmart wants sellers it trusts to treat shoppers well.
  • Products with valid GTINs/UPCs and nothing on Walmart's prohibited products list.
  • Fast shipping and U.S. returns, either from your own location or through Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS).

There's no monthly or setup fee to hold an account. You pay a referral fee when you sell; see Walmart seller fees for the category rates.

How to create a Walmart seller account (sign-up steps)

The Walmart seller sign-up, sometimes searched as "Walmart Seller Central sign up," starts on the Walmart Marketplace site, not on Walmart.com.

  1. Gather your documents: legal business name and address, EIN, bank details, tax form, a list of products with UPCs and prices, and links to where you already sell.
  2. Start the application: go to marketplace.walmart.com, click Join Marketplace, and pick your market and country of incorporation.
  3. Enter company details exactly as they appear on your IRS documents.
  4. Describe your business: current sales channels, yearly online sales, planned categories and number of items.
  5. Choose fulfillment: ship it yourself or use WFS.
  6. Submit and verify. Walmart checks that your company name and address match its records and may email you for more documents.

Walmart seller approval: how long it takes

Walmart doesn't publish an official review time. Seller guides put the typical Walmart seller account approval at one to four weeks for a complete application. The fastest way through is a clean application: the business name on the application, EIN letter and bank account all match, your website and email use your business domain, and you can show real sales history.

How to get approved to sell on Walmart

  • Use a business email, not a free Gmail or Yahoo address.
  • Show sales history and good ratings on another marketplace.
  • List categories and products you can actually supply at a competitive price.
  • Answer any follow-up email from Walmart quickly and upload clear documents.

Walmart seller application denied or declined

Walmart doesn't always explain a denial. Common causes are mismatched business information, limited e-commerce experience, prohibited products, weak fulfillment plans and uncompetitive prices. If you think the decision was a mistake, log in and open a case with Seller Support through the Help button. If the appeal is rejected, you'll need to reapply. There's no stated waiting period, but fix the real problem first; reapplying with the same gaps usually gets the same answer.

Walmart seller account login

After approval, the Walmart seller account login is at seller.walmart.com, the Walmart Seller Center. That's where you set up payments, shipping, returns, tax settings and listings. Our Walmart Seller Center guide walks through each area and how to reach support.

Walmart seller account suspended or terminated

Walmart enforces its Seller Performance Standards and policies with several levels of action:

StatusWhat happens
SuppressionYour seller-fulfilled listings can't be bought; WFS listings stay live.
PauseBoth seller-fulfilled and WFS listings can't be bought.
SuspensionListings are hidden, Seller Center access is limited and payments are held. You can usually appeal.
TerminationListings are removed and selling privileges are permanently revoked. Walmart says appeals are not granted.

How to appeal a suspension

Walmart's guide says to check the Performance page to see what went wrong, write a plan of action that explains the cause and the steps you've taken to fix it, then submit it through Help > Contact support. Depending on the issue, you may need supplier invoices from the last two months, warehouse photos or intellectual property documents. Appeals are handled in the order received, and an appeal doesn't guarantee reinstatement.

The best protection is never needing to appeal: ship on time, keep cancellations low, buy from sources you can document, and avoid listings with brand or IP risk. Walmart seller vacation mode

Walmart doesn't call it "vacation mode," but you have two options. Marketplace Learn has a guide to pausing sales and order operations in Seller Center for longer breaks. For short breaks, many sellers instead add Additional Days Off in the shipping settings, so orders keep coming in but ship dates move past your days away. If your stock is in WFS, Walmart keeps shipping it, so you may not need to pause at all.

How to close a Walmart seller account

We couldn't find a self-serve "close account" button in Walmart's public guides, so the safest route is to open a case through Help > Contact support and ask for the account to be deactivated. Before you do:

  • Ship or cancel open orders and let the return window run out.
  • Remove or sell through any inventory stored in WFS.
  • Download your payment reports and tax forms, including any Form 1099-K.
  • Make sure your final payout has landed in your bank account.

Questions

Can you sell on Walmart as an individual?

Not as a person using a Social Security number. Walmart requires a registered business with a U.S. EIN. Many solo resellers form a single-member LLC to qualify.

How long does Walmart seller approval take?

Walmart doesn't publish an official time. Seller guides report about one to four weeks for a complete application. Mismatched business details are the most common delay.

Does a Walmart seller account cost anything?

There's no setup or monthly fee. Walmart charges a referral fee on each sale, plus WFS fees if Walmart ships for you.

Can I reapply if my Walmart application is denied?

Yes. You can appeal a denial through a Seller Support case, and if that's rejected you can reapply. Fix the reason for the denial first.

Is there a Walmart reseller program?

There's no separate reseller program. Resellers apply for a normal Walmart Marketplace seller account and match existing listings by UPC.

What happens to my money if my Walmart seller account is suspended?

Walmart says payments are put on hold during a suspension or termination. A successful appeal is the way to get selling and payouts moving again.

Do I need a seller account to use Margenos?

No. Margenos works on any Walmart.com product page without a seller account, so you can research products while you wait for approval.
