How many sellers are on Walmart Marketplace? Walmart doesn't publish an official count. The most-cited independent estimate is from Marketplace Pulse, which estimated about 200,000 active sellers in June 2026, up from about 100,000 in September 2023. Walmart's own reports show the marketplace growing fast: in the quarter ended July 31, 2026, Walmart said its U.S. marketplace net sales grew more than 50%. Marketplace Pulse counts a seller as active based on what it sees on Walmart.com, so the figures depend on its method. In its September 2024 report it also found that international sellers, "nearly all from China," made up 35% of active sellers.

What share of sellers use WFS?

Walmart doesn't publish the share of sellers using Walmart Fulfillment Services, but it does publish related numbers:

  • Nearly 50% of the marketplace business flowed through fulfillment services, Walmart said in its Q2 FY27 release (Aug 20, 2026).
  • 50% GMV growth, on average, for sellers using WFS, per Walmart first-party data from Feb 1, 2025 to Mar 27, 2026, on the Marketplace site.
  • Two-thirds of top Walmart marketplace sellers use WFS, estimated Marketplace Pulse (Aug 29, 2024). The same article dates WFS's launch to February 2020.

For costs, see Walmart WFS fees.

How long has Walmart Marketplace been around?

Walmart launched its marketplace in August 2009. Walmart's own press release said the launch added "nearly one million new items" from three retailers: CSN Stores, eBags and Pro Team. Marketplace Pulse describes the marketplace as mostly dormant for its first seven years, with growth starting in 2016, the year Walmart acquired Jet.com. Walmart's marketplace has been around for more than 17 years, but most of its sellers joined in the last few.

Seller counts roughly doubling in under three years means more competition, and a big share of new sellers are international.
