Walmart seller shipping fee: what it is and how to avoid it

Why some Walmart orders show seller shipping, what Walmart+ covers, and how shipping charges change a seller's profit and Buy Box odds.

A Walmart seller shipping fee is a shipping charge set by a Marketplace seller, not by Walmart. When you buy an item that is "sold and shipped by" a third-party seller, that seller ships it from their own warehouse and decides, within Walmart's rules, whether to charge you for shipping. That's why one cart can show free shipping on Walmart's own items and a separate "seller shipping" line for a Marketplace item. Walmart+ doesn't usually cover it: Walmart's Walmart+ page says its free shipping "excludes most Marketplace items."

What is seller shipping on Walmart?

"Seller shipping" means the item comes from a third-party seller on Walmart Marketplace. On the item page, check the line that says who sells and ships the item. If it names a company other than Walmart, that seller sets the shipping method and cost. Walmart's shipping methods overview (updated Jun 3, 2025) shows which methods let a seller charge:

Shipping methodTransit timeCan the seller charge shipping?
Value6–7 daysNo
Standard3–5 daysYes, or free
TwoDay2 daysNo
OneDay1 dayYes, or free
Freight6–10 daysYes, or free

So if you see a seller shipping fee, the seller is usually shipping by Standard, OneDay or Freight with a paid rate.

Why is Walmart charging me seller shipping?

Because the offer you picked belongs to a Marketplace seller that charges for shipping, and that charge isn't part of Walmart's own free-shipping deals. A few common reasons the fee looks high:

  • Heavy or bulky items. Large items shipped by freight cost more to deliver.
  • Faster delivery. A seller may charge for OneDay delivery.
  • The seller prices low and adds shipping. Compare item price plus shipping, not item price alone.

There is a ceiling. Walmart's pricing rules (updated Dec 12, 2025) include an Egregious Shipping Cost rule: offers with extremely high shipping fees can be unpublished or made ineligible for the Buy Box, and Walmart reviews the shipping fee along with the price.

Seller delivery fee and seller pickup fee

Walmart doesn't publish a definition of a "seller delivery fee" or "seller pickup fee" that we could find. If one of these appears on your order, open the order details to see which seller it belongs to, and contact Walmart customer care before you check out if it isn't clear. Don't assume it's a Walmart+ charge; on Marketplace items it usually comes from the seller's shipping settings.

Does Walmart+ cover seller shipping?

Usually not. The Walmart+ page promises "free next-day & two-day shipping from Walmart.com with no order minimum," then adds: "Excludes most Marketplace items. Freight & location surcharges may apply." The important exception is items stored and shipped by Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS). Walmart tells sellers that WFS items are "automatically included in the Walmart+ 'free shipping' subscription program" (5 ways WFS can benefit your business). On the item page, these usually show a "Fulfilled by Walmart" note even though a Marketplace seller owns the stock.

How to avoid the Walmart seller shipping fee

  1. Look for the "sold and shipped by Walmart" offer. If Walmart sells the same item, it may be in the list of other sellers on the item page.
  2. Pick a WFS offer. Items fulfilled by Walmart are included in Walmart+ free shipping.
  3. Compare all sellers. Open the list of other sellers on the item page and compare price plus shipping. Another seller may ship free.

The seller's side: how shipping fees affect profit and the Buy Box

For a Marketplace seller, the shipping charge is a trade-off. Charging shipping recovers your label cost, but Walmart says it costs you visibility:

  • Buy Box. Walmart's Buy Box guide says the winner is picked on price, shipping fee, delivery speed, seller performance and customer experience. A shipping fee counts against you.
  • Search and prominence. The shipping methods overview says that if you charge a fee for Standard shipping, the item "will not be eligible for benefits such as a boost in search results or Buy Box prominence."
  • Conversion. Shoppers who see free shipping on Walmart's own items compare your total, not your item price.

If you offer free shipping instead, the label comes out of your margin. Either way, shipping cost has to be in your math before you buy the stock. That's where many resellers get caught: a deal that looks good at the item price turns thin once a heavy box ships for free. For the bigger fee picture, see Walmart seller fees.

Why WFS removes the shipping question

With WFS, Walmart stores, packs and ships the order, and you pay a fulfillment fee per unit instead of buying a label. Walmart's WFS fees page (updated May 27, 2026) lists the standard base fee at $3.45 for items of 1 lb or less, rising with weight, plus storage fees. In return, the item can show a two-day delivery promise (heavier items may take longer) and is included in Walmart+ free shipping, so the shopper sees no seller shipping fee. Whether that's cheaper than shipping it yourself depends on size and weight; compare on WFS fees and WFS vs FBA.

Questions

What is the seller shipping fee on Walmart?

It's a shipping charge set by a Marketplace seller for an item they sell and ship themselves. Walmart doesn't set it; the seller does, within Walmart's shipping and pricing rules.

Does Walmart+ cover seller shipping?

Usually not. The Walmart+ page says its free shipping excludes most Marketplace items. Items fulfilled by Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) are the main exception: Walmart says they're included in Walmart+ free shipping.

How do I avoid the Walmart seller shipping fee?

Pick the offer sold and shipped by Walmart if there is one, choose an item fulfilled by Walmart (WFS), or compare the other sellers on the item page for one that ships free.

Why is Walmart seller shipping so expensive?

Heavy, bulky or freight items cost more to ship, and some sellers charge for faster delivery. Walmart's Egregious Shipping Cost rule can unpublish offers with extremely high shipping fees.

Does charging shipping hurt a seller's Buy Box chances?

Yes. Walmart's Buy Box guide lists the shipping fee as a factor, and its shipping methods guide says items with a paid Standard shipping fee lose search boost and Buy Box prominence.

Who pays shipping on Walmart Marketplace orders?

The seller buys the label. The shopper only pays shipping if the seller charges a rate on a method that allows it. With WFS, the seller pays Walmart a fulfillment fee instead.
