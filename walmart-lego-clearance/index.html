Walmart LEGO clearance: finding deals and checking resale value

Where LEGO clearance shows up at Walmart, how to check the real price, and how to tell whether a set is worth reselling on Walmart Marketplace.

LEGO is one of the most popular clearance finds for resellers, because retired sets can hold value. But not every clearance set is a deal: some are marked down because they aren't selling, and popular sets fill up with other resellers fast. Here's how to find Walmart LEGO clearance and check it before you buy.

Where to find LEGO clearance at Walmart

  • The toy aisle and end caps after holidays and toy resets, when stores clear space for new sets.
  • Walmart.com's clearance section, filtered to LEGO and your store.
  • Scanning in the Walmart app: the price at the register can be lower than the shelf tag. See Walmart hidden clearance.

Walmart doesn't publish a LEGO markdown schedule; see Walmart clearance schedule for what's known.

Can you sell LEGO on Walmart Marketplace?

Walmart Marketplace has many third-party LEGO offers, and Walmart's Collectibles Experience page lists LEGO among the toys it welcomes in its Collectibles program. Whether your account can list a given brand depends on Walmart's brand rules; check in Seller Center before you buy, and see Walmart restricted brands. New, sealed sets in good boxes are the safest to list.

Hold or sell now?

Some resellers hold sets until they retire. That ties up cash and storage, and WFS charges monthly storage. If you plan to hold, factor storage into your cost. Price history on the listing shows whether similar sets have climbed after retirement or not.

Questions

When does Walmart put LEGO on clearance?

Walmart doesn't publish a schedule. Resellers commonly find LEGO markdowns after holidays and during toy resets, but timing varies by store.

How do I find hidden LEGO clearance at Walmart?

Scan LEGO sets with the Walmart app's barcode scanner to see the current price, which can be lower than the shelf tag, and check Walmart.com's clearance section filtered to your store.

Is it worth reselling Walmart LEGO clearance?

Sometimes. Check the Walmart.com price, fees, sales estimate, number of sellers and price history first. Heavy sets can have high WFS fees, and popular clearance often attracts many resellers.

Can I sell LEGO on Walmart Marketplace?

Many third-party sellers list LEGO on Walmart, and Walmart lists LEGO among the toys in its Collectibles program. Brand rules can apply to individual accounts, so check Seller Center before buying.
