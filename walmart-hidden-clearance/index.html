Walmart hidden clearance: what it is and how to find it

How hidden clearance works, how people find it in store and online, which tools help, and how to tell if a find is worth reselling.

Walmart hidden clearance is a shopper and reseller term for items that are marked down in Walmart's system but don't have a yellow clearance tag on the shelf yet, or are sitting in the regular aisle instead of the clearance section. It's real in the sense that shelf tags and register prices don't always match, and you find it by scanning barcodes with the Walmart app. It isn't an official Walmart program, Walmart doesn't publish a hidden clearance list, and nobody can promise you'll find it in a given store on a given day.

It doesn't show store prices or store stock. It answers the next question: once you find a cheap item, can you resell it on Walmart.com for a profit?

Is Walmart hidden clearance real or a scam?

The markdowns are real; the hype around them is where the scams live. Walmart runs a public clearance page on Walmart.com with an in-store filter, and store prices can change before shelf tags do. What Walmart doesn't do is announce which items are "hidden." Anything sold as a guaranteed list of hidden clearance at your store is a guess, often recycled from other locations.

Be careful with:

  • Paid "hidden clearance" groups and lists that promise specific prices at your store. Store prices vary, and a screenshot from another state means little.
  • Sites or posts asking you to log in or pay to unlock "today's list." Walmart doesn't sell access to its markdowns.
  • Viral "everything is a penny" claims. See our page on Walmart penny items for what's actually reported.

Free Facebook and Discord groups can be useful for spotting patterns, like a category going on clearance across many stores. Treat every post as a tip to check, not a fact.

How Walmart hidden clearance works (and when markdowns start)

Walmart doesn't publish its markdown schedule. What resellers and deal hunters report is a pattern of stepped markdowns: an item's price drops in stages as it gets closer to being pulled from the shelf, and seasonal goods get cut after the season ends. Timing differs by store, department and item, so "when does Walmart's hidden clearance start" has no single answer. Post-holiday weeks and seasonal resets are when many people look hardest.

The practical rule: the shelf tag tells you what the store meant to charge at some point; the scan tells you what the register thinks right now.

How to find Walmart hidden clearance in store

  1. Install the Walmart app and set your store. Walmart's Check a price page says the app's scanner lets you "know the price of any item just by scanning it." The scanner is in the search bar and on the Services tab.
  2. Scan items with old or missing tags. End caps, the back of the shelf, seasonal aisles and items sitting out of place are where mismatches show up.
  3. Compare the scan with the shelf tag. If the app shows a lower price than the tag, ask an associate to confirm at a price checker or the register before you load a cart.
  4. Check the resale value before you buy several. A cheap price only matters if the item still sells online above your costs. Scan the same barcode in a reseller app to see fees and profit.

Walmart's price match policy says U.S. stores will match the price of an identical item advertised on Walmart.com, with restrictions, and "the Walmart store manager on duty has the final decision on any price match." Store staff can also decline to sell an item that's been pulled from sale.

Walmart hidden clearance online and by zip code

Online, start with Walmart.com's clearance page, set your store, and use the in-store filter to see what Walmart lists as available near you. Prices on Walmart.com can differ from the shelf, so treat it as a lead.

"By zip code" searches usually mean third-party inventory checkers. BrickSeek's inventory checker guide says you look items up by the retailer's SKU or UPC, that "items at local retailers may be priced differently from store-to-store," and that searching by zip code is a BrickSeek One member feature; Basic members see up to two results per search. It also notes reported data may differ from what's in the store. For current Walmart coverage and membership prices, check their site.

Hidden clearance toys, LEGO and electronics: what's worth reselling

Toys, LEGO sets and electronics come up often in clearance posts because they have recognizable UPCs and an online market. That doesn't make every one a flip. Before you buy, check:

  • Profit after fees. Walmart's referral fee and WFS fees come off the sale price. Our Walmart profit calculator shows the math.
  • Competition. If a dozen sellers are already on the listing, or Walmart itself is selling at your target price, you'll wait or cut price.
  • Sales speed. A slow seller ties up cash and racks up storage fees.
  • Brand approval. Some brands need approval to sell on Walmart Marketplace. See Walmart restricted brands.
  • Condition. Walmart's item condition guide defines New as "brand new, unused, unopened, undamaged and in its original packaging." Crushed or opened boxes don't qualify.

Does Walmart Canada have hidden clearance?

Walmart Canada has its own clearance section on Walmart.ca, and the same shelf-tag-versus-scan idea applies in Canadian stores. Walmart Canada's pricing policy says Rollback and Clearance prices aren't price matched, and price-drop refunds exclude clearance items. Margenos works on Walmart.com (U.S.) listings only, so it won't check resale on Walmart.ca.

Questions

What is Walmart hidden clearance?

It's an informal term for items marked down in Walmart's system that don't have a clearance tag on the shelf yet, or are in the regular aisle instead of the clearance section. You find them by scanning barcodes with the Walmart app and comparing the price to the shelf tag.

Is Walmart hidden clearance real?

Price mismatches between shelf tags and scans are real, but Walmart has no official hidden clearance program or published list. Results vary by store and day, and paid lists that promise specific prices at your store deserve skepticism.

How do I find hidden clearance at Walmart?

Use the scanner in the Walmart app with your store set, scan items with old or missing tags, and confirm the price with an associate. Online, check Walmart.com's clearance page with the in-store filter.

Is there an app that finds Walmart hidden clearance?

No app can see every store's markdowns. The Walmart app shows your store's current price when you scan, and third-party checkers like BrickSeek look up prices and stock by SKU or UPC. Margenos doesn't find clearance; it shows whether an item resells for a profit on Walmart.com.

When does Walmart hidden clearance start?

Walmart doesn't publish a markdown schedule. Shoppers report stepped markdowns and big seasonal cuts after holidays, but timing varies by store, department and item.

Can a Walmart store refuse to sell a hidden clearance item?

Yes. Store staff can decline to sell items that have been pulled from sale, and Walmart's price match policy says the store manager on duty has the final say on price matches.

Can I resell Walmart hidden clearance items online?

Many resellers do, on Walmart Marketplace and elsewhere. Check brand restrictions, condition (New must be unopened and undamaged) and profit after Walmart's fees before buying in quantity.
