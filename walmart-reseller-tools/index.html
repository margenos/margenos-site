Walmart repricer: how it works

Walmart's Repricer automatically adjusts your prices based on rules you set. After you accept Walmart's Automate Pricing terms, you can assign items to a strategy:

  • Walmart Buy Box only: prices against the current Buy Box price.
  • Buy Box and external retailers: uses whichever is lower, the Buy Box price or competing retailer prices.
  • External retailers only: prices against the lowest competing retailer price, including shipping.
  • AI-powered: offered to eligible sellers and items at Walmart's discretion.

Every item needs a minimum and maximum price before it can join a strategy. That minimum is where most sellers go wrong. Set it from your real breakeven, not a guess: Margenos shows your breakeven and max buy cost after referral fees, WFS fees, prep and storage months. Third-party repricers, including ones that reprice on Amazon and Walmart together, work the same way and need the same floor. For the strategy side, read how to win the Walmart Buy Box.

Walmart Marketplace API and seller API

The Walmart Marketplace APIs give sellers and approved solution providers direct access to Walmart's systems for items, inventory, orders, pricing, promotions and reporting. You generate a Client ID and Client Secret in the Walmart Developer Portal after completing onboarding. Most small resellers never touch the API directly; they use it through software that connects for them, such as listing and repricing tools. Looking for an app to run your store? See the Walmart seller app. Profit analytics tools like sellerboard, which Walmart lists as a solution provider, connect to your account to track profit on sales you've already made.
