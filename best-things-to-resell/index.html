The best things to resell are items you can buy below market, that sell quickly, and that aren't crowded with other sellers. The category matters less than those three numbers. A cheap item that sits for months, or a hot item with 40 sellers on the listing, isn't a good resell. Below are categories resellers commonly do well with, followed by how to check any item before you buy.

This page focuses on new items you can resell online, especially on Walmart Marketplace. For used clothing and one-off finds, see reseller apps by job.

What makes an item worth reselling: a 5-point check

  1. Buy cost well under the selling price. Many resellers aim for at least 30% ROI after fees.
  2. Fees you can afford. On Walmart, that's the referral fee by category plus WFS or shipping. See Walmart seller fees.
  3. Proven demand. A sales estimate shows how many sell a month. See the Walmart sales estimator.
  4. Manageable competition. Count sellers with stock and check who wins the Buy Box.
  5. Allowed to sell. Check brand and category rules: restricted brands and prohibited items.

Questions

What are the best things to resell for profit?

Items you can buy well below their online price that sell quickly and aren't crowded with sellers. Toys, household, beauty, home and kitchen, sports, pet supplies and video games are common categories, but the numbers matter more than the category.

What sells best when reselling on Walmart?

Items with steady demand, few competing sellers and a price high enough to cover Walmart's referral fee and fulfillment. Check the sales estimate and seller count for each listing.

What is a good ROI for reselling?

Many resellers aim for at least 30% ROI after all fees, and higher for slow-selling or risky items. Your target depends on how fast the item sells.

How do I know if an item will resell?

Check its online selling price, fees, monthly sales estimate, number of sellers and price history. Margenos shows all of these for Walmart.com listings from one scan.

Do I need a business to resell on Walmart?

Yes. Walmart Marketplace requires a registered business with a U.S. tax ID. See whether you can sell on Walmart as an individual.
