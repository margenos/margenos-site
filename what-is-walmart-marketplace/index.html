What is Walmart Marketplace?

How Walmart's third-party marketplace works, who sells on it, and what it means for shoppers and resellers.

Walmart Marketplace is the part of Walmart.com where independent businesses, not Walmart itself, sell their products. When you shop on Walmart.com, some items are sold by Walmart and many are sold by these third-party sellers, all in the same search results and the same checkout. For sellers, Walmart Marketplace is a way to reach Walmart's online shoppers without a monthly fee: you apply, get approved, list products and pay a referral fee when something sells.

What is Walmart Marketplace, in plain terms?

Think of Walmart.com as two stores in one. One is Walmart's own inventory, bought from suppliers and sold as "sold and shipped by Walmart.com." The other is the marketplace: thousands of outside companies that list products on Walmart.com and ship them (or let Walmart ship them). Shoppers get more choice. Walmart earns a fee on each marketplace sale without buying the inventory.

Walmart's online marketplace launched in 2009 and grew slowly at first. It has grown fast in recent years. Marketplace Pulse, which tracks marketplaces, reported in September 2024 that Walmart had reached about 150,000 active sellers, up from around 50,000 in 2022, with roughly a third of them based outside the U.S., mostly in China.

How Walmart Marketplace works

  1. A business applies. Walmart reviews each application and only approves sellers it expects to meet its standards. See how to open a Walmart seller account.
  2. The seller lists products in Walmart Seller Center, either by matching a product that's already on Walmart.com (same UPC) or by creating a new listing.
  3. Shoppers buy on Walmart.com through the normal Walmart checkout. When several sellers offer the same item, one offer wins the main "Add to cart" button, called the Buy Box.
  4. The order gets shipped, either by the seller from their own location or by Walmart through Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS).
  5. Walmart pays the seller the sale price minus its referral fee (and WFS fees if used).

Who the Walmart sellers are

"Walmart seller" covers a wide mix of businesses:

  • Brands selling their own products directly.
  • Resellers who buy brand-name products through retail arbitrage, online arbitrage or wholesale and resell them. See Walmart arbitrage.
  • Multichannel sellers who already sell on Amazon, eBay or Shopify and add Walmart as another channel.
  • International sellers, a large share of them from China, according to Marketplace Pulse.

Every one of them had to apply and be approved, and all of them are held to the same performance standards and return policy. Shoppers worried about trust can read our guide to Walmart third-party sellers.

What Walmart Marketplace means for shoppers

For shoppers, the marketplace mostly shows up as more choice: more brands, more sizes and more sellers to compare on the same item. A few things work differently from buying from Walmart directly. The product page names the seller, return rules can vary a little by seller (within Walmart's minimum policy), and delivery times depend on where the seller ships from. Items marked as fulfilled by Walmart ship from Walmart's own warehouses even though a third-party seller owns them. If you want extra confidence, look for the Pro Seller badge, explained in what is a Walmart Pro Seller.

What sells on Walmart Marketplace

Almost every category that Walmart.com carries has marketplace sellers, from home and kitchen to toys, beauty, grocery and electronics. Some categories and brands need extra approval, and some products are prohibited. Resellers tend to do best with everyday brand-name items that sell steadily and don't have a crowd of sellers or Walmart itself on the listing. Our list of the best items to sell on Walmart covers what to look for.

Who can sell on Walmart Marketplace?

Walmart Marketplace isn't open to everyone. Sellers generally need:

  • A registered business with a U.S. tax ID (EIN). Individuals using a Social Security number aren't accepted.
  • A U.S. address and bank account for payouts.
  • Products with valid UPCs that aren't prohibited.
  • Fast shipping and U.S. returns, or WFS.
  • Ideally, a history of selling online elsewhere.

The full process is in how to sell on Walmart.

What it costs to sell

Walmart says there are no setup or monthly fees. Sellers pay a referral fee on each sale, a percentage set by category. Many categories, including Home, Toys and "Everything Else," are 15%, and a few categories are tiered by price. If Walmart ships your orders through WFS, you also pay fulfillment and storage fees. Why Walmart Marketplace matters to resellers

Fewer sellers per listing means a better shot at the Buy Box. The tradeoff is research. Walmart doesn't show a public sales rank, so it's harder to know how fast something sells, and you have to account for Walmart's fees, which are different from Amazon's.

Questions

What is Walmart Marketplace?

It's the part of Walmart.com where approved third-party businesses sell their own products alongside Walmart's inventory, using Walmart's checkout.

Is Walmart Marketplace the same as Walmart.com?

It's part of Walmart.com. Some items are sold by Walmart itself and others by marketplace sellers; the product page shows who the seller is.

How many sellers are on Walmart Marketplace?

Walmart rarely discloses it. Marketplace Pulse reported about 150,000 active sellers in September 2024, and the number has kept growing.

Can anyone sell on Walmart Marketplace?

No. You need a registered business with a U.S. EIN, and Walmart must approve your application. Individuals using a Social Security number aren't accepted.

Does it cost money to sell on Walmart Marketplace?

There's no setup or monthly fee. Walmart takes a referral fee on each sale, plus WFS fees if Walmart fulfills your orders.

Is Walmart Marketplace better than Amazon?

Neither is better for everyone. Amazon has more traffic and is easier to join; Walmart has fewer sellers per listing and no monthly fee. Many resellers sell on both.

Is Walmart Marketplace the same as Walmart Neighborhood Market?

No. Walmart Marketplace is the online program that lets approved third-party sellers list products on Walmart.com, while a Walmart Neighborhood Market is a physical store: Walmart describes it as a small-format grocery store with fresh food, household items and a pharmacy. Searches for "Walmart marketplace" plus a city name are usually looking for a local Neighborhood Market store, not the seller program.

What does "Walmart seller" mean on a product page?

It tells you who is selling that item. "Sold and shipped by Walmart.com" means Walmart is the seller; any other name next to "Sold by" or "Sold and shipped by" is an independent Marketplace seller, according to Walmart's Marketplace sellers help page.
