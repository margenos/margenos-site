Why resellers need a clearance scanner, not just a price

A low shelf price feels like a win, but clearance often means the item isn't selling fast anywhere. Before you buy ten, check:

  • Profit after fees. Walmart's referral fee by category plus the WFS fee by weight can eat a small margin.
  • Sales speed. A sales estimate shows whether the item moves or sits.
  • Competition. If other resellers bought the same clearance, the listing fills with sellers and the price drops.
  • Restrictions. Some brands are restricted. See Walmart restricted brands.

Questions

Is there a Walmart clearance app?

Walmart doesn't have a separate clearance app, but the Walmart app's barcode scanner shows an item's current price at your store. Resellers add a clearance scanner like Margenos to check profit, sales and sellers.

What is the best Walmart clearance scanner app?

For finding the price, the Walmart app. For deciding whether to resell an item, Margenos shows Walmart fees, profit, ROI, a sales estimate and every competing seller from one scan.

How do I find hidden clearance at Walmart?

Scan items with yellow or end-cap tags in the Walmart app; the price at the register can be lower than the shelf tag. Our Walmart hidden clearance guide covers where to look.

Can I resell Walmart clearance on Walmart Marketplace?

Yes, many resellers do, as long as the brand and category aren't restricted for you. Check profit after fees and competition before buying in quantity.

Does Margenos show Walmart store clearance prices?

No. Margenos shows Walmart.com Marketplace prices, sellers and history. You enter the clearance price you paid as your cost to see your profit.

Is there a free Walmart clearance scanner?
