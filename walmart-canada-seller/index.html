How to become a Walmart Canada seller

Requirements, fees, Seller Center and WFS for Walmart.ca, and what changes if you already sell on Walmart.com.

Updated November 10, 2026 · By the Margenos team

To become a Walmart Canada seller, you apply to Walmart Marketplace Canada, which sells on Walmart.ca and the Walmart app. You need a business number or tax ID, a photo ID, documents that verify your business name and address, a return address in Canada or the U.S., and a bank account. Signing up is free; Walmart charges a referral fee on each item sold, set by category. Walmart Canada uses the same Seller Center as the U.S. marketplace, so a U.S. seller can add Canada as a market, and Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) runs in Canada too.

How to sell on Walmart Canada: requirements

Walmart's Walmart Marketplace Canada guide lists what to have ready:

  • Business verification: a federal or provincial business number or business tax ID, your legal business name, address, email and phone.
  • Personal ID: driver's license, passport or other valid government photo ID.
  • Supporting documents: for example a Notice of Assessment, Articles of Incorporation, Articles of Amalgamation or Notice of Registration (Quebec only), plus a utility bill or bank statement from the last six months.
  • Market details: customer service email and phone, and Canada tax registration numbers if applicable.
  • A return center located in Canada or the United States.
  • Bank account information for payments.

Documents go in as PNG, JPG or PDF files under 5MB. Walmart's tax classification guide lists the documents for Canadian companies onboarding without a U.S. EIN, such as GST/HST and, where they apply, BC PST or Quebec QST screenshots.

How to become a Walmart seller in Canada, step by step

  1. Apply or add the market. New sellers sign up through Walmart's Marketplace application. Existing U.S. sellers select the flag icon on the Seller Center home page and add Canada, according to Walmart's international seller FAQ.
  2. Verify your business. Enter details exactly as they appear on your documents; mismatches delay approval.
  3. Add market details such as customer service contacts and tax registration numbers.
  4. Set fulfillment and returns: ship yourself, use WFS Canada, or for U.S. sellers, Walmart Remote Fulfillment.
  5. Add payment details, then list your catalog and add inventory.

The U.S. requirements are different; compare them on Walmart seller requirements and how to sell on Walmart.

Walmart Canada seller fees

There are no setup or monthly fees, as Walmart's Walmart.ca seller page says. The referral fee is a percentage of the sale, by category. Walmart Canada's referral fee guide (last updated Oct 30, 2024) lists rates from 6% to 20%, including:

CategoryReferral fee
Personal Computers6%
Consumer Electronics, Cell Phones, Camera & Photo, Video Game Consoles, Major Appliances8%
Grocery, Gourmet Food, Tires & Wheels10%
Automotive, Tools & Home Improvement, Industrial & Scientific, Musical Instruments12%
Most other categories (Toys, Baby, Beauty, Home, Kitchen, Pet Supplies, Sporting Goods, Video Games, Everything Else)15%
Jewellery20%

Walmart notes these contract categories don't match item setup categories one-to-one. Because the guide is from 2024, confirm your rate in Seller Center. U.S. rates are on Walmart seller fees.

New-Seller Savings in Canada

Walmart's New-Seller Savings guide (updated Oct 22, 2026) applies to first-time Walmart Marketplace Canada sellers who go live on or after February 1, 2026, until January 31, 2027:

  • 25% off base referral fees on seller-fulfilled items; 50% off on WFS items and on items shipped with Walmart Remote Fulfillment (WRF).
  • WFS: 25% off fulfillment fees, 50% off storage fees, and 100% off Walmart Preferred Carrier and inventory removal and disposal.
  • Up to $1,000 CAD in Sponsored Products ad credits when you spend $2,000 CAD in your launch quarter.

Referral fee discounts are paid back quarterly, after the quarter ends, on non-returned items. Sellers are auto-enrolled.

Walmart Canada Seller Center login

There isn't a separate Canadian login site. Walmart's guides for Canada link to the same Seller Center at seller.walmart.com, and Walmart's international FAQ says you manage your catalog from "one single Seller Center account" across markets. You switch markets with the flag icon. For login problems, see Walmart seller login. For Walmart seller support in Canada, use the Help button in the Seller Center menu bar to open a case.

Does WFS operate in Canada?

Yes. Walmart's WFS Canada guide (updated Oct 10, 2025) says WFS picks, packs and ships Walmart.ca orders, handles customer support and returns, and shows "Fulfilled by Walmart" tags. Key points from the guide:

  • Fulfillment centers in Mississauga and Calgary. Stock held only in Calgary isn't available to customers in the Maritime provinces.
  • WFS Canada ships only to Canadian addresses and only fulfills items sold on Walmart.ca.
  • Restrictions include a 500 lb maximum, no perishable or regulated products, and no aerosols.
  • Referral fees still apply; WFS storage and fulfillment fees are on top. Walmart has a WFS fee calculator for Canada on its fees page.

Margenos's WFS fees and the WFS fees page are for the U.S. program only.

Can a U.S. seller list on Walmart.ca?

Yes, if eligible. Walmart's U.S. onboarding guide says U.S. Marketplace sellers "may be eligible" to sell on the Walmart Canada, Mexico or Chile marketplaces after completing setup for that market. Walmart Canada accepts a return center in the U.S., and its New-Seller Savings terms describe Walmart Remote Fulfillment (WRF) for sellers fulfilling from the U.S. You may need Canadian tax registration; talk to a tax professional. It works the other way too: Walmart's FAQ says Canada sellers can also sell on the U.S. marketplace from the same account. See selling on Walmart from outside the U.S.

Walmart Canada vendor vs. marketplace seller

A Marketplace seller lists its own inventory on Walmart.ca and sets its own prices. A vendor (supplier) sells wholesale to Walmart Canada, which then sells the product itself. Those are separate processes. Walmart Canada runs a Canada Growth Summit where Canadian small and medium businesses, including Marketplace sellers, meet its merchandising and Marketplace teams; the 2026 event took place June 23, 2026. For how the two models compare, see Walmart 1P vs 3P and how to get your product into Walmart.

On dropshipping: Walmart's U.S. packaging policy bans buying from another retailer and having it ship to a Walmart customer. Check Walmart Canada's own policies in Seller Center before you plan a dropshipping model; our Walmart dropshipping page covers the U.S. rules.

Questions

How do I sell on Walmart Canada?

Apply to Walmart Marketplace Canada with a business number or tax ID, photo ID, documents verifying your business name and address, a return address in Canada or the U.S., and bank details. Existing U.S. sellers can add Canada as a market from the flag icon in Seller Center.

What are Walmart Canada seller fees?

No setup or monthly fees. Walmart Canada charges a referral fee per item, by category: its fee guide lists 6% to 20%, with most categories at 15%. First-time sellers who go live from February 1, 2026 get referral fee discounts until January 31, 2027.

Is the Walmart Canada seller account separate from Walmart.com?

Walmart Canada uses the same Seller Center, and Walmart says you can manage your catalog from one Seller Center account across markets. Each market still has its own setup steps.

Does Walmart Fulfillment Services work in Canada?

Yes. WFS Canada stores, packs and ships Walmart.ca orders from fulfillment centers in Mississauga and Calgary, and handles customer support and returns. It only ships to Canadian addresses.

Can a U.S. seller sell on Walmart Canada?

Yes, if eligible. U.S. Marketplace sellers can add the Canada market in Seller Center, use a U.S. return center, and fulfill from the U.S. with Walmart Remote Fulfillment or their own shipping.

How do I become a Walmart Canada vendor?

Becoming a supplier that sells wholesale to Walmart Canada is a separate process from Marketplace. Walmart Canada's Growth Summit is one route for Canadian businesses to meet its merchandising and Marketplace teams.
