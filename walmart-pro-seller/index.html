What is a Walmart Pro Seller?

What the Pro Seller badge means for shoppers, the criteria sellers have to hit, and the perks that come with it.

A Walmart Pro Seller is a third-party Marketplace seller that Walmart has recognized for strong, consistent performance: fast and on-time delivery, few cancellations, quick replies to customers, good listings, competitive prices and a seller rating of at least 4.0. These sellers get a Pro Seller badge next to their business name on their Walmart.com listings. For shoppers, it's a sign the seller has a good track record. For sellers, it comes with real perks, including a referral fee discount on eligible listings.

What does Pro Seller mean on Walmart?

Pro Seller is the top tier of Walmart's seller recognition program. Walmart's Marketplace Learn guide describes three tiers: Rising Seller, Advanced Seller and Pro Seller. Only the Pro Seller tier gets the badge that shoppers see. A Pro Seller is still a Marketplace seller, not Walmart itself. The badge means Walmart has measured the seller's recent performance and found it meets every Pro Seller requirement.

Walmart Pro Seller badge criteria

Walmart scores sellers on these metrics, mostly over the last 90 days. To earn the Pro Seller badge, a seller has to meet all of them:

MetricPro Seller requirement
On-time delivery rate95% or higher
Seller-canceled orders1.5% or lower
Response rate (messages answered within 48 hours, last 30 days)95% or higher
Shipping speed score (free 3-day or faster delivery)50% or higher
Content quality score75% or higher
Price competitiveness score (last 30 days)75% or higher
Orders (rolling 90 days)More than 100
Active days on Marketplace90 or more
Seller rating4.0 or higher
Policy complianceFull compliance with Marketplace policies

Rising Seller and Advanced Seller have lower bars on the same metrics (for example, 90% and 92.5% on-time delivery) and no seller rating requirement. Walmart calculates the metrics daily and refreshes everyone's tier on the 5th and 20th of each month. A seller who drops below the criteria for two refresh cycles in a row can lose the status, and policy warnings can make a seller ineligible.

How to become a Pro Seller on Walmart

  1. Ship fast and on time. Use shipping templates with 3-day or faster free shipping, or put inventory in WFS, which is built for fast delivery.
  2. Don't cancel orders. Most cancellations come from selling stock you don't have. Keep inventory counts accurate.
  3. Answer every customer message within 48 hours.
  4. Improve listing content for items where you own the listing, so your content quality score reaches 75%.
  5. Price competitively. Walmart scores your prices against the market. Check current prices and price history before you buy inventory, not after.
  6. Build volume and time: 90 active days and more than 100 orders in the last 90 days.
  7. Watch the Performance page in Seller Center for your tier progress.

The price competitiveness score is where research matters most. If you buy a product that's only profitable at a price above what everyone else charges, you either lose money or hurt your score. Pro Seller benefits

According to Walmart's Marketplace Learn guide, Pro Sellers get:

  • The Pro Seller badge on their Walmart.com listings
  • A 10% referral fee discount on eligible "Pro Listings"
  • A $50 monthly credit for search engine marketing
  • Access to Flash Deals and quarterly rotating rewards
  • Discounts on Ship with Walmart shipping labels, if eligible
  • Faster payouts, special Walmart Marketplace Capital terms if eligible, and a post-purchase reviews credit

Advanced Sellers get a smaller 5% referral fee discount on Pro Listings plus some of the same perks. If you're running the numbers on a product, that fee discount changes your margin; see Walmart seller fees and the Walmart profit calculator.

Tracking your progress

Because tiers refresh twice a month, a bad week can show up fast. Check the Performance page after busy periods like Q4, when late shipments and cancellations tend to spike. If one metric is slipping, fix that first: a seller who misses only the response rate, for example, can often recover by the next refresh just by answering messages faster. Walmart also offers a Seller Academy course called Optimizing Your Listings for Pro Seller Success on Marketplace Learn.

Pro Seller vs fulfilled by Walmart

These are two different things. "Fulfilled by Walmart" means the item ships from Walmart's warehouses through WFS. Pro Seller is a badge for the seller's overall performance. A seller can use WFS without being a Pro Seller, and a Pro Seller can ship its own orders. Using WFS does help with the fast-shipping part of the criteria.

Are Walmart Pro Sellers legit?

The badge is a good sign, not a guarantee. To earn it, a seller needs high on-time delivery, very few cancellations, fast replies and a seller rating of at least 4.0, all measured by Walmart on recent orders. That's a meaningful filter. Still, read the seller's reviews and the return policy, and remember every Marketplace order is covered by Walmart's return policy whether or not the seller has the badge. Questions

What is a Pro Seller on Walmart?

A third-party Marketplace seller that meets all of Walmart's top-tier performance targets, including 95% on-time delivery, 1.5% or fewer cancellations and a 4.0+ seller rating. They get a badge on their listings.

Is a Walmart Pro Seller the same as Walmart?

No. Pro Sellers are independent businesses selling on Walmart Marketplace. The badge means Walmart rates their recent performance highly.

How often does Walmart update Pro Seller status?

Walmart calculates the metrics daily and refreshes seller tiers on the 5th and 20th of each month.

Can you lose the Walmart Pro Seller badge?

Yes. Falling below the criteria for two refresh cycles in a row can cost a seller the status, and policy warnings can make a seller ineligible.

Do Pro Sellers pay lower Walmart fees?

Walmart lists a 10% referral fee discount on eligible Pro Listings for Pro Sellers, and a 5% discount for Advanced Sellers.

Are Walmart Pro Seller reviews trustworthy?

The seller rating is based on buyer feedback, and Pro Sellers need 4.0 or higher. Read recent reviews on the seller's page for the details behind the number.
