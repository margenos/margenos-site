Questions

What is Margenos?

Margenos is a Walmart reseller tool (Chrome extension and Android app) that shows whether a product is worth selling on Walmart Marketplace: fees, profit, ROI, sales estimates, every competing seller with stock, the Buy Box winner and price and Buy Box history.

How much does Margenos cost?

Margenos has three plans: Pro at $24.99 a month, Plus at $34.99 a month and Elite at $44.99 a month. Yearly billing saves 20%. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial.

Is Margenos free?

No. Margenos is a paid subscription, and every plan starts with a 14-day free trial so you can use the full plan before you pay. The Walmart profit calculator on margenos.com is free to use.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Pick a plan in the Chrome extension or Android app and add a card. Nothing is charged during the 14 days. If you don't cancel, your plan starts when the trial ends.

How do I cancel Margenos?

Cancel anytime from Manage subscription in the app or the Chrome extension. Cancel before the trial ends and you won't be charged. You can also email support@margenos.com.

Is there a Margenos app for iPhone?

The iPhone app is coming soon. Today Margenos is available as a Chrome extension and an Android app on Google Play, and iPhone users can use the Chrome extension on a computer.

Does Margenos work on Android?

Yes. The Margenos app is on Google Play. It scans barcodes in stores and shares one account and scan allowance with the Chrome extension.

Does Margenos work on Microsoft Edge or other browsers?

The extension is built for Google Chrome. Many Chromium browsers can install Chrome Web Store extensions, but Chrome is the supported browser.

Do I need a Walmart seller account to use Margenos?

No. Margenos works without connecting a Walmart seller account, so you can research products before or while you apply.

Is Margenos affiliated with Walmart?

No. Margenos is independent and is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Walmart Inc. It's made by Lemont Holdings LLC, a Texas company.

Is Margenos legit?

Margenos is a real product from Lemont Holdings LLC, published on the Chrome Web Store and Google Play, with support at support@margenos.com. Try it with the 14-day free trial and cancel from the app if it isn't for you.

How accurate are Margenos sales estimates?

Walmart doesn't publish a sales rank, so Margenos estimates from Walmart's own signals such as sales badges and stock changes, shows the estimate as a range with its source, and shows "not enough data" instead of guessing when signals are thin.

Where do Margenos fees come from?

Margenos applies Walmart's referral fee for the product's category and the WFS fulfillment fee for its weight, plus your cost, prep and storage months, to show profit, ROI, breakeven and max buy cost.

Does Margenos work for Walmart Canada or Amazon?

No. Margenos works only with Walmart.com (U.S.) listings. It doesn't show Walmart Canada or Amazon prices or fees.

Does the Margenos extension buy or click anything?

No. It reads only the Walmart pages you open yourself and never clicks, browses or adds anything to a cart.

Does Margenos work for wholesale sourcing?

Yes. Type the UPC and your wholesale cost to see profit and ROI per unit before you place an order.

What counts as a scan?

Each product you check uses one scan from your monthly allowance, shared between the phone app and the Chrome extension. Storefront lookups, which show another seller's products, are counted separately.
